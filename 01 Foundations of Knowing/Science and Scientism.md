# Science and Scientism

**Science and Scientism** — the distinction between science as a method of disciplined inquiry and scientism as the metaphysical belief that this method exhausts what is real.

The two are different in kind. **Science** is a procedure for disciplined public comparison among records: it tests predictions, makes error visible, rejects explanations that fail under repeatable conditions, and steadily widens the human [[The Assay|assay]]. Its power comes precisely from *controlled limitation*. **Scientism** is the further, unearned step — the doctrine that only measurable relations exist, the conviction that whatever cannot be tested is identical to what is untrue. The first is indispensable; the second exceeds the evidence the first can supply.

The trouble is a silent promotion: a method for investigating measurable relations is recast as a claim about all of reality. Because every record is only a partial [[Measurement as Transformation|transformation of its object]], a discipline built on records cannot certify from within that nothing escapes them. Scientism is today the largest faith on Earth, and the least aware of itself as a faith. Physics is downstream of metaphysics: every theory of the measurable presupposes a prior, revisable theory of what there is. Reductionism's failure to reconstruct a mind from its cells is a structural limit, not a temporary embarrassment.

The evidence that reality overflows the measurable is convergence: Plato and the Buddha reached the atom half a world and no conversation apart, and isolated cultures independently built internally consistent cosmologies — the pattern of [[Radical Syncretism|radical syncretism]]. A genuine account of everything must therefore explain purpose, society and this recurrence, not only the behaviour of particles — a demand no [[The 3.5-Dimensional Being|3.5-dimensional]] instrument can meet from inside, and one that points toward an [[The Open Theory of Everything|open theory of everything]] and toward reading [[Trip Reports and Revelation|revelation as testimony]]. Established results are never contradicted here; they are re-interpreted.
