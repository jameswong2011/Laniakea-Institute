# Science and Scientism

**Science and Scientism** — the distinction between science as a method of disciplined inquiry and scientism as the metaphysical belief that this method exhausts what is real.

Science is a method; scientism is a faith that grew up around it. The two differ in kind. **Science** is a procedure for disciplined public comparison among records. It tests predictions, makes error visible, throws out explanations that fail under repeatable conditions, and steadily widens the human [[The Assay|sample of reality]]. Its power comes precisely from *controlled limitation* — from agreeing to look only at what can be checked. **Scientism** takes a further, unearned step. It is the doctrine that only measurable relations exist — the conviction that whatever cannot be tested is the same as what is untrue. The first is indispensable. The second claims far more than the first could ever supply.

The trouble is a silent promotion. A method for investigating measurable relations is quietly recast as a claim about the whole of reality. But because every record is only a partial [[Measurement as Transformation|transformation of its object]], a discipline built entirely on records cannot certify from within that nothing escapes them. It is like judging a city by its budget spreadsheet and then declaring that whatever the spreadsheet omits does not exist. Scientism is today the largest faith on Earth, and the least aware of itself as a faith. Physics is downstream of metaphysics: every theory of the measurable already assumes a prior, revisable theory of what there is — the reason [[Metaphysics Precedes Physics|the shape of reality must be settled before its measurement]]. Reductionism's failure to rebuild a mind from its cells is a structural limit, not a temporary embarrassment.

The evidence that reality overflows the measurable is convergence. Plato and the Buddha reached the idea of the atom half a world and no conversation apart. Isolated cultures independently built internally consistent cosmologies that mirror one another — the pattern of [[Radical Syncretism|one cosmology seen through many cultural lenses]]. A genuine account of everything must therefore explain purpose, society and this very recurrence, not only the behaviour of particles. No [[The 3.5-Dimensional Being|3.5-dimensional]] instrument can settle that from inside, which is why the honest path is an [[The Open Theory of Everything|open theory of everything]] and the reading of [[Trip Reports and Revelation|revelation as testimony]]. Nothing established here is overturned; established results are re-interpreted, not contradicted.
