# Metaphysics Precedes Physics

**Metaphysics Precedes Physics** — the principle that any account of what can be measured presupposes a prior, revisable account of what there is, so that a physics severed from metaphysical truth-seeking builds excessive inference on unexamined foundations.

Before you can measure reality, you have to decide what reality is made of. That decision is metaphysical — a claim about what fundamentally exists — and it comes first, whether or not anyone admits it. Physics is downstream of metaphysics. Every measurement already assumes an answer to a prior question: what counts as a thing, a cause, an observer, a variable worth recording. Those answers are settled before the first instrument is switched on. Picture surveyors mapping a coastline. Before a single distance is taken, they have already fixed where the land ends and the sea begins. Move that hidden decision and every later number shifts with it. So it is with physics: its foundations are chosen, not measured.

The earliest recorded physicists knew this. The Greek *physikoi* — Thales, Anaximander, Heraclitus and those after them — asked what everything is made of, the *archē* or first principle, and never split that question from the nature of order and the good. *Physis* meant nature as a living whole. The Vedic, Buddhist, Daoist, Hermetic and Gnostic systems did the same, keeping cosmology and conduct inside one structure. Their frameworks were not primitive superstition. They were disciplined attempts to unite what a later age would tear apart.

Modern theoretical physics, in its dominant institutional form, has forgotten this order and pays for the amnesia. It shrank the phrase "theory of everything" down to the union of quantum mechanics and gravity, mislabelling one sub-problem as the whole. Cut loose from any account of what it is even about, it drifts into speculative ontology — a theory of what exists — while still picturing itself as purely empirical. The [[The String Theory Cautionary Tale|string-theory cautionary tale]] is the exhibit. Half a century of eleven-dimensional formalism of real mathematical beauty has yielded no testable prediction. That is metaphysics done with mathematics, dressed as science. A formalism does not interpret itself, and internal consistency is not evidence about the world.

The corrective is not to abandon empiricism but to place it properly. Because the senses give only a partial [[The Assay|sample of reality]], and every instrument [[Measurement as Transformation|sheds an uncaptured remainder]], a model resting on the measurable alone is structurally incomplete. A genuine account must explain not only particles but purpose, mind, and why [[Consciousness as Primary|consciousness]] is present at all. Restoring the ancient union of physics and metaphysics is the first move toward an [[The Open Theory of Everything|open theory of everything]] — and toward taking seriously the [[Radical Syncretism|convergent testimony]] of cultures that never spoke to one another yet mapped the same structure.
