# The Open Theory of Everything

**The Open Theory of Everything** — the replacement of the search for a single closed equation with four coordinated disciplines, each carrying its own evidential grammar, in an account kept open by design.

Modern physics uses "theory of everything" for a narrower task — the unification of quantum theory and gravitation. A genuine account of *everything* must do more: relate physical regularity to consciousness, say why experience has qualitative character, explain how agents, societies and moral systems arise, and show how a model built inside the universe can claim to contain its own conditions — a demand [[Science and Scientism|scientism's]] closed models cannot meet. Posed this way, the project restores the ancient union of physics and metaphysics found in Greek, Buddhist, Vedic, Daoist, Hermetic and Gnostic systems, which placed cosmology and conduct in one structure.

In place of one equation stand four coordinated disciplines: an **empirical** discipline that observes, measures and reproduces; a **logical** discipline that exposes contradiction and follows implication to its [[The Conceptual Terminus|conceptual terminus]]; a **phenomenological** discipline that studies experience, attention and value from the inside; and a **comparative-metaphysical** discipline that sets myths, scriptures, contemplative maps, dreams and [[Trip Reports and Revelation|channelled reports]] side by side to find recurring structure. These are not interchangeable — a syllogism is not a meditation, a particle-detector result is not an account of pain — and each carries a proper evidential grammar. Synthesis becomes possible only when that grammar is stated rather than hidden.

The theory must remain open because closure is unavailable to a situated mind. A truly complete map would have to include the mapmaker, every instrument, every higher-order system enclosing it, and every future transformation of its own concepts — the map becoming the territory while staying a map. Following from [[The Assay|the assay]] and the [[The 3.5-Dimensional Being|3.5-dimensional]] limit, the aim is a *generative grammar* rather than a final inventory: a small set of principles explaining why worlds, minds and moralities recur, and where the grammar itself must stay unfinished.
