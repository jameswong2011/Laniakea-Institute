# The Open Theory of Everything

**The Open Theory of Everything** — the replacement of the search for a single closed equation with four coordinated disciplines, each carrying its own evidential grammar, in an account kept open by design.

A theory of everything need not be a single final equation. Modern physics uses the phrase for a narrower job, the joining of quantum theory to gravitation. A real account of *everything* has to do far more. It must relate physical regularity to consciousness, say why experience has any felt quality at all, explain how agents, societies and moral systems arise, and show how a model built inside the universe can honestly claim to contain its own conditions. No closed system of the [[Science and Scientism|scientistic]] kind can meet that demand. Posed this way, the project restores the ancient union of physics and metaphysics found in Greek, Buddhist, Vedic, Daoist, Hermetic and Gnostic systems, which kept cosmology and conduct inside one structure.

In place of one equation stand four coordinated disciplines, each a distinct way of gathering evidence. The **empirical** discipline observes, measures and reproduces. The **logical** discipline exposes contradiction and follows implication to its [[The Conceptual Terminus|conceptual terminus]]. The **phenomenological** discipline studies experience, attention and value from the inside: what a state is like to undergo, beyond how it registers on a dial. The **comparative-metaphysical** discipline sets myths, scriptures, contemplative maps, dreams and [[Trip Reports and Revelation|channelled reports]] side by side to find recurring structure. These are not interchangeable. A syllogism is not a meditation, and a particle-detector reading is not an account of pain. Each carries its own *evidential grammar*, its own rules for what counts as good evidence. Synthesis becomes possible only when that grammar is stated out loud, never smuggled in. The error the method guards against is not speculation; it is speculation disguised as closed fact.

The theory must stay open because closure is unavailable to a situated mind. A truly complete map would have to include the mapmaker, every instrument, every larger system enclosing it, and every future change in its own concepts. The map would have to become the territory while somehow staying a map, a diagram of a room that must also contain a full diagram of itself, endlessly. Following from [[The Assay|the partial sample every knower receives]] and the [[The 3.5-Dimensional Being|3.5-dimensional]] limit, the aim is a *generative grammar*: a small set of principles explaining why worlds, minds and moralities recur, marking where the grammar itself must stay unfinished. A final inventory is unavailable. Those deliberate gaps are held open as [[The Open Tensions|the tensions the system refuses to close]].
