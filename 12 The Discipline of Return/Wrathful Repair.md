# Wrathful Repair

**Wrathful Repair** — the deliberate switch to the negative pole — exposure, boundary, judgement, removal — undertaken not as revenge but to restore the conditions under which trust and peaceful relation can again be honest.

The setting is the account of truth as more than correct description: truth is the reliability that lets many agents coordinate without continual defence. Trust is a property of an ecosystem's neural structure. When [[Promises and the Hierarchy of Freedom|promises]] are kept, signals become cheaper and collaboration can grow more complex; when deception or predation spreads, every node must spend energy checking every other node, and the body fragments. Beings no longer confined by the self–world division — the Tathāgatas of the tradition — carry a duty to uphold this reliability across [[The Dharmakāya|the dharmakāya's]] ecosystems.

Repair sometimes requires force against the process abusing trust. The purpose is not emotional revenge but the restoration of honest relation: the wrathful protector destroys the lie that compassion must remain passive. A corrupted structure may interpret unconditional soothing as permission to continue — wrathful compassion ends the pattern that peaceful compassion could only comfort. Yet wrath without clear awareness becomes ordinary violence wearing sacred costume, so three tests distinguish wrathful compassion from sanctified aggression: **precision** — force is directed at the harmful relation rather than an undifferentiated category of beings; **proportion** — no more agency is removed than repair requires; **reintegration** — an honest route back into relation remains wherever reintegration is possible.

In the larger architecture, wrathful repair is the operational face of the [[Peaceful and Wrathful Deities|peaceful and wrathful deities]] and the ethical core of [[Creative Destruction|creative destruction]]. Its judgements presuppose the work of [[Understanding Opposed Dharmas|understanding opposed dharmas]] — force is a last resort after reconstruction from within — with [[Yaldabaoth and Anti-Reality|anti-reality]], the pattern that attacks the possibility of future relation itself, as the limiting case where opposition is not negotiable.
