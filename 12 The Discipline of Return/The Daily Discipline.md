# The Daily Discipline

**The Daily Discipline** — the ten-step cycle the whole practice compresses into, running from attention to a renewed question.

A metaphysics is worth nothing until it changes what you do on an ordinary day. So the entire system folds down into one repeatable cycle. Run it on any real event — a hard conversation, a piece of bad news, a flash of anger — and it converts inherited reaction into deliberate choice:

1. **Attend** — separate the awareness that watches from the stream of thoughts it watches. This is the base move of [[Sovereign Attention|sovereign attention]] (meditation understood as the precondition of being able to choose at all).
2. **Feel** — take in the raw sensation, the qualia, without forcing it to be pleasant and without rushing to label it.
3. **Assay** — name what your perception, memory, language and standpoint cannot reach, in the spirit of [[The Assay|the assay]] (every mind receives a selective sample of reality, never the whole).
4. **Trace** — find the causes, promises and systems feeding into the event.
5. **Polarise** — state the strongest live alternatives, including the one you are about to reject.
6. **Empty** — test every apparent object, self and rule for the conditions it depends on, the working use of [[Śūnyatā (Emptiness)|emptiness]] (nothing carries a fixed essence of its own).
7. **Choose** — act from the widest [[Free Will and Decision-Space|decision-space]] (the real room to choose left once fear and inattention are subtracted) available now.
8. **Repair** — take the consequence, settle the debt, and rebuild trust where you can.
9. **Compress** — keep the principle; let go of the expired form it arrived in.
10. **Open** — let the outcome become a fresh question.

Enlightenment here is not escape from the cycle. It is seeing more of its grammar while the cycle runs. The awakened act reads more of [[The Seed Phrase|the seed phrase]] (the compact intention a world unfolds from) in the present moment, and so wastes less suffering on the illusion of being separate.

One final test guards the whole system against its own grandeur. Practice is measured not by the splendour of its cosmology but by plain results: attention freer, truth more bearable, promises cleaner, humour less cruel, power more accountable, and difference more able to collaborate.
