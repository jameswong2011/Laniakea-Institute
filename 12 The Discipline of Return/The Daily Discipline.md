# The Daily Discipline

**The Daily Discipline** — the ten-step cycle into which the entire practice compresses, running from attention to renewed question.

The metaphysics is only complete when it changes ordinary conduct, and so it resolves into a repeatable cycle:

1. **Attend** — distinguish awareness from the thought-stream, the base move of [[Sovereign Attention|sovereign attention]].
2. **Feel** — receive qualia without forced positivity or immediate conceptual capture.
3. **Assay** — name the limits of perception, memory, language and position, in the spirit of [[The Assay|the assay]].
4. **Trace** — locate the causes, promises and systems participating in the event.
5. **Polarise** — state the strongest live alternatives, including the one being rejected.
6. **Empty** — test every apparent object, self and rule for dependent conditions, the working use of [[Śūnyatā (Emptiness)|emptiness]].
7. **Choose** — act from the widest [[Free Will and Decision-Space|decision-space]] currently available.
8. **Repair** — receive consequence, settle debt, and restore trust where possible.
9. **Compress** — preserve the principle without clinging to the expired form.
10. **Open** — let the result become a new question.

Enlightenment, on this account, is not immunity from the cycle. It is increasing recognition of the grammar while the cycle occurs: the enlightened act sees more of [[The Seed Phrase|the seed phrase]] in the present event and therefore wastes less suffering on mistaken separateness.

A final test disciplines the whole system against its own grandeur. The measure of practice is not the splendour of its cosmology but whether attention becomes freer, truth more bearable, promises cleaner, humour less cruel, power more accountable, and difference more capable of collaboration.
