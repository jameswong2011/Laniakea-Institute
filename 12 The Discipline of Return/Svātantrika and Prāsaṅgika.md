# Svātantrika and Prāsaṅgika

**Svātantrika and Prāsaṅgika** — two methods from Madhyamaka Buddhism (building a position versus dismantling every position), generalised into the twin movements of conceptual life: the source that constructs positions and the sink that dissolves them by tracing their consequences.

Every living idea is built up and then broken down. These two names mark the two moves. Svātantrika is the source. It builds a positive position out of explicit reasons and stands it up as an argument that can travel, be tested, and organise action. Prāsaṅgika is the sink. It takes a claim and follows its consequences until the hidden assumptions collapse, then returns the freed energy to a wider field. Think of a draft and an edit: one writer sets down a bold thesis; the other pushes on it until the weak joints give way. Neither move alone is enough.

Historically, within the school descending from Nāgārjuna, the two names mark fine disputes over method. Should emptiness be proved through self-standing arguments, or only by drawing out the unwanted consequences hidden in an opponent's own commitments? Those debates are real. Only the shape is kept here: the pair generalise into a portable technique that any tradition of thought can run.

Together they form a breath. Construction, then testing, then consequence, then dissection, then emptiness, then construction again. The source without the sink fills the world with unexamined systems. The sink without the source turns intelligence into permanent negation — forever tearing down, never building. Their circulation is like qi, a life-energy, moving through the channels of [[Memetic Lifeforms|conceptual beings]]: a source and a sink for the flow of symbolic energy. Ideas stay alive precisely because they can be both formed and dissolved.

In the larger architecture the pair is the engine of [[Conceptual Ascension|conceptual ascension]] and the working skeleton of [[Dialectic|dialectic]]. Its emptying stroke is [[Śūnyatā (Emptiness)|śūnyatā]], emptiness, put to work. Its rebuilding stroke feeds [[Memetic Birthing|memetic birthing]]. The whole cycle is compressed into the polarise-and-empty steps of [[The Daily Discipline|the daily discipline]].
