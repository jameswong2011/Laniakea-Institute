# Understanding Opposed Dharmas

**Understanding Opposed Dharmas** — the discipline of treating most perceived evil as contact with another truth-and-morality system, and of reconstructing that system from within before passing judgement on it.

The claim is diagnostic: one framework values loyalty where another values autonomy; one protects purity where another protects inclusion; one treats hierarchy as care where another treats it as domination. The first duty on meeting apparent evil is therefore to reconstruct the opposing [[Dharma-Law|dharma]] from within — an unintelligible enemy cannot be integrated and will be fought as a caricature. The frame is cosmic: the universe is a soup of [[Dialectic|dialectical]] opposition between mostly self-stable dharma-systems, and the friction between them generates the polarity that conscious activity runs on.

Understanding does not require submission. Some systems become self-stable by externalising all cost, concealing exit, and destroying rival ways of knowing; their internal morality explains them without making them acceptable to the wider [[The Dharmakāya|dharmakāya]]. And one case stays categorically distinct: [[Yaldabaoth and Anti-Reality|anti-reality]], the pattern that attacks the possibility of future relation itself, is the limiting case that no reconstruction redeems. The position is not relativism but dialectical *maturity* — you cannot defeat what you refuse to inhabit.

Judgement then proceeds through nested questions rather than a single verdict: What value is this action trying to preserve? Which beings count inside its moral boundary? Where are its costs placed? Does it enlarge or close future decision-space? Can the governing rule survive application to the actor? What promise, consent, or dependency changes the relation? Which larger body bears the result? The answers may not yield one universal sign, but they produce a more accurate entry in the ledger of [[Karma as Contextual Law|contextual karma]].

At the scale of a single thought, the opposite-branch exercise among [[The Three Attention Practices|the three attention practices]] rehearses this discipline; at the scale of ecosystems, [[Wrathful Repair|wrathful repair]] governs the cases where understanding ends in justified opposition.
