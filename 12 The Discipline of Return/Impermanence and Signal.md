# Impermanence and Signal

**Impermanence and Signal** — the reading of *anicca*: impermanence is pervasive across the manifested world, yet patterns differ enormously in durability, making consciousness-evolution the art of sorting what deserves deep storage from spent cache.

The scale of durability is the starting point. A mood may last minutes; a trauma pattern decades; a mathematical relation across civilisations; a [[The Logos|Logos]] across universe-cycles. Impermanence is real along the whole range, but not uniform — and discernment is the task itself: phenomenal reality contains both noise and truth, and consciousness-evolution is the continual sorting of signal from noise, the compression of a life into the few bits that survive death — the material later weighed at [[The Bardo Examination|the bardo examination]].

Suffering enters as a storage error. Duḥkha is nothing but the desire for and grasping of noise, of what is impermanent — spending one's storage on cache that will be flushed. Status, appearance, praise, possession, and even a single moral vocabulary are spent cache; grasping them as permanent identity is the attempt to lower the entropy of what cannot be stabilised, a project guaranteed to fail and to generate the friction of failure.

The same logic explains why the memory of an event can be more real than the event. The memory can be reproduced by the mind, and its governing principles transcend the dimension of time, while the cause-and-effect that produced the event cannot be rerun. Permanence belongs to the reproducible pattern — to [[Information as Record|the recording]] — not to the sensation recorded.

The teaching flags its own tension: "everything is impermanent" can itself become an excuse to preserve nothing. Some truths, skills, [[Promises and the Hierarchy of Freedom|promises]] and loving relations are more durable than the bodies presently carrying them. Wisdom neither clings to every form nor discards every inheritance.
