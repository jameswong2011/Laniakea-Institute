# Toxic Positivity

**Toxic Positivity** — spirituality that demands pleasant interpretation as the price of belonging, so that accurate perception of grief, anger, danger or injustice becomes a moral failure.

The warning is aimed at spirituality itself: practice can become another farm. Where membership in a school or scene is conditioned on reporting uplift, the preferred metaphysical story is protected by the practitioners' own self-censorship, and the community's picture of reality degrades exactly where pain carried its information. Law-of-attraction and manifestation teachings become solipsistic at the point where another person's suffering is read as proof of incorrect vibration while the conditions producing the suffering are left intact. New-age spirituality's explicit request that practitioners gaslight themselves and others toward a positive mindset is the corruption at the bedrock of manifestation practice.

The argument rests on the reading of [[Pain, Pleasure, and Change|pain]] as information: the signal of damage, violated trust, incoherent action, exhausted capacity, or needed change. Denying the signal does not lower the entropy it reports; it breaks the system's ability to detect it. A positive mindset is useful precisely when it enlarges action — and becomes gaslighting when it makes accurate perception a moral failure.

The alternative offered is not gloom. It is [[Sahā and Endurance|endurance]] — staying engaged with a reality seen clearly — and, at civilisational scale, [[Lucid Despair|lucid despair]]: retaining the moral compass while seeing [[The Farmed Planet|the enclosure]] without cosmetic filters. Face the darkness, understand its function, and act from informed agency rather than naïve optimism. This, and not manufactured cheer, is what keeps [[Hope and Despair|hope]] from collapsing into fantasy.
