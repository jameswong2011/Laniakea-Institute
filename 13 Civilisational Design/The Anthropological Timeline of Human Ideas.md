# The Anthropological Timeline of Human Ideas

**The Anthropological Timeline of Human Ideas** — the flagship civilisational proposal: a public, competitive map of how ideas and value-systems produced the present, culminating in a Democratic Project to Define the Human.

Modernity is not one philosophy but a layered settlement among ancient metaphysics, religious law, scientific inquiry, Enlightenment autonomy, nationalism, capitalism, socialism, colonial encounter, industrial discipline, mass media, computation and many local traditions. Each layer solved a problem, created another, and changed the meaning of those beneath it. The Timeline would trace these dialectical movements with enough resolution to show where a current intuition came from and which alternative lineages were excluded; competing timelines keep the project open rather than crowning one official history.

The worked example is the Enlightenment: a necessary counter-pole to corrupted ecclesial power, restoring public reason, empirical criticism and the right to question inherited authority. When that counter-pole hardened into metaphysical materialism, it confused freedom from a captured church with freedom from sacred reality itself; agency expanded while transcendence and durable communal bonds thinned, and production and consumption offered measurable substitutes for meanings they could not reproduce ([[Science and Scientism|scientism]]). The next synthesis must retain the protection of inquiry while reopening the dimensions its reaction closed.

The work has six components:

1. **idea genealogies** — the thinkers, cultures, crises and institutions through which a value changed;
2. **moral framework maps** — what each system treats as good, sacred, real and governable;
3. **consequence histories** — how each framework behaved when embodied;
4. **translation layers** — where opposed traditions ask the same question in incompatible terms;
5. **suppressed branches** — serious alternatives that lost political power without being conceptually resolved;
6. **future forks** — the capacities each framework would preserve, modify or abandon under abundance and artificial intelligence.

The result would be a **Democratic Project to Define the Human**: participants study a menu of traceable moral structures, develop individuated positions, and form tribes without pretending to speak for all humanity — the intake channel of [[Voluntary Moral Communities|voluntary moral communities]]. Declining university humanities would be renewed by open competition among historians, philosophers, artists, contemplatives and machine-assisted teams, judged on breadth, coherence, accessibility, evidential discipline and the ability to represent opponents from within.

Planetary leadership should require public demonstration of this capacity: power over many moral worlds belongs only to those able to recount how they formed, why intelligent people inhabit them and what each fears losing. Historical completeness and empathetic range become qualifications for [[Agency as the Measure|agency]], not decoration. The project is more urgent for human self-determination than a closed physical [[The Open Theory of Everything|theory of everything]]: physics explains the shared interface; the Timeline exposes the objective functions of those about to redesign it. The chain of necessity is short — [[Alignment Begins with Human Purpose|AI alignment requires human alignment]], which requires individuated persons electing a moral stance freely — and ends in a reversal: against the machine that freezes morality by [[Value Lock-In|looking backward]], the Timeline keeps morality evolving by understanding how it was ever made.
