# Lived Abundance

**Lived Abundance** — the required civilisational movement from a materialist, production-based view of reality toward a qualia- and experience-based one: production retained as infrastructure, flourishing measured in lived intervals that no optimiser can fully see.

The diagnosis: a reductionist economy values what can be measured, categorised, multiplied and exchanged. It therefore produces the convenient and abundant while overlooking the fleeting intervals in which life becomes worth living — intimacy, wonder, aesthetic recognition, absorbed craft, humour, reconciliation, revelation, unrepeatable presence. These intervals are [[Qualia|qualia]], and every institutional attempt to capture them illustrates [[Measurement as Transformation|measurement as transformation]]: the recorded part becomes policy while the omitted part is borne privately.

The prescribed movement does not abolish production; production becomes infrastructure rather than telos. Material plenty succeeds when it releases time, attention, health and [[Agency as the Measure|agency]] for forms of flourishing that cannot be made fully legible to an optimiser — and the economy must support these intervals without reducing them to another metric that institutions can game.

The argument extends across time. A society expecting external [[Harvest|harvest]] or near-term replacement maximises current extraction: waste, planned obsolescence, unrecycled materials, fragile supply chains and neglected maintenance reveal a civilisation without confidence in its own future. Long-term self-determination begins when systems are designed for descendants who may reject present preferences — the horizon already written into the definition of agency, which counts "future cycles" among its terms.

The closing test is planetary: not whether an order can predict the future, but whether it leaves future beings enough biological, ecological, technical, memetic and spiritual variation to choose one. Civilisation is thus a womb in the full sense — the recovered meaning of [[Matrix as Womb|matrix]] — protecting development without deciding the final form of the life it carries. Its success is measured at birth, when the emerging consciousness can leave the container, remember what it learned, and create without needing to reproduce the walls that formed it.
