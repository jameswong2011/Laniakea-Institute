# Agency as the Measure

**Agency as the Measure** — the criterion by which every institution, economy and rendered world is evaluated: agency, defined as "the capacity to exert will in ways that enlarge freedom or worthwhile enjoyment for a being and its near relations across future cycles."

The definition is engineered to stop agency collapsing into momentary choice. Addiction supplies choices while shrinking future freedom; wealth may enlarge one person's decision-space by narrowing that of employees, debtors or descendants. Authentic agency is therefore time-extended and relational: it preserves the ability to choose again. The test is not whether an option was selected but whether the being selecting it retains [[Free Will and Decision-Space|decision-space]] — for itself and its near relations — across future cycles.

Applied at scale, the definition becomes the institutional audit. The [[The Farmed Planet|farmed planet]] is to be measured not by living standards or output but by whether people can understand and alter the systems that sustain them; a comfortable enclosure remains an enclosure if every exit has become materially, conceptually or spiritually unavailable. Seeing this clearly while the moral compass stays active produces [[Lucid Despair|lucid despair]] — not nihilism, but the pain of retaining enough agency to see what conformity would make painless. The response is neither forced optimism nor fascination with doom: build small zones where truthful action remains possible, and where institutional incentives have become inseparable from the harm they reproduce, accept that [[Creative Destruction|creative destruction]] may be needed — destruction with memory, craft and local trust rather than ruin.

Within the wider architecture the measure is load-bearing. It supplies one of the five criteria by which [[Simulacra and Fullness|worlds are judged]], the variable that decides when [[Hierarchy and Its Conditions|hierarchy]] hardens into caste, and the quantity that both economic poles concentrate at their terminals. Agency is the system's supreme value.
