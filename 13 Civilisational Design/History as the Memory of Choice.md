# History as the Memory of Choice

**History as the Memory of Choice** — history as a civilisation's karmic ledger: the record of which arrangements were attempted, which promises were made, how power moved, what pain was hidden, what forms endured and which possibilities were pruned.

The stakes of the definition are practical: a society without this memory can be made to repeat an old choice as though it were new. The ledger framing slots history into the wider karmic architecture — what [[The Soul Ledger|the soul ledger]] is to a single stream of experience, history is to a civilisation, with [[Karma as Contextual Law|karma]] understood throughout as record and jurisdiction rather than conscience.

The surviving record is always partial. Archives burn, languages die, victors rename events, institutions protect legitimating narratives, and ordinary lives leave fewer documents than elite lives; even sincere historians work through the [[The Assay|assay]] of their period. History is therefore neither a transparent film of the past nor arbitrary fiction, but a disciplined reconstruction from uneven traces.

Alongside the official chronicle stand other modes of memory: myth, religious memory, architecture, folklore, games, science fiction, dreams and recurring symbolic forms preserve what chronology omits, their information [[Compression and Decompression|compressed]] rather than literal. A fictional cosmic war may retain the moral geometry of a forgotten conflict without supplying its date; a sacred genealogy may preserve a relation among peoples while collapsing centuries into persons — the same logic at work in [[Creation Myths|creation myths]]. The task is to compare modes of memory rather than grant one mode automatic totality.

Finally, ordinary history follows one realised timeline, whereas [[Dependent Origination|dependent origination]] is history enlarged to include consciousness and alternative branches. It asks which choices made a branch available, which unrealised alternatives continued elsewhere in [[The Entropic Branching Engine|the branching engine]], how later interpretations changed the causal meaning of earlier events, and how future attractors organise present action — the study of time as a living relational field, in keeping with [[Time from Choice|time as the exhaust of choice]].
