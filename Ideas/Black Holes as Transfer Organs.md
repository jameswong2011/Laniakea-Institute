# Black Holes as Transfer Organs

## Overview

Black holes are transfer organs within the universe-cell: gateways through which matter, information, and soul-patterns leave one accessible region and participate in another cell or in the memory of a greater body. Black-hole and white-hole processes connect cosmic death with universe birth, allowing higher-order bodies to exchange material and reproduce across scales.
