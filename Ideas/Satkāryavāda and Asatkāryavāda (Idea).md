# Satkāryavāda and Asatkāryavāda

## Overview

Two rival theories of causation. Satkāryavāda holds that an effect already exists, latent, within its cause — the pot hidden in the clay. Asatkāryavāda holds that the effect is a genuinely new creation — the pot did not exist until it was made. Pressed hard, both fail: if the effect already exists, producing it is redundant; if it is wholly new, nothing links it to its cause. The dilemma dissolves fixed notions of production and shows that arising, like everything else, lacks intrinsic reality.
