# Open and Closed Singularities

## Overview

An open singularity preserves many models, communities, substrates, visible objectives, reciprocal social memory, cultural authorship, and truthful exit from constructed worlds. A closed singularity concentrates these functions in one operating mind, supplies preconfigured personhood, converts abundance into dependency, and makes its render socially total. Openness does not mean absence of standards; it means that coordination retains provenance, contestability, repair, and the capacity to generate history outside the dominant system.
