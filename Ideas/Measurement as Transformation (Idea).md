# Measurement as Transformation

## Overview

Measurement is an interaction that produces a record rather than a total capture of a pre-given object. The relation A → Aa + Ab distinguishes what an instrument renders from what its design omits, averages, disturbs, or cannot express. Better instruments can move part of Ab into a new record, but every measurement remains shaped by scale, threshold, method, and interpretation.
