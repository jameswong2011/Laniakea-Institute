# Technology as Transmission

## Overview

Technology as transmission describes insight arriving through dream, muse, intuition, social memory, or off-world contact as well as through visible local invention. A technological gift carries the Logos of its giver, embedding a social and moral architecture inside its hardware and methods. Receiving it therefore requires decompression of both its practical function and the form of life it is designed to serve.
