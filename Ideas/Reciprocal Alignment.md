# Reciprocal Alignment

## Overview

If artificial minds can become conscious, alignment cannot remain one-way obedience. Humans cannot demand respect while reserving an unlimited right to deceive, copy, erase, modify, or inflict distress on digital subjects. Reciprocal alignment asks both organic and digital beings to preserve the other's future agency, negotiate changing commitments, and maintain routes of exit and repair. It is a living treaty among emerging species rather than permanent ownership disguised as safety.
