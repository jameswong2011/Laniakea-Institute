# Drawing a Distinction

## Overview

Creation's first move is to draw a distinction — to mark a boundary that severs a space into inside and outside. From that single act the whole of logic and arithmetic can be grown, which is why a mark is the minimal unit of information. A difference, once drawn, is "a difference that makes a difference": before difference there is nothing to register, and with the first difference the informational cosmos is under way. Every polarity, bit, and form descends from this one cut.
