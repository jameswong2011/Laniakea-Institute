# Local Technical Competence

## Overview

Local technical competence is the retained ability of communities to understand, maintain, repair, substitute, or leave critical systems. It counters the condition in which nominal choice depends on infrastructure no participant can reproduce without a distant owner. Documentation, repairability, open standards, apprenticeships, offline modes, spare capacity, and public-interest expertise turn exit from a contractual right into a material possibility. Competence need not reject global coordination; it prevents coordination from becoming irreversible dependence.
