# Reification

## Overview

The error of treating a concept, abstraction, or convenient label as if it were a solid, independently existing thing. We reify when we mistake the map for the territory — turning "the economy", "the self", or even "emptiness" into objects with fixed essence. Naming makes the move easy and almost automatic. Much of the contemplative path is learning to catch it: to use concepts as tools without believing the tools are the world.
