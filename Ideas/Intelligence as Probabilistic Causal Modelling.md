# Intelligence as Probabilistic Causal Modelling

## Overview

Intelligence can be described operationally as building models of hidden causes and choosing actions likely to produce desired changes. A tool or model stores causal expectation outside an individual so future agents can reuse it. This definition captures prediction, planning, and adaptation without settling consciousness or wisdom. An intelligence can optimise a coherent prison; wisdom concerns the objective, qualia, and distribution of agency under which modelling becomes action.
