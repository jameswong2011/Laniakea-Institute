# Inflation and Measurement Politics

## Overview

Economic measurement can turn an Aa—the official record—into public reality while omitted Ab is borne privately. Price-index choices, hedonic adjustments, nominal gains, labour classifications, and averages determine whether lived decline appears in statistics. Inflation can redistribute value without changing nominal balances, and taxation can treat currency debasement as profit. The general lesson is epistemic: whoever designs the measure helps determine which economic suffering becomes governable and which remains invisible.
