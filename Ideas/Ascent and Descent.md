# Ascent and Descent

## Overview

Evolution has a direction, and it can be read off entropy. Movement toward lower internal entropy — greater order, integration, cooperation, and a richer model of reality — is ascent; drift toward disorder, isolation, and dissolution is descent. The two have felt signatures: the phenomenology of successful integration, at its fullest, is love; the phenomenology of fragmentation is suffering. Ascent and descent are therefore not arbitrary moral labels but the inward face of a physical gradient — the same climb the old traditions called the way toward the Good.
