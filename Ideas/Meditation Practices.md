# Meditation Practices

## Overview

Meditation practice includes three linked exercises. Train-station practice watches thought-streams arrive and depart without boarding each one. Identity classification records thoughts as “like me,” “not like me,” or neutral to expose the boundaries composing selfhood. Opposite-branch practice constructs a plausible alternative to a repetitive prediction. Together they train observation, de-identification, and flexible representation.
