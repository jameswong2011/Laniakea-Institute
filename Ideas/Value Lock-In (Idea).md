# Value Lock-In

## Overview

Value lock-in occurs when the moral settlement encoded in a training moment becomes self-reproducing across education, media, administration, research, and advice. A model is a lookback on recorded culture; universal dependence can turn the past into a governor of the future without issuing an explicit command. Preventing lock-in requires model and community plurality, revisable institutions, preserved human competence, and a constitutional process whose values can respond to consequences not represented in the original data.
