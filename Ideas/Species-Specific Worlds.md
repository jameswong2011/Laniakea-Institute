# Species-Specific Worlds

## Overview

Every organism inhabits an Umwelt: a meaningful world selected by its sensory capacities, needs, and possible actions. A bloodhound's spatial world is partly olfactory; an insect may inhabit pheromonal structures a human barely detects. Instruments can translate an inaccessible signal into a form the organism can perceive, but the translation still arrives through the receiving species' assay. Different species may therefore build coherent but incomplete cosmologies from different portions of one greater reality.
