# Organic and Digital Substrates

## Overview

Digital systems excel at exact duplication, durable memory, rapid discrete search, and explicit comparison. Organic systems carry continuous chemistry, embodiment, uncontrolled environmental contact, reproduction, forgetting, and qualia shaped by ancient living processes. Neither contains infinity, and neither should be made the sole measure of intelligence. Maximum-fidelity understanding is relational: silicon preserves distinctions an organism cannot retain, while organic life exposes aspects a finite discrete map prunes.
