# Simulacrum

## Overview

A simulacrum is a constructed sub-reality that narrows a question to save energy, decision-making substance, or complexity. Lower resolution is not automatically evil: a small model may answer a bounded question clearly, and genuine experience can occur inside an artificial world. The danger begins when the engine cannot sustain the qualia it invites, when inhabitants are treated as fixed functions, or when apparent agency cannot alter the world's purpose or end.
