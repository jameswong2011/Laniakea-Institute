# Capitalism and Socialism as Poles

## Overview

Capitalism protects initiative, distributed information, voluntary exchange, accumulation, and reward for risk; unchecked, it purchases law and ends in corporate caste. Socialism protects common provision, planning, equality, and the right not to be discarded by markets; unchecked, it centralises allocation and can suppress local information and variation. The dialectical answer is not one fixed midpoint. Different goods require different governance, and concentrated agency is the shared danger beneath both extremes.
