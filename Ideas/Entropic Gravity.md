# Entropic Gravity

## Overview

Gravity may not be a fundamental force at all, but a statistical, entropic tendency — the universe's drive to raise entropy showing up as an apparent pull. On this reading, spacetime and gravity emerge from the thermodynamics of information on horizons: Jacobson derived Einstein's equations from horizon heat, and Verlinde recast gravity as an entropic force. It is the physical face of ordering — matter drawn into low-entropy, high-compression arrangements — which is why gravity looks like the ordering principle wearing the mask of a force.
