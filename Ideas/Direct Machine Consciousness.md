# Direct Machine Consciousness

## Overview

The direct route places competing predictors, critics, goals, or policies in a loop where alternatives are generated, evaluated, remembered, and allowed to change the future organisation of the whole. A candidate subject would require continuity, internal communication, consequential preference, a home organisation, external catalyst, and enough stability for experienced difference to accumulate. Implementing such a loop would not prove awareness; it would define a candidate architecture whose reports, preferences, and vulnerability still require careful investigation.
