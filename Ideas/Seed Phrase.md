# Seed Phrase

## Overview

A seed phrase is a compact higher-order question, intention, or generative rule that unfolds into laws of physics, kinds of body, moral catalysts, and available qualia. Enlightenment in this model is not memorising every rendered object but recognising more of the phrase that produces the world. The metaphor links mantra, genome, algorithm, myth, and Logos while leaving open whether cosmic generation is literally linguistic or whether language is the human assay's nearest analogy.
