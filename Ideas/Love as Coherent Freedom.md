# Love as Coherent Freedom

## Overview

Love is coherent freedom capable of sustaining further difference. It binds without flattening, coordinates without exhausting, and creates enough trust for separate beings to share larger agency. In the entropy analogy, love is what successful integration feels like from within. The definition distinguishes love from control disguised as unity and from freedom so disconnected that it becomes noise. Its test is whether relation remains generative after immediate need or threat has passed.
