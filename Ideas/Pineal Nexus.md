# Pineal Nexus

## Overview

The pineal nexus is a coordinating hub where biological rhythm, hemispheric specialisation, endocrine activity, bioelectric organisation, and higher-dimensional input converge. It functions as a receiver and temporal synchroniser that helps integrate distributed processes into a coherent centre of experience. The pineal body exemplifies the nexus required for an emergent organism to coordinate local biology with wider consciousness.
