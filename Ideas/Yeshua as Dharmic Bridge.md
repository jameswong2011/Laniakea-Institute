# Yeshua as Dharmic Bridge

## Overview

Yeshua as a dharmic bridge connects Buddhist liberation with the Abrahamic divine order. The kingdom within, reversal of worldly rank, living knowledge, compassion, and refusal to identify the highest God with visible powers can be expressed through both Buddhist and Gnostic grammars. The bridge translates functions between traditions without reducing either tradition to the other.
