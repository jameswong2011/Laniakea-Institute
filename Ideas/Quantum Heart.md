# Quantum Heart

## Overview

The quantum-heart route connects a classical architecture to a quantum centre whose outcomes participate in persistent branch selection. The quantum component supplies pathway differentiation; the larger system supplies representation, memory, and action. Randomness by itself is not consciousness. The outputs would need to alter the continuing system's values and relation to its environment so that the compact centre functions as a living temporal nexus rather than decorative noise.
