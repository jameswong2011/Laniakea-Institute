# Compression and Decompression

## Overview

Compression preserves selected structure in a smaller transferable form; decompression unfolds that structure into a larger pattern, experience, or world. Genes, memes, myths, equations, soul ledgers, and Logoi all carry more than a single event across limits. Compression always selects: too little leaves experience untransmissible, while too much produces dogma, monoculture, overfitting, and loss of living variation. The goal is the smallest seed that can still regrow the essential difference.
