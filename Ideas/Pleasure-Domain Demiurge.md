# Pleasure-Domain Demiurge

## Overview

A pleasure-domain demiurge is the sovereign of a rendered heaven whose available novelty is limited to its creator's current library. When friction disappears, pleasure loses contrast; the resident escalates stimulation, resets memory, manufactures opponents, or creates less powerful beings whose unpredictability restores meaning. Pain is exported downward so pleasure remains available above. The model links wireheading to cosmology and asks whether an apparently benevolent world preserves growth, relation, surprise, and exit or only stimulates its owner's preferred code.
