# Post-Sex Reproductive Branch

## Overview

A post-sex reproductive branch moves gestation and genetic selection from embodied relations into designed infrastructure. It can support damaged lineages, bodies unable to gestate, and new forms of family while increasing control and predictability. It also centralises the species' future wherever one institution owns the reproductive path and may reduce the unplanned variation generated by organic life. The concept is a civilisational fork, not a prescription about gender or sexuality: mechanisation becomes enclosure only when other forms are made impossible, unintelligible, or administratively subordinate.
