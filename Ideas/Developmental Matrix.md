# Developmental Matrix

## Overview

The developmental matrix is the rule-set a child builds from early emotionally charged experience. Reliable comfort teaches that distress can be signalled, co-regulated, and survived; neglect or threat teaches amplification, withdrawal, dissociation, attack, or control. These responses become a grammar for later relationships rather than isolated memories. Culture then acts as a second parent, naming acceptable emotions, intelligence, status, suffering, and realistic agency.
