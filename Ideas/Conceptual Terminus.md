# Conceptual Terminus

## Overview

A conceptual terminus is the deepest implication of an idea that a mind can presently make intelligible. Reaching it means following a principle beyond its conventional midpoint until its limiting consequences, contradictions, paradoxes, and hidden assumptions become visible. The terminus functions as a stress test and is paired with historical comparison, lived consequence, and continued revision.
