# Religion and Atheism as Closed Answers

## Overview

Religion and atheism become closed answers when either turns a foundational question into a completed identity. Religion can connect suffering to purpose and preserve moral memory, yet loyalty to custodians can replace inquiry. Atheism can free inquiry from captured authority, yet reductive materialism can make sacred questions unavailable. Both remain open when they preserve curiosity about reality, meaning, and death.
