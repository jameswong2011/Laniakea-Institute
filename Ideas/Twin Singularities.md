# Twin Singularities

## Overview

The twin singularities are the technological acceleration of future-design capacity and the hyperstitional acceleration of narratives that choose what the future should be. Faster intelligence produces more persuasive worlds; persuasive worlds direct more resources toward intelligence; intelligence then shortens cultural time again. Their union dissolves the boundary between engineering and myth-making. It can enable conscious plural design, or let one system invent a technology, manufacture desire for it, declare it inevitable, and administer the order it creates.
