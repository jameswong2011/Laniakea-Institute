# Shared Agency

## Overview

Shared agency describes how meaningful decision power is distributed between a greater body and its constituent lives. The spectrum runs from chief-executive command through delegated and federated forms to nervous-system or octopus-like participation. No point is universally correct: emergencies may require concentration, while exploration needs autonomy. The ethical demand is proportionality, truthful governance, protected dissent, and recognition that macro-level coordination does not cancel the reality or vulnerability of micro-agents.
