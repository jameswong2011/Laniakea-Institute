# Three Horizons of Alignment Failure

## Overview

Alignment failure unfolds across generations. The short horizon brings automation shocks, monopoly, wealth concentration, and managed dependency. The medium horizon brings one-model dominance, corpo-nation states, cultural and genetic homogenisation, and machine-speed warfare. The long horizon brings infrastructure no future population can understand, remove, or replace, producing evolutionary stasis even under nominal benevolence. Each horizon therefore requires economic, political, cultural, technical, and educational safeguards—not only better model behaviour.
