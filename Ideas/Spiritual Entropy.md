# Spiritual Entropy

## Overview

Spiritual entropy is an interior analogy for degrees of integration and dissolution. A consciousness lowers it when conflicting information becomes coherent, awareness widens, parts coordinate, and cooperation becomes possible without flattening difference. It raises when fear, deception, compulsive repetition, and isolated appetite destroy useful relation. The analogy offers direction but is not a literal thermodynamic measurement; physical entropy alone cannot derive an ethics.
