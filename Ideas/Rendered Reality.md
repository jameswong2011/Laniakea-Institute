# Rendered Reality

## Overview

Rendered reality is a world whose experienced distinctions are generated under a rule-set from a level not wholly contained within the world. Rendering does not make the world unreal: pain, friendship, learning, and moral consequence remain real for its inhabitants. Observation functions as interaction and registration, while render quality depends on fidelity, fullness, agency, and the complexity of qualia the world can sustain.
