# First Ones and Cosmic Seeding

## Overview

The First Ones are the earliest complex consciousnesses to arise within a universe-cell, before any prior local civilisation could harvest them. Mature planetary or stellar minds are then imagined to seed biochemical templates on suitable worlds, creating new lineages that vary the seeder's archetype. Firstness does not guarantee permanent wisdom: an ancestral civilisation can become rigid or predatory. Purity in a renewal cycle means retaining enough uncommitted range to act as a stem cell of creation.
