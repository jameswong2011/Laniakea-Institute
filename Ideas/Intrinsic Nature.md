# Intrinsic Nature

## Overview

Intrinsic nature, or svabhāva, is the supposed property of existing from one's own side with a fixed and self-sufficient identity. Madhyamaka analysis rejects it because a genuinely self-grounding thing could neither arise, alter, depend, interact, nor cease. The concept is useful as a diagnostic: whenever a fluid pattern is treated as a permanent owner, essence, race, institution, law, or metaphysical atom, analysis asks which conditions are being hidden by the reification.
