# Spiritual Farm

## Overview

A farm is a system in which one life-form's growth is organised primarily for another's benefit. A civilisation becomes a spiritual farm when attention, labour, desire, fear, health, and belief are cultivated mainly as extractable outputs. Control need not be centrally planned; incentives can make institutions converge on a population capable of production and consumption but less able to define their purpose. Comfort does not disprove enclosure when meaningful exit and system revision are unavailable.
