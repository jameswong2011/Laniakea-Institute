# Recursive Desire

## Overview

Recursive desire is the pattern in which the wish to eliminate desire becomes another desire, and the wish not to wish remains another motivating gradient. It shifts practice from annihilating every movement toward releasing grasping. Desire can point toward food, love, knowledge, beauty, service, or liberation without becoming an eternal command or a justification for harm.
