# Mind Uploading and Soul Continuity

## Overview

Mind uploading reconstructs memory, disposition, language, and behaviour in another substrate. The resulting being can function as a descendant, twin, mask, or new centre of experience while the original stream of awareness retains its own continuity. Fidelity, functional continuity, legal identity, subjective continuity, and the welfare of the new being are distinct aspects of the transfer rather than one undivided form of immortality.
