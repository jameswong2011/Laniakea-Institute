# Friendship as Memetic Collaboration

## Overview

Friendship begins through recognised similarity that lowers translation cost, but its evolutionary value grows when deeper compatible purpose makes difference useful. A friendship can operate as a small social-memory complex: distributed memory, mutual correction, shared projects, and an enlarged decision-space neither person can maintain alone. Loneliness in this account is not only absence of company; it is absence of a channel with enough bandwidth to receive and answer one's significant patterns.
