# God-Body and Multiversal Organism

## Overview

A God-Body is the emergent organism formed when many universe-cells collaborate at a higher order. It is the nearest cosmic body or local Brahman relative to our universe, not the absolute totality. Just as an organism has capacities no cell possesses alone, a multiversal body may have its own memory, metabolism, decisions, conflicts, and qualia. From above, a law of physics could appear as a biochemical habit of that greater life.
