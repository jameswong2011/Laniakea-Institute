# Stability and Novelty

## Overview

Human appetite seeks new sensation and information; contemplative practice trains contentment in relative stasis. Neither pole is sufficient. Ceaseless novelty becomes scattered dependence on stimulation, and absolute stability goes inert and refuses creation. Meditation develops stability so that novelty can be chosen rather than compulsively consumed, and creative life keeps tranquillity from sealing itself into a static heaven. The working criterion is rhythmic: enough order for trust and memory, and enough opening for difference and surprise.
