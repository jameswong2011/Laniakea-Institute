# Stability and Novelty

## Overview

Human appetite seeks new sensation and information, while contemplative practice trains contentment in relative stasis. Neither pole is sufficient. Ceaseless novelty becomes scattered dependence on stimulation; absolute stability becomes inert and refuses creation. Meditation develops stability so novelty can be chosen rather than compulsively consumed, while creative life prevents tranquillity from sealing itself into a static heaven. The working criterion is rhythmic: enough order for trust and memory, and enough opening for difference and surprise.
