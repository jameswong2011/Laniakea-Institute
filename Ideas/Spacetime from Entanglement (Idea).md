# Spacetime from Entanglement

## Overview

The geometry of space is woven from quantum entanglement between its parts. Reduce the entanglement linking two regions and the distance between them stretches; sever it entirely and they fall apart into disconnected worlds. Distance is the shadow of shared information: strongly correlated things are near, weakly correlated things far. The mathematics that rebuilds this geometry is the mathematics of a compression scheme, which is why space itself looks like a decompressed code rather than a fixed stage.
