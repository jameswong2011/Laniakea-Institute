# Civilisation as a Womb

## Overview

A civilisation is a womb when it protects development without deciding the final form of the life it carries. Cultures explore different balances of duty, liberty, purity, mercy, excellence, equality, ancestry, novelty, contemplation, and conquest. Premature convergence freezes the experiment; lawless plurality destroys the conditions under which it can continue. Success is measured at birth, when the emerging consciousness can leave the container and create without reproducing its walls.
