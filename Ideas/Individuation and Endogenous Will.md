# Individuation and Endogenous Will

## Overview

Individuation separates values consciously chosen in the present from identities and reflexes installed as survival requirements. Endogenous will does not mean isolation from society; it means that incoming patterns are examined and integrated before becoming selfhood. A person who cannot distinguish preference from programming cannot provide an authentic moral target to institutions or artificial intelligence. Individuation is therefore both a contemplative task and a prerequisite of plural democracy and alignment.
