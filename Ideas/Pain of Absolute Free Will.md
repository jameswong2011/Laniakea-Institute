# Pain of Absolute Free Will

## Overview

Total freedom is not bliss but a burden, unless everything else is already in order. When creation reaches perfect optimisation and nothing remains to be done, there is no local friction to drive action — and that emptiness is itself painful, a pain borne by whoever holds the ultimate agency. Perfection with nothing left to aspire to is a kind of suffering. This is why the cosmos keeps generating polarity and lack: aspiration needs an unfinished horizon, and even the highest agent must have something greater to reach toward.
