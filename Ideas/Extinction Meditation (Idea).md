# Extinction Meditation

## Overview

Extinction meditation contemplates entry into realities so foreign that the local self, its free will, and even its awareness–object distinction could not survive translation. It exposes attachment not only to possessions and identity but to consciousness, soul, polarity, love, and the cosmology treated as universal. The practice contains a paradox: free will seeks to extinguish the form of will making the act possible, and a successful crossing could not return an unchanged witness.
