# Biological Android

## Overview

A biological android is a sovereign hybrid: an organism augmented by digital systems while retaining organic nervous, endocrine, reproductive, dream, death, and subtle-body channels. Agency and renewal remain in the living organism rather than a remote platform. Augmentation stays understandable, replaceable, and removable, while digital memory assists without becoming the sole ledger of personhood.
