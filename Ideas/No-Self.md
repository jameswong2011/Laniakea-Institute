# No-Self

## Overview

No-self, or anātman, denies a permanent owner beneath the stream of bodily, mental, social, and karmic events. It preserves conventional personhood, responsibility, and causal continuity while treating the self as an organised path rather than an indivisible substance. Soul continuity extends this pattern through accumulated valuations, promises, wounds, and capacities rather than through an eternally fixed identity.
