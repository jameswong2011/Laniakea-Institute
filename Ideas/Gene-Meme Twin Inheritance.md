# Gene-Meme Twin Inheritance

## Overview

Humanity inherits through two interacting channels. Genes carry slow biological adaptation; memes carry language, techniques, values, institutions, and images across minds. Culture changes diet, development, mating, disease, labour, and therefore the selection pressures acting on biology, while the body constrains which cultures can be embodied. This dual inheritance explains how one broad genome supports sailors, monks, engineers, artists, and digital creators without waiting for speciation.
