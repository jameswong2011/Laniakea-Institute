# Dependent Origination

## Overview

Dependent origination means that events and identities arise through conditions rather than isolated essences. It applies to objects, perception, suffering, institutions, causal stories, gods, and the concepts used to describe them. A present state carries past conditions, possible branches, later interpretations, and future attractors. Whatever is dependently arisen is therefore empty of independent own-being.
