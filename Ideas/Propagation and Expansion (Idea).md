# Propagation and Expansion

## Overview

A rendered cosmos runs on two rates, not one. Propagation is motion through the field (a photon crossing space, a planet orbiting), governed by the speed of light. Expansion is growth of the field itself — space stretching, the fractal unfolding, possibility branching — governed by the exponential constant. One rate describes change within a layer, the other the growth of the layer. The distinction shows up physically in cosmic inflation, where space stretched exponentially while nothing inside moved faster than light.
