# Non-Self-Similar Infinity

## Overview

Non-self-similar infinity names realities outside the local fractal family. They may not organise experience through gravity, entropy, ordinary time, bodies, or any familiar contrast, even if relation and emergence still occur. The concept prevents one successful cosmology from being mistaken for the whole of Sacred Infinity. Encountering such a reality would require new primitives of thought, not simply discovery of an exotic object inside the current grammar.
