# Qualia

## Overview

Qualia are the felt actuality of relation: redness, grief, warmth, humiliation, relief, moral clarity, or sacred presence as experienced from within. They are actual as events and empty as substances because every quale depends on contrast, a sense or subtle field, a history, an interactee, and a consciousness able to be affected. Qualia give consequence value; without them a law would regulate outputs without anything ever being lived.
