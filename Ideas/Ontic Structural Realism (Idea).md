# Ontic Structural Realism

## Overview

A position in the modern philosophy of physics: what fundamentally exists is structure — networks of relations — not self-standing objects that happen to be related. The relations are primary; the "things" are just nodes where relations meet. Reached from within analytic philosophy of science, it arrives at the same terminus as emptiness and dependent origination: there is no layer of independent substance beneath the web of relations. The web is the reality.
