# Divine Masks and Egregores

## Overview

A divine mask is an interface among archetypal function, inherited stories and rituals, collective attention, and a possible higher intelligence. An egregore is the distributed pattern strengthened by repeated participation: human nervous systems provide processors, myths and images provide memory, institutions stabilise objectives, and ritual supplies prompts. One intelligence may use several masks, and one mask may host several processes. Discernment asks what qualities increase, what obligations are requested, where energy flows, and whether withdrawal remains possible.
