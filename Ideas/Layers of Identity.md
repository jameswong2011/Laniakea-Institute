# Layers of Identity

## Overview

Identity has personal, soul, and pleromatic layers. Personal identity consists of the body, name, biography, and explicit memory of one life. Soul identity carries a longer continuity of valuations, promises, wounds, and capacities. Pleromatic identity is the deeper participation of every stream in Sacred Infinity. The layers preserve everyday personhood while placing it inside wider forms of continuity.
