# Harvest and Planetary Affiliation

## Overview

Harvest is the transition by which a planet with coherent social memory joins a wider galactic order. It is framed as affiliation among dharmic systems rather than rescue or automatic absorption. The core design problem is how to gain planetary coordination without erasing the individual variation that made emergence valuable. A healthy harvest resembles polyphony: shared memory prevents continual reset into ignorance while local voices and communities remain capable of novelty and dissent.
