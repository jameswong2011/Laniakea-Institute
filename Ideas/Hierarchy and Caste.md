# Hierarchy and Caste

## Overview

Hierarchy is a recurring coordination technology for differences in skill, responsibility, knowledge, time horizon, and risk. It becomes caste when temporary function hardens into permanent claim over another's agency, when truth is occulted as a reward for service, or when inherited mystique replaces demonstrated competence. The question is not whether every rank can vanish, but whether rank remains legible, contestable, proportionate, and capable of dissolving when its task changes.
