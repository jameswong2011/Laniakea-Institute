# Logos

## Overview

Logos is the organising intelligence or grammar that makes a coherent possibility-space. It gives difference regularity, translation, proportion, boundary, and permission so that distinct processes can collaborate without immediately dissolving one another. Logoi recur fractally: a galaxy, star, planet, civilisation, person, or designed world can organise a lower-order field. Logos is not the absolute source; it is both a product of earlier experience and a maker of new conditions.
