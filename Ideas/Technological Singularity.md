# Technological Singularity

## Overview

The technological singularity is the threshold at which machine-assisted intelligence improves the systems that produce machine-assisted intelligence, shortening development cycles beyond ordinary human forecasting. It is a transfer of effective future-design capacity rather than one magical instant. The threshold is crossed when research, engineering, media, finance, governance, and war depend on processes that human decision-makers cannot independently understand, reproduce, or interrupt.
