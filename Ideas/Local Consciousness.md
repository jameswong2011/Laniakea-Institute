# Local Consciousness

## Overview

A local consciousness is an organised centre of experience formed where a home stream meets a non-home environment and must register, remember, and choose among meaningful alternatives. Universal awareness and a particular viewpoint are therefore not identical. The point of view requires a boundary, a continuing organisation, and consequences that matter to later states. This two-scale account lets consciousness be primordial without declaring every possible partition a complete person.
