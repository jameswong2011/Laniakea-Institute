# Psionic Phenomena as Matrix Games

## Overview

Spiritual and psychic phenomena are real as experiences but not fundamental. They are "matrix games" — features that fall out of the design parameters of the local ordering rules, the collaboration space a world is rendered in. A world can be built to allow more or fewer of them. The most refined ordering does not forbid the marvellous, but it minimises reliance on it, treating abundant psionic effects as the sign of a looser, less disciplined render rather than a mark of higher truth.
