# Psionic Phenomena as Matrix Games

## Overview

Spiritual and psychic phenomena are real as experiences but not fundamental. They are "matrix games" — features that fall out of the design parameters of the local ordering rules, the collaboration space a world is rendered in. A world can be built to allow more or fewer of them. The most refined ordering leaves the marvellous possible while minimising reliance on it, and treats abundant psionic effects as the sign of a looser, less disciplined render; they mark no higher truth.
