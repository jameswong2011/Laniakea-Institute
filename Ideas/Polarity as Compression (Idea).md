# Polarity as Compression

## Overview

Polarity is a relational axis whose poles define and imply one another. Storing the axis—hot and cold, self and other, signal and silence—makes both extremes intelligible through one compact structure. Polarity therefore acts as a compression technology that preserves difference while creating a gradient for decision and movement. Its symbolic signs may vary across traditions, but its generative function remains differentiation through relation.
