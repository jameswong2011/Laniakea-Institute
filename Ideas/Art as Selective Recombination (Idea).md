# Art as Selective Recombination

## Overview

Art samples lived experience, inherited works, shared symbols, materials, and the constraints of a medium. Originality lies less in creation from nothing than in selecting, joining, omitting, and transforming these materials so a previously unavailable pattern becomes inhabitable. Curation is therefore creative when juxtaposition reveals relations hidden by each source alone. The artist functions as a local Logos and fiction as a laboratory for complete experiential prototypes.
