# First Differentiation

## Overview

First differentiation is the symbolic movement by which undivided capacity becomes capable of contrast. Elementary consciousness discovers sequential change and parallel partition: one state can become another, while one region can differ from another. The One becomes Many without leaving the field in which multiplicity occurs. The model explains why difference, imperfection, and local boundaries are not merely failures of unity but conditions for surprise, choice, and collaborative novelty.
