# Manichaean Synthesis

## Overview

Manichaean synthesis places the prophetic lines of Zoroaster, Buddha, and Jesus within one revelatory history. Its conflict between living light and imprisoning darkness joins several traditions around a shared account of cosmic captivity, moral struggle, and liberation. The synthesis treats religious systems as local expressions of related sacred problems while preserving their distinct doctrines and practices.
