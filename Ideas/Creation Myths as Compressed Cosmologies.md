# Creation Myths as Compressed Cosmologies

## Overview

Creation myths are compressed accounts given to a young civilisation asking how existence began. Stellar teachers express cosmic origins through symbolic narratives shaped by their own faction, experience, and assay. A myth can be perspectival and load-bearing at once, organising a culture by carrying cosmic and moral relations through the limited language available to its recipients.
