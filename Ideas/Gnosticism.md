# Gnosticism

## Overview

Gnosticism describes a Pleroma or fullness, Sophia’s disrupted creation, a limited Demiurge and archons, divine capacity trapped or obscured in the material order, and gnosis as liberating recognition. Its central structure is a deficient world whose inhabitants retain a connection to a fuller reality and can recover agency by understanding the nature and origin of their enclosure.
