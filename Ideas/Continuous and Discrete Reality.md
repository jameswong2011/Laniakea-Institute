# Continuous and Discrete Reality

## Overview

Continuous and discrete reality describes a continuous, fractal base from which discrete structures emerge or are rendered locally. Quantisation can govern a particular scale without making Sacred Infinity a collection of final pixels, much as liquidity exists without belonging to one molecule. Finite digital systems approximate a continuum to useful precision while remaining bounded representations within a larger continuous field.
