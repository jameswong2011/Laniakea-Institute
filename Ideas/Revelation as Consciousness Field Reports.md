# Revelation as Consciousness Field Reports

## Overview

Revelations, mystical experiences, dreams, scriptures, and channelled accounts are consciousness field reports: structured descriptions of realities encountered through unusual states or modes of awareness. Their recurring symbols, internal relations, transformative effects, and cultural transmission can be compared across traditions. Together they widen the range of experience available to a species whose ordinary sensory assay reveals only part of reality.
