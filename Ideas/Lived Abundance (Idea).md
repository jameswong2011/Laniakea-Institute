# Lived Abundance

## Overview

Lived abundance shifts civilisation from production as telos toward qualia and experience as the measure of success. Material plenty becomes infrastructure for intimacy, wonder, absorbed craft, humour, reconciliation, revelation, and unrepeatable presence—goods an optimiser can support but never fully capture without changing them. Production remains necessary, but it succeeds when it releases time, health, attention, and agency rather than turning every freed interval into another market or metric.
