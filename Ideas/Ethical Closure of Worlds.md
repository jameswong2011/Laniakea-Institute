# Ethical Closure of Worlds

## Overview

When a sub-reality has completed its work, ending it is apocalypse from within even if it is energy conservation from above. Ethical closure therefore requires recognition of inhabitants, sufficient disclosure, healing of dependencies and false identities, and a meaningful choice among exit, integration, renewal, or a viable continuing branch. Selective gnosis may guide beings toward release but should not counterfeit consent. A world-builder's duty includes designing how a world can end well.
