# Qualia Stickers

## Overview

Qualia stickers are durable experiential valuations attached to a continuing stream without implying a permanent owner. They describe how a life can leave more than factual memory: humiliation, courage, love, avoidance, skill, and unfinished obligation alter the weighting of future states. When branches split or minds merge, the distribution of these weights becomes an open problem. The term is a bridge between karmic continuity, bundle theories of self, and the qualitative reality of experience.
