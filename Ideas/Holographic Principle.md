# Holographic Principle

## Overview

The maximum information a region of space can hold scales with its boundary area, not its volume. A three-dimensional interior can therefore be described completely by data written on its two-dimensional surface, one dimension lower — the interior is a projection of the boundary, as a flat holographic film reconstructs a solid image. It is the strongest physical hint that reality is compression: a lower-dimensional record decompressed into the world we inhabit. Its exact, calculable form is the AdS/CFT correspondence.
