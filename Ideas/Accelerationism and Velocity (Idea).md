# Accelerationism and Velocity

## Overview

Accelerationism seeks to cross an unavoidable technological transition quickly rather than prolong it through ineffective resistance. Velocity adds direction, timing, and the capacity to stop to the pursuit of speed. The distinction matters because rapid development can cross irreversible thresholds before consent, governance, or organic alternatives exist, while deliberate non-action can preserve possibilities that premature deployment would close.
