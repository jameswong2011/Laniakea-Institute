# Paraconsistent Negation

## Overview

A form of logic that can tolerate certain contradictions at a boundary without everything collapsing into nonsense. Where standard logic says a single contradiction proves anything, paraconsistent logic contains it. It is the tool some modern readers use to make sense of the four-cornered negation: at the very limit of what can be said about reality, statements that seem to affirm and deny at once can be handled precisely rather than dismissed. The edge of the sayable has its own careful grammar.
