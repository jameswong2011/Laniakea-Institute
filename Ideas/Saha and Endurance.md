# Saha and Endurance

## Overview

Sahā, meaning “Endure,” is the capacity to remain morally and perceptually engaged after easy consolation fails. Endurance bears a difficult gradient long enough for its information to become transformation while still allowing action, grief, boundaries, and departure. Its purpose is wider agency rather than passive tolerance of pain or subjugation.
