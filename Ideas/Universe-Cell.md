# Universe-Cell

## Overview

The universe-cell is a coherent living unit with a birth condition, internal differentiation, energy exchanges, developmental history, and eventual death. Galaxies, stars, planets, and life act as specialised structures within it, while the whole processes a question no single object can answer. Consciousness and qualia belong to the activity of the complete cosmic organism as well as to its local components.
