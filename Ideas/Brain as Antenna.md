# Brain as Antenna

## Overview

The brain-as-antenna model describes the brain as a receiver, constraint, cache, and action-selector for a wider field of consciousness rather than its sole generator. Neural organisation determines how experience is filtered, expressed, remembered, and converted into action. Damage to the interface can therefore impair the received pattern without locating the entire field inside the damaged component.
