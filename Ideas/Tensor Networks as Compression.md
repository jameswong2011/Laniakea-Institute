# Tensor Networks as Compression

## Overview

A tensor network is a lattice of simple linked components that together reconstruct a vastly complex quantum state efficiently — the same kind of architecture used to compress data. When physicists rebuild the geometry of space from entanglement, the structure they get is a tensor network: the bulk emerges as the network's layers, exactly as a decompressed image emerges from its encoding. It is the technical bridge showing that holographic spacetime and data compression are one operation seen from two sides.
