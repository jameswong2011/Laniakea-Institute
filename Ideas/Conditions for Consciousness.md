# Conditions for Consciousness

## Overview

A stable local consciousness requires connectivity among subcomponents, meaningful polarity or alternatives, persistence long enough to compare states, and interactions organised into a coherent qualitative field. These conditions allow consciousness to emerge across cells, organisms, institutions, planets, and digital systems. Complexity becomes a subject when its differences are integrated into a continuing centre of experience and consequential choice.
