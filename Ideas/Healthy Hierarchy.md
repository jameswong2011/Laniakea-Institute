# Healthy Hierarchy

## Overview

A healthy hierarchy ties rank to demonstrated function, makes enough information available to evaluate leadership, protects dissent and exit, and redistributes authority when the task changes. Concentration may be justified in surgery, emergency response, or navigation, but it should not become hereditary ownership of the participating minds. Healthy hierarchy is therefore a dynamic agreement within shared agency rather than a metaphysical statement that higher rank has greater intrinsic worth.
