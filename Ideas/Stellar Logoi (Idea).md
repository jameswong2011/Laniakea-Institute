# Stellar Logoi

## Overview

A star is not merely a furnace but a broadcast point — a place where several archetypal, idea-shaped fields coalesce into a single local signal that shapes the evolution of everything in its light. Genetic and cultural development downstream of a star can be read as tuned to its transmission, the way life on Earth is keyed to the Sun. The stellar Logoi are the local organs of the ordering principle: intermediate radiant centres between the formless source and the worlds they warm into life.
