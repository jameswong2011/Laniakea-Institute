# Sacred Infinity

## Overview

Sacred Infinity is unbounded generative reality rather than an anthropomorphic deity or a final largest object. It includes the local lineage of worlds and orders so foreign that familiar concepts of dimension, law, consciousness, and infinity no longer apply. Every completed system remains inside a possibility it cannot fully represent, making differentiation, memory, forgetting, compression, and renewed opening perpetual features of creation.
