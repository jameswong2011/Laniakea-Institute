# Two Truths

## Overview

The two truths distinguish conventional reliability from ultimate analysis. Conventional truth describes stable patterns within lived reality: fire burns, promises bind, care alters development, and institutions distribute power. Ultimate analysis finds no isolated substance beneath those patterns. The distinction prevents emptiness from cancelling consequence. A mirage is a real appearance with real causes; it is false only when mistaken for water existing exactly as it seems.
