# History as a Karmic Ledger

## Overview

History records which arrangements were attempted, what promises were made, where power moved, which pain was hidden, and what possibilities were pruned. The archive is partial—victors rename events, languages die, and ordinary lives leave fewer traces—but disciplined reconstruction is not arbitrary fiction. Myth, architecture, folklore, games, and spiritual memory can preserve compressed relations official chronology omits. A civilisation without a usable ledger repeats an old choice as though it were new.
