# Perpetual Opening

## Overview

Perpetual opening is the rule that every completed answer can become the seed of another question. It follows from situated knowledge, genuine emergence, moral creativity, and the possibility of realities built from concepts foreign to the present grammar. An open system can still make commitments; it simply builds institutions, promises, technologies, and myths so that correction, exit, repair, and renewal remain possible. Closure without reopening turns compression into a prison.
