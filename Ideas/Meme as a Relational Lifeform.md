# Meme as a Relational Lifeform

## Overview

A meme is a pattern able to reproduce through minds, bodies, artefacts, and institutions. Its life is not identical to a written or digital record: a score becomes music when relation decompresses it into experience, and a moral rule becomes active when a community embodies it. Memes need substrate, attention, qualia, and sufficient fidelity for recurrence. This relational account avoids both reducing culture to inert files and treating ideas as disembodied substances.
