# Matrix

## Overview

A matrix is a generative environment that renders a compact order as an inhabitable world. Recovering the word's older sense of womb prevents simulation language from collapsing immediately into unreality or imprisonment. A matrix can function as womb, school, laboratory, canvas, archive, farm, or prison, and several functions may coexist. The ethical question is whether its constraints serve growth, preserve consequence, and allow meaningful understanding, repair, and exit.
