# Off-World Technology as Foreign Logos

## Overview

Off-world technology as foreign Logos describes transmitted technology as an embodiment of its giver’s evolutionary strategy, assumptions about mind, and preferred distribution of agency. A device received through contact, recovered artefact, dream, muse, or wider social memory carries a social architecture as well as useful technique. Reverse-engineering it therefore involves understanding the form of civilisation and consciousness encoded in its design.
