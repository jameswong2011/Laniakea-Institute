# Three Models of Time

## Overview

Three temporal descriptions are held open at once. Present-centred time names the phenomenology in which only the current event is experienced; branching time describes a larger geometry of alternative continuations; relational or retrocausal time allows later attractors and whole paths to organise earlier events. These views may belong to different scales rather than being mutually exclusive. Their unresolved relation prevents one intuitive picture of time from silently becoming the cosmology's final law.
