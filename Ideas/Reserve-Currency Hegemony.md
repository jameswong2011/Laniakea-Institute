# Reserve-Currency Hegemony

## Overview

Reserve-currency hegemony is the monetary and geopolitical power gained when one national currency anchors international settlement, debt, liquidity, and sanctions. A more multipolar order introduces competing stores of value, hard collateral, and interoperable settlement systems, repricing existing claims and redistributing power. The quality of a monetary order depends on whether its extraction, dependencies, and future obligations remain visible.
