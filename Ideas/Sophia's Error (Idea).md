# Sophia's Error

## Overview

Sophia’s error is creation beyond balanced capacity: too much high-dimensional information or too complex a qualia-question loaded into an engine unable to contain it. The failure lies in the mismatch between generative reach and substrate rather than malicious intent. It produces distorted outputs, a deficient administrator, and inhabitants who mistake a partial world for the whole, while showing how knowledge delivered faster than a recipient can integrate can become harmful.
