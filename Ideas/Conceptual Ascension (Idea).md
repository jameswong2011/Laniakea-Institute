# Conceptual Ascension

## Overview

Conceptual ascension is the ability to perceive the generative relation beneath several belief systems, dissolve reified forms, and construct a wider Logos from what remains. It alternates a source movement that builds explicit positions with a sink movement that follows claims until their fixation collapses. Accumulating exotic beliefs is not ascent. The test is whether a person can form, empty, reconstruct, and embody ideas without mistaking any current model for final reality.
