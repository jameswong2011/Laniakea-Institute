# Hard Problem of Consciousness

## Overview

The hard problem of consciousness asks why physical or functional processing is accompanied by subjective experience. Descriptions of discrimination, report, attention, and neural firing explain capacities and correlations but leave out what redness, pain, or grief feels like. This explanatory gap motivates a consciousness-first account in which universal experiential capacity becomes local through boundary, organisation, memory, and relation.
