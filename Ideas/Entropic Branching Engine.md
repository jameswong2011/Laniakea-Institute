# Entropic Branching Engine

## Overview

The entropic branching engine models choice as the production of differentiated continuations. From a shared prior state, alternatives A and B can develop into distinct paths, each accumulating a different ledger. The image resembles many-worlds structure but extends it from quantum outcomes into value, responsibility, and lived attention. Even if all branches exist, choice remains meaningful to the path that inherits a particular body of memory, relationship, capacity, and obligation.
