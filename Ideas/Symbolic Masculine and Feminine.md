# Symbolic Masculine and Feminine

## Overview

Masculine and feminine are used as symbolic functions rather than prescriptions for biological sexes. Chaos–yang–masculine names disruption, assertion, dominance, and beginning; order–yin–feminine names reception, nurture, continuity, and stabilisation. Either can be expressed by any person, and either becomes destructive without the other. A society that suppresses disruption cannot defend or renew itself; one that worships disruption cannot preserve what it creates.
