# Karma as Contextual Law

## Overview

Karma is the lawful inheritance of action across bodies, minds, relationships, institutions, and incarnations. An act changes the actor, affected beings, the shared world, and future decision-space; its valence belongs to the whole field rather than surface appearance alone. Karma operates like nested ecology or jurisdiction, allowing a local rule to be judged against the wider bodies and relations it affects.
