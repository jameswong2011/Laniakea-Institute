# Information as Record

## Overview

Information is a registered or interpretable distinction: a record produced when a living relation is measured, remembered, or read. It is indispensable to manifested order but does not contain the whole event that generated it. A map of pain is not pain, a genome is not the complete organism, and a language model is not every continuous condition of meaning. Consciousness is capacity, relation is grammar, qualia is actuality, and information is record.
