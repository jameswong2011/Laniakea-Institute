# Emptiness of Emptiness

## Overview

Emptiness is not a hidden substance behind appearances, and it must not be turned into one. To treat "emptiness" as an ultimate thing — a ground, an absolute, something you could grasp — is to misunderstand it completely; emptiness is itself empty. The blank canvas is not another painting. This second turn guards against swapping one fixed absolute for another, and keeps the teaching a medicine rather than a new disease.
