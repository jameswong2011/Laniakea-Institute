# Science and Scientism

## Overview

Science is a method of disciplined observation, prediction, reproducibility, and error correction. Scientism is the metaphysical position that only relations measurable through this method are real or meaningful. Scientific experiments provide powerful public records within their assay, while experience, value, purpose, and the assumptions behind measurement require additional forms of inquiry.
