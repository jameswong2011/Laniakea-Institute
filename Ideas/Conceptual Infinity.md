# Conceptual Infinity

## Overview

Conceptual infinity begins where dimension, law, body, matter, and universe are recognised as concepts generated by a local assay. A law of physics might be the biochemistry of a greater organism; a dimension might be one mode by which concepts can relate. Memetic or archetypal beings belong near this boundary because ideas may repeatedly acquire physical hosts and act through them. The category marks vocabulary failure, not a final highest compartment.
