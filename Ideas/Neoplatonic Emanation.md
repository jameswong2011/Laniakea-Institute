# Neoplatonic Emanation

## Overview

Neoplatonic emanation describes reality unfolding from the ineffable One through Nous, the intelligible field of Forms, into World Soul and material appearance. Each stage becomes more determinate without becoming wholly separate from its source, and return complements procession. The sequence links transcendence with manifested order through nested creation, compression, and the organising activity of Logos.
