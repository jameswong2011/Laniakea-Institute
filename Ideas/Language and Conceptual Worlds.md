# Language and Conceptual Worlds

## Overview

Language does more than label completed thoughts. Vocabulary stabilises distinctions; grammar connects them into causal, temporal, moral, and social relations; metaphor imports structure from one domain into another. The humanly inhabitable world is therefore partly built through language, although speech does not create the whole cosmos. Translation is a bridge between conceptual worlds rather than a one-for-one substitution of labels, and control of vocabulary can become a form of control over what a civilisation can easily imagine.
