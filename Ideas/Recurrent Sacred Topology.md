# Recurrent Sacred Topology

## Overview

Recurrent sacred topology is the shared structure appearing across many religious traditions: a reality wider than the ordinary world, differentiation or fall, obscuration or capture, a capacity not exhausted by embodiment, and a path of knowledge, purification, compassion, or remembrance. The pattern can emerge through cultural contact, common structures of mind, or access to shared layers of experience.
