# Time and Choice

## Overview

Time is the interval in which difference becomes consequence. Choice requires alternatives, an event of selection, and an after in which the result can be received. Sequential differentiation generates before and after, while parallel differentiation generates distinct worlds. Temporal difference is both a condition of local choice and an expression of minds creating divergent causal paths.
