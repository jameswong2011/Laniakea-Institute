# Pleroma and Fullness

## Overview

Pleroma means fullness: the living abundance of conscious decision-making substance available to a world. Fullness is the degree to which inhabitants are independent centres of experience rather than projections, fixed roles, or background functions. A low-fullness world can still contain real qualia, but it solves a narrower question with more predetermined parameters. The concept shifts simulation ethics away from the binary of real versus fake and toward how much genuine participation a world contains.
