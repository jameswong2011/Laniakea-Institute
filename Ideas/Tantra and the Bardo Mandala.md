# Tantra and the Bardo Mandala

## Overview

Tantra makes polarity vivid and reunites it through embodied practice: deity and practitioner, peace and wrath, masculine and feminine, purity and impurity, life and death. The bardo mandala maps dissolution and transition through gateways guarded by herukas whose forms encode the capacities needed to pass. Dense transmission can transform several layers at once, but partial fragments can destabilise. Taboo, terror, sexuality, and bliss require preparation, consent, and integration rather than being liberating by mere violation.
