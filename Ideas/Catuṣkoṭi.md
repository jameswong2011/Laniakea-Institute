# Catuṣkoṭi

## Overview

The four-cornered logic of classical Indian analysis: for any claim, examine four options — it is so, it is not so, it is both, it is neither — and show that none can be held as an independent, final truth. Applied to any candidate for intrinsic existence, all four corners collapse, revealing that nothing stands on its own side. The tetralemma is not confusion but a precision instrument for dismantling fixed positions without erecting a new one in their place.
