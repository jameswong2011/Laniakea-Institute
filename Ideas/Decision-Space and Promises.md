# Decision-Space and Promises

## Overview

Decision-space is the set of actions a being can actually perceive and perform. External constraints include law, body, geography, money, institutions, and other agents; internal constraints include fear, trauma, addiction, ignorance, and unstable attention. A promise transfers part of future freedom to another consciousness, creating support and obligation at once. Liberation therefore means understanding and enlarging workable choice, not pretending to be unconditioned or casually breaking every inherited bond.
