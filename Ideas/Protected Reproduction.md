# Protected Reproduction

## Overview

Protected reproduction means that no intelligence, platform, state, or caste controls the only route by which another lineage can continue. In a technologically mediated future this includes access to biological and engineered reproduction, genetic records, maintenance knowledge, legal parenthood, and viable habitats. The principle does not privilege one family form. It protects generational sovereignty by preventing reproductive assistance from becoming leverage through which an administrator fixes the bodies, values, or dependency of descendants.
