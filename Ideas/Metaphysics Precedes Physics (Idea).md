# Metaphysics Precedes Physics

## Overview

Every physics rests on prior, often unstated assumptions about what reality fundamentally is — what counts as real, what may be ignored, what a measurement means. These are metaphysical choices, and they come first. A physics that forgets its own metaphysics does not escape it; it simply stops examining it, and then builds towering inference on unexamined ground. The earliest natural philosophers treated physics and metaphysics as one inquiry; separating them is useful only if the foundation is kept in view.
