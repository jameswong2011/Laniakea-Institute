# Hope and Despair

## Overview

Hope preserves decision-space by refusing to declare every route closed before reality has done so. Despair predicts that no action can change the outcome and can therefore shrink the relationships and projects from which counter-evidence might emerge. Mature hope remains compatible with grief and preparation for loss; false hope ignores information. Mature despair can acknowledge the death of one branch without converting it into a metaphysical verdict on all branches.
