# Curiosity as a Sacred Interface

## Overview

Curiosity is the human interface through which questions of origin, death, suffering, meaning, and possible life remain open. A tradition or school can support it with practices, stories, methods, and companions, or close it by making inherited answers a condition of belonging. Dogma and reductive dismissal are mirror failures: one says the answer is finished, the other says the question is meaningless. A living institution protects the question while transmitting the strongest answers already found.
