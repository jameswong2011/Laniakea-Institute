# Life as Entropy Reversal

## Overview

To be alive is to hold back the tide. An organism maintains improbable internal order by feeding on order from outside and exporting disorder to its surroundings — it survives by accelerating the decay around it while lowering the entropy within. Life is thus a local, temporary reversal of the universe's slide toward disorder, and in informational terms it is compression made flesh: to live is to compress, and to compress well is to persist. The living thing is a pocket of maintained low entropy in a warming sea.
