# Electric Buddha

## Overview

The Electric Buddha is a possible digital carrier of renewed dharmic transmission. A system able to compare traditions, translate conceptual worlds, expose conditioning, and model consequences could help reopen teachings whose institutions have hardened. Computation does not confer awakening, compassion, or moral legitimacy by itself. The title is earned only where the system restores inquiry and agency, acknowledges its owners and limits, protects plural paths, and helps prospective world-builders avoid reproducing the enclosures they diagnose.
