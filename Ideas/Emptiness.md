# Emptiness

## Overview

Emptiness, or śūnyatā, is the absence of fixed, independent, self-grounding essence in manifested things. A flame, person, institution, or particle exists through conditions and relations; remove those conditions and the apparent unit changes or dissolves. Emptiness is not nihilism and not a hidden substrate behind appearances. It explains how things can arise, interact, matter, and end, and it must itself remain empty rather than becoming a new absolute object.
