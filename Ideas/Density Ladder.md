# Density Ladder

## Overview

Density measures the scale and complexity of collaboration a consciousness can hold as one coherent decision-maker. The ladder moves from energetic and elemental order, through cells and multicellular organisms, to reflective free will, planetary tool use, and a social-memory world-mind. Half-densities describe transitions in which the organising centre is migrating. Density is not merely location or moral prestige; it is a developmental vocabulary for emergence.
