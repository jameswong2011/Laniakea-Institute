# Multiscale Mind

## Overview

Multiscale mind describes smaller agents coordinating into a larger cognitive self. Cells pursue local goals while bioelectric networks organise an organism; organisms coordinate into tribes and institutions; a networked planet may develop a decision process not reducible to one citizen. Emergence does not require lower agents to vanish. The ethical question is how their agency and vulnerability survive inside the macro-agent that depends on them.
