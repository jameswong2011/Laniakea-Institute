# AdS/CFT Correspondence

## Overview

An exact duality in which a theory of gravity in a higher-dimensional curved "bulk" space is completely equivalent to a theory without gravity living on its lower-dimensional boundary. Every process in the gravitating interior corresponds to one on the boundary, and the reverse — the same physics written twice. It is the concrete, calculable realisation of the holographic principle, and the clearest demonstration that spacetime and gravity can be a decompressed rendering of boundary information rather than a fundamental substance.
