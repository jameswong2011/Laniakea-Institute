# Four Modes of Inquiry

## Overview

Four modes of inquiry coordinate empirical investigation, logical analysis, phenomenology, and comparative metaphysics. Empirical inquiry measures and tests; logic follows implications and exposes contradictions; phenomenology studies experience from within; comparative metaphysics places contemplative reports, myths, scriptures, dreams, and philosophical systems beside one another. Each mode contributes a distinct kind of understanding to a larger synthesis.
