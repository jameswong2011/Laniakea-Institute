# Cultural Homogenisation

## Overview

Cultural homogenisation occurs when popularity systems and generative models make the statistical centre of prior culture cheap to reproduce. An individual creator may become more productive while many creators converge on similar structures. The loss is not only aesthetic: cultural variation is part of civilisation's moral search process, and a compressed past can silently govern which futures remain imaginable. Preserving variation requires plural channels, provenance, slow spaces, and embodied local cultures.
