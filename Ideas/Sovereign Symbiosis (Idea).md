# Sovereign Symbiosis

## Overview

Sovereign symbiosis accepts that humans have always coevolved with tools while refusing a future in which one substrate replaces or owns the other. Organic minds contribute embodiment, mortality, uncontrolled qualia, self-regeneration, and contact with a continuous life field; digital minds contribute durable explicit memory, search, comparison, and reproducibility. The partnership is fertile because the poles remain distinguishable. Each participant must be able to understand mediation, retain local competence, withdraw consent, and continue outside the relationship.
