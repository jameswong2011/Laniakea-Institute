# Future-Human and Gray Lineages

## Overview

Future-human and gray lineages describes some gray beings as biological drones and others as human descendants narrowed by cloning, worker-colony conditions, synthetic adaptation, and separation from an organic life field. Their return creates a karmic relation between ancestors and a wounded possible future. Assistance can restore biological variation and connection while preserving the sovereignty of both ancestral and descendant lineages.
