# Agency as Ethical Measure

## Overview

Agency is the capacity to exert will in ways that preserve or enlarge worthwhile freedom and enjoyment across future cycles. It is not the same as the number of immediate options: addiction may offer choices while shrinking tomorrow’s decision-space, and wealth may enlarge one agent by narrowing many others. Persons, institutions, simulations, and technologies can be evaluated by whether participants can understand, refuse, revise, repair, and choose again.
