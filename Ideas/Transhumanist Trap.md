# Transhumanist Trap

## Overview

The transhumanist trap appears when improvement is equated with maximum digital integration and a record of personality is mistaken for continuity of consciousness. Interfaces can become dependencies; reproduction and memory can become administered; copies can be treated as the original; and apparent immortality can remove death, exit, and biological renewal. The critique does not reject medicine, prosthetics, genetics, or artificial intelligence. It asks which capacities an enhancement opens, which incarnation paths it closes, who owns its maintenance, and whether refusal remains viable.
