# Memetic Birthing

## Overview

Memetic birthing decomposes an idea into elementary values, causal claims, emotional rewards, and identity functions, then recombines them into stable alternatives. Three procedures supply variation: extrapolation to a conceptual terminus, reverse search through historical and evolutionary experiments, and intuitive or contemplative seed input. Novelty must then be tested for positive qualia, genuine difference, and the minimum order needed for continued collaboration.
