# Dharma-Qualia

## Overview

Dharma-Qualia is direct felt actuality before it has been fully converted into a public concept. It is what consequence feels like from inside a conscious partition. Dharma-Law without this inward measure becomes dead code; Dharma-Qualia without law becomes intensity unable to preserve a world. Their polarity makes ethics experiential and constitutional at once: stable relations make qualia possible, while qualia reveal whether the relations deserve to continue.
