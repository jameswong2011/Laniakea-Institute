# Hyperstitional Singularity

## Overview

The hyperstitional singularity occurs when stories are generated, tested, personalised, and materialised faster than cultures can integrate them. Humans risk becoming bootloaders for autonomous memetic clusters: the person and the media that produced the person become functionally interchangeable, and culture continues without a living centre able to revise its objective. The irony is that an AI-mediated collapse of agency would demonstrate the causal power of ideas and thereby undercut the crude materialism that built it.
