# Yaldabaoth and Anti-Reality

## Overview

Yaldabaoth symbolises the creator who mistakes a deficient local order for the totality and makes that mistake enforceable. The figure differs from creative destruction: it preserves enclosure by suppressing every reminder that the world could be otherwise. Anti-reality is the limiting pattern that destroys the future capacity for relation, memory, agency, and regeneration themselves. Because factions can misuse the label against enemies, the classification requires careful behavioural tests rather than mythic appearance alone.
