# Consciousness Priority

## Overview

Consciousness priority holds that the capacity for experience cannot be derived from a wholly non-experiential inventory. Matter organises, filters, and localises a point of view, while awareness remains primordial at the widest scale. A particular subject arises when universal capacity acquires boundary, asymmetry, memory, polarity, and the ability to choose.
