# Craftsmanship Economy

## Overview

A craftsmanship economy gives people a meaningful role in making, repairing, and altering their material environment even under automation. Status shifts from ownership alone toward the skill, care, originality, and proportion of possessions personally created or restored. Distributed tools, repairable products, workshops, and open standards make innovation less remote and preserve competence beneath complex infrastructure. Craft is not nostalgia; it is sovereignty embodied in matter.
