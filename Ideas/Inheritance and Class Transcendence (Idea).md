# Inheritance and Class Transcendence

## Overview

Inheritance carries family memory, security, capital, and unfinished projects across generations. Class transcendence occurs when one life becomes the bridge by which a lineage enters a wider field of agency. Unlimited concentration can turn historical luck into permanent command, while banning private inheritance may leave only large institutions able to accumulate. The design question is how many independent lineages can persist and whether every child retains access to the commons needed to begin one.
