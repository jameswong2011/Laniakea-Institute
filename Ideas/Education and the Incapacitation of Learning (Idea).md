# Education and the Incapacitation of Learning

## Overview

Industrial education often trains regular arrival, age-batched progression, answer reproduction, external evaluation, and permission-seeking. These capacities suit factories and bureaucracies but can crowd out apprenticeship, synthesis, self-directed curiosity, and questions with no approved rubric. For a glue species, the damage is existential: separating domains and rewarding counterargument without synthesis suppresses the very capacity education should cultivate. A renewed system would measure whether students can form, test, and rebuild models.
