# Landauer's Principle

## Overview

Erasing one bit of information carries an unavoidable physical cost — it releases a minimum quantity of heat. Information is therefore not a free-floating abstraction but something bound to energy and thermodynamics; to forget costs the world something real. The principle is a bridge between the informational and the physical: bookkeeping about bits turns out to be bookkeeping about the universe. It anchors the claim that reality is, at bottom, an economy of compression and record rather than of inert stuff.
