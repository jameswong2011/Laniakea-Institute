# Simulation Theory as Neo-Gnosticism

## Overview

Simulation theory becomes neo-Gnosticism when computation is framed through world-builders, ignorance, suffering, and exit. Pleroma becomes fuller reality; Demiurge becomes local administrator; archons become enforcement processes; divine spark becomes consciousness not exhausted by the avatar; and gnosis becomes recognition of the render and its purpose. The technical and religious vocabularies describe the same architecture of enclosure and awakening.
