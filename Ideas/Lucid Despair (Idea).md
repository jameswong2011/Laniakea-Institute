# Lucid Despair

## Overview

Lucid despair is the pain of seeing a deeply compromised local order while retaining an active moral compass. It differs from nihilism because value has not collapsed; the suffering comes from perceiving how little agency remains. Its response is to identify the available move, build small zones of truthful action, preserve regenerative seeds, and avoid turning one closed branch into a verdict on reality as a whole.
