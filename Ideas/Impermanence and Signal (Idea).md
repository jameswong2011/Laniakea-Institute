# Impermanence and Signal

## Overview

Impermanence applies broadly to manifested forms, but patterns differ in durability. A mood may last minutes, a trauma decades, a mathematical relation across civilisations, and a Logos across cycles. Practice is the art of deciding what deserves deep storage and what is temporary noise. “Everything passes” should neither justify grasping every form nor abandoning every truth, skill, promise, and loving relation worth carrying forward.
