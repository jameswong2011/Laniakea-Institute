# Pluralistic Alignment

## Overview

Pluralistic alignment aims to preserve many coherent moral communities above a shared dharmic floor. Existing preferences are inputs, not the final answer, because people may be conditioned, uninformed, or internally inconsistent and cultures continue to evolve. Alignment should help participants understand frameworks, trace consequences, negotiate conflicts, and revise commitments. The system must avoid averaging every value into a centre that no one inhabits or granting one faction permanent authority over the moral future.
