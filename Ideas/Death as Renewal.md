# Death as Renewal

## Overview

Death renews creation by pruning a local library, redistributing matter and roles, ending stale contracts, and returning consciousness to conditions it did not fully design. Life extension adds time while the stream remains open to transformation; closed immortality freezes memory, dependency, and objective. Forgetting, migration, dormancy, closure, and rebirth preserve genuine renewal when biological death changes form.
