# Dharma-Law

## Overview

Dharma-Law is the evolving code through which divided consciousness collaborates. It includes physical regularities, karmic relations, ethical precepts, vows, social duties, and the rules of contemplative paths at different scales. Mature Dharma-Law protects the conditions of relation and revision, and its quality is revealed through the experiences and forms of agency it produces.
