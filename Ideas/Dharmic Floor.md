# Dharmic Floor

## Overview

A dharmic floor is the minimum constitutional order that allows substantial moral difference without turning experimentation into captivity. It includes informed entry, practical exit, protection of dependants, legible governance, non-predation, exchange across communities, ecological duties, and preservation of future agency. The floor is not morally neutral and must remain revisable. Its purpose is a common grammar for plural Logoi, not a ceiling that determines every good life.
