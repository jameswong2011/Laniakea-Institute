# Masks, Ego and Consensus

## Overview

Social masks exchange occupation, rank, safe interests, and acceptable opinions so participants can estimate how to proceed. This is useful, but a culture may remain at a level where masks converse and transformative centres never meet. An inflated species-ego performs a similar defence by assuming humanity is the summit of sentience, making more powerful minds seem humiliating or absurd. Consensus then becomes easy to engineer through status, fear of judgement, and mapped identity clusters.
