# Peaceful and Wrathful Deities

## Overview

Peaceful and wrathful deities are polar faces of transformative function. Peace attracts, reassures, and integrates; wrath exposes, cuts, judges, or removes a pattern that uses consolation to avoid change. Multiple heads, arms, flames, and weapons symbolise simultaneous attention and precise instruments rather than uncontrolled rage. Wrath becomes sacred only where it remains lucid, proportionate, and oriented toward repair; otherwise violence has merely borrowed religious costume.
