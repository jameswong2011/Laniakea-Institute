# Money as Compressed Trust

## Overview

Money is a portable record of social trust and claim. Credit brings expected future production into present action; capital stores a history of deferred consumption, risk, and coordination. These protocols can enable cooperation across strangers and time, but their apparent agency always belongs to people, institutions, classes, or egregores acting through them. Treating capital as a sentient universal villain hides the distributed decisions that make extraction possible.
