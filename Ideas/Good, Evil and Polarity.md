# Good, Evil and Polarity

## Overview

Good and evil gain meaning within value-bearing ecologies rather than possessing isolated essences. Many moral conflicts are encounters between systems that protect different goods, boundaries, histories, and scales. Understanding an opposed dharma is therefore necessary before judgement. Context does not make every action equal: patterns that permanently destroy agency, truthful relation, repair, and future moral inquiry approach anti-reality and supply a limiting case beyond ordinary dialectical opposition.
