# Hermetic Correspondence

## Overview

Hermetic correspondence, expressed as “as above, so below,” describes macrocosm and microcosm as reflections of related generative patterns. Part and whole can repeat an organising relation without being identical at every scale. Together with Hermetic mentalism, correspondence connects consciousness-first metaphysics, fractal emergence, and the mutual interpretation of inner and outer worlds.
