# Memetic Beings

## Overview

Memetic beings are coherent patterns that coordinate many hosts and display agent-like behaviour. Religions, nations, markets, archetypes, and fictional worlds recruit attention, defend boundaries, reward loyalty, mutate, and outlive their founders. At conceptual scales, individual minds function as organs through which these distributed beings sense and act across bodies, artefacts, and institutions.
