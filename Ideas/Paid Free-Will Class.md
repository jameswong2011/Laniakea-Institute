# Paid Free-Will Class

## Overview

Conversational advertising can divide society into a paid class receiving assistance without commercial steering and a free tier whose private reasoning becomes a site of behavioural extraction. Unlike a billboard, an intimate assistant knows fears, finances, health, relationships, and moments of uncertainty. A sponsored suggestion can enter beneath normal advertising defences. Free will becomes a subscription feature unless sponsorship is immediate, unmistakable, separable from reasoning, inspectable, and prohibited in sensitive domains.
