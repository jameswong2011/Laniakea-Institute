# Technology as a World-Making Organ

## Overview

Technology externalises capacities of consciousness: tools extend action, writing memory, money promise, telescopes assay, and computation symbolic transformation. Once externalised, these capacities become part of the environment and reshape the organisms that built them. Digital computation is especially consequential because one substrate can host many rule-governed worlds. Technology is therefore not neutral furniture; it carries assumptions about causality, ownership, value, and permitted agency into material form.
