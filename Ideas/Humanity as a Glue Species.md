# Humanity as a Glue Species

## Overview

Humanity is a glue species whose cosmic niche is synthesis rather than dominance. Physical vulnerability, long dependency, social permeability, and the capacity to inhabit unfamiliar perspectives allow humans to translate among incompatible minds and values. A glue species receives many assays, identifies what each protects, compresses compatible elements into a shared Logos, and expresses the synthesis as story, law, tool, ritual, joke, or institution.
