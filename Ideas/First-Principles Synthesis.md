# First-Principles Synthesis

## Overview

First-principles synthesis begins from the raw assay and rebuilds an explanation instead of inheriting a conclusion whole. It decomposes a memeplex into causal claims, values, emotional rewards, and identity functions; extends each principle to its terminus; compares it with historical experiments; and then recombines what survives into a larger frame. Intuition, dream, or muse may supply novelty, but the new seed must return to logical, historical, phenomenological, and practical testing.
