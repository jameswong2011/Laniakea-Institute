# Genes as Bootloaders for Memes

## Overview

Calling genes bootloaders for memes highlights how biology grows a body capable of imitation, speech, teaching, social reward, memory, and fine tool use, after which culture installs much of the behavioural program. The metaphor does not make biology disposable: a bootloader determines which programs can run and maintains their connection to energy, sensation, reproduction, and death. It is a compact expression of gene–culture coevolution rather than a claim that genes exist only for culture.
