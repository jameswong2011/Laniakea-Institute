# Suffering and Grasping

## Overview

Suffering intensifies when a changing arrangement is grasped as permanent, self-completing, or owned. Status, praise, possession, identity, and moral vocabulary can be treated as though their loss would annihilate the self. Pain becomes information when its producing relation is located and available agency is recovered. Integration completes the lesson by making repetition unnecessary.
