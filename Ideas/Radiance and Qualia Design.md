# Radiance and Qualia Design

## Overview

Radiance is a compression pattern in which a coherent centre emits a form that many receivers can interpret without controlling each response. A symbol, ritual, interface, artwork, or world can carry a generative structure into many different minds. Qualia design is the deliberate shaping of what relations can feel like within that field. Authority over symbolic or technical design therefore becomes authority over what a civilisation can notice, desire, suffer, and eventually enact.
