# Sovereign Attention

## Overview

Sovereign attention is the recovery of enough space between stimulus and action for a person to choose identification, value, and direction. Thoughts arrive already shaped by genes, karma, family, language, culture, and media. Practice does not stop thought; it notices the sequence by which an incoming image receives emotional valence, retrieves associations, becomes worldview, and guides action. Attention makes conditioning visible enough to metabolise rather than merely obey.
