# Toxic Positivity

## Overview

Toxic positivity makes grief, anger, danger, or injustice into failures of spiritual attitude so that a preferred worldview remains untroubled. It becomes solipsistic when another person's suffering is blamed on their vibration while the conditions producing harm are ignored. Accurate pain is information. A constructive positive orientation enlarges action after receiving that information; gaslighting demands pleasant interpretation in exchange for belonging and turns spirituality into another farm.
