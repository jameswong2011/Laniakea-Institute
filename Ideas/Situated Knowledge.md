# Situated Knowledge

## Overview

Situated knowledge is the principle that knowing always occurs from within biological, linguistic, social, dimensional, and historical conditions. It rejects both omniscient certainty and the claim that all perspectives are equally adequate. A perspective can be rigorous within its range while remaining partial at its border. The practical demand is to state the standpoint, conversion chain, and evidential grammar behind a claim so that other knowers can compare, extend, or correct it.
