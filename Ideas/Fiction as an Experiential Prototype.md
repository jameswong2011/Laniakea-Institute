# Fiction as an Experiential Prototype

## Overview

Fiction lets an audience rehearse a world rather than merely hear a proposition about it. Characters become temporary moral interfaces, imagined institutions expose their felt consequences, and invented technologies teach desire before engineering makes them available. This gives fiction both revelatory and dangerous power: it can carry structures a culture would reject as doctrine, warn against a future, or supply the reference design that helps build it.
