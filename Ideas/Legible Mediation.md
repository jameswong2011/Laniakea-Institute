# Legible Mediation

## Overview

Legible mediation means that a recommendation, model-generated perception, bodily alteration, or sensory substitution can be traced to the process, data, and objective that produced it. Legibility does not require every person to inspect every circuit. It requires accessible explanations, independent experts, audit rights, meaningful provenance, and the option to compare or replace mediators. Without this, an interface can edit the world before awareness while presenting its own intervention as reality itself.
