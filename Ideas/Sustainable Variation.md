# Sustainable Variation

## Overview

Sustainable variation is the goal of preserving genuine novelty while raising net positive qualia rather than externalising suffering. It evaluates a system through four measures: distribution of pain and pleasure, continued availability of different ways of life, participant agency, and regeneration of the conditions for future creativity. Variation without care licenses experimentation on vulnerable beings; pleasure without variation converges on a closed loop. The concept is a bridge between cosmology, ethics, culture, and governance.
