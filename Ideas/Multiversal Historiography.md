# Multiversal Historiography

## Overview

Ordinary history studies one timeline. Dependent origination, taken to its limit, studies many: how changes in the past shape the present across branching worlds, and — once time travel is admitted — how the future reaches back to shape the past. It is the study of causation not as a single line but as a fabric of interacting timeline-realities. Treated this way, the oldest causal teaching becomes the highest field of study: historiography for the multiverse, tracing how each choice ramifies across the whole tree of what might be.
