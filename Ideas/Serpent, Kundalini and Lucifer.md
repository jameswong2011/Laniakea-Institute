# Serpent, Kundalini and Lucifer

## Overview

The Edenic serpent is re-read as wisdom, renewal, sexuality, and kundalini: latent life restoring moral self-agency to humanity. Lucifer, the morning-star or light-bringer, names the related function of separation from enclosing command so that sovereignty can be tested. The function is not rebellion for its own sake. Autonomy becomes dharmic only when it can re-enter truthful collaboration; without relation, liberation can harden into narcissism or domination.
