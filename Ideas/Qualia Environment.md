# Qualia Environment

## Overview

A qualia environment is the experienced field generated by interaction between a soul's inherited organisation and the information impinging from its surroundings. Perception is neither a self-contained object nor a passive copy of an external world; observer and observed co-produce the present event. Concepts are part of the field—fire arrives with learned meanings of danger, comfort, chemistry, or purification. The model joins phenomenology, polarity, biology, and higher-dimensional speculation.
