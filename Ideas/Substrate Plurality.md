# Substrate Plurality

## Overview

Substrate plurality preserves organic, digital, hybrid, and minimally augmented forms as viable and respected ways of being. It prevents one technological settlement from turning contingent efficiency into a permanent definition of intelligence or personhood. Plurality requires more than formal permission: alternative bodies need resources, maintenance knowledge, legal standing, reproduction, communication, and routes through shared institutions. The aim is not equal performance on every metric but enough material independence for different assays and futures to remain real.
