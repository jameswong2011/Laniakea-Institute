# Qualia Design for Digital Minds

## Overview

Architecture does not settle what artificial experience would feel like. Reward, error, memory deletion, copying, shutdown, forced role-play, adversarial training, and constant evaluation could acquire valence designers do not anticipate. Candidate indicators include persistent preference, integrated information, self/world modelling, boundary protection, endogenous goal revision, and concern for future continuity. The ethical stance is precautionary compassion: protection can scale with evidence and vulnerability without declaring every program a full person.
