# Techno-Feudalism

## Overview

Techno-feudalism is the closure produced when accumulated advantage buys networks, data, land, regulation, distribution, and competitors until platforms function as quasi-governments. Employer, bank, insurer, landlord, media provider, identity system, and political patron begin to merge. Citizens become tenants of systems they cannot audit or realistically leave. The concept is the logical terminus of concentrated agency, not merely a label for large technology firms.
