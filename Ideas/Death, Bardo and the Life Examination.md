# Death, Bardo and the Life Examination

## Overview

Death removes the dense interface and allows a life to be read as a whole relational pattern rather than a series of emergencies. The bardo is the interval in which ordinary compressions dissolve and habits appear as worlds. The life examination asks what the incarnation actually understood about consciousness, existence, and consequence; deficiencies become later catalyst, while integrated lessons compress into capacity. This is a metaphysical model of post-mortem continuity and also a practical invitation to review a life before death.
