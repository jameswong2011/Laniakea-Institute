# Nested Moral Jurisdictions

## Overview

Nested moral jurisdictions recognise that the same action participates in several collaborative bodies. A ruler may be rewarded by a predatory institution while indebted to the people harmed; a rebel may violate local law while repairing a wider dharmakaya. Moral inquiry asks which body is being protected, where costs are placed, whether consent and promises are valid, and whether future repair remains possible. No local code receives automatic final authority.
