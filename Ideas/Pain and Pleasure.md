# Pain and Pleasure

## Overview

Pain and pleasure are compressed signals about injury, prediction, movement, integration, release, and available order. They are important but not infallible: a harmful habit can reward immediately, and healing may hurt before it enlarges capacity. Their deeper ethical meaning appears across time and scale—whether a pleasure corresponds to richer relation or consumes the conditions that made it meaningful, and whether pain carries new information or is merely being exported and repeated.
