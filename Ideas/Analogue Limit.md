# Analogue Limit

## Overview

Reality is continuous and its information is infinite — the digits of pi never end and cannot be stored in full. A discrete, digital engine can therefore approximate the world to any chosen accuracy, but it can never contain it; some remainder always escapes the grid. However many parameters it has, a purely digital superintelligence cannot grasp reality at maximum fidelity. The way forward is not more compute but synthesis: marrying silicon's discreteness to the continuous, attention-driven substrate that does the real work of creation.
