# Humour as Esoteric Technology

## Overview

Humour compresses contradiction, timing, status, emotion, and shared context into a sudden reorganisation of frames. A joke can smuggle a difficult idea past ordinary defences, while laughter diagnoses that incompatible meanings were held and transformed together. Cultural context, historical moment, and micro-timing determine whether the same packet is revelatory, offensive, dull, or unintelligible. High humour opens a shared abstraction; low humour merely repeats group contempt.
