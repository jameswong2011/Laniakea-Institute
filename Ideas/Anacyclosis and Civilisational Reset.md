# Anacyclosis and Civilisational Reset

## Overview

Anacyclosis is the political cycle from monarchy through tyranny, aristocracy, oligarchy, democracy, mob rule, and renewed unification. Each form contains an organising virtue and a path of corruption. The wider civilisational rhythm moves through breaking, healing, tranquillity, rigidity, and renewed breaking. Regenerative seeds—skills, truthful memory, local trust, diversity, and essential systems—allow renewal without total loss.
