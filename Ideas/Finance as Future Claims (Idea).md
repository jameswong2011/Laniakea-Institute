# Finance as Future Claims

## Overview

Finance coordinates promises, risk, and resources across time. Its farming inversion begins when the encoding becomes too opaque for those making the promise to understand who holds it, how its value changes, or which rules determine settlement. Complexity becomes a moat and information asymmetry becomes rent. A dharmic financial system makes extraction, inflation exposure, leverage, and public guarantees legible enough for participants to evaluate the future agency they are transferring.
