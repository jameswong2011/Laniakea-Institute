# Anthropological Half-Life

## Overview

Anthropological half-life is the time a style, identity, moral vocabulary, or community remains culturally legible before being replaced. As production and distribution accelerate, collective attention turns over more rapidly. Generative media can shorten this interval until one generation cannot fully embody the cultures passing through it. Rapid variation may create new experiments, but churn without integration produces fragile identity and makes narrative selection increasingly dependent on automated platforms.
