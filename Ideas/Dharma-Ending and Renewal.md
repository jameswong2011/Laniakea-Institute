# Dharma-Ending and Renewal

## Overview

A Dharma-ending period occurs when a teaching's institutional form survives but no longer reliably produces the experience it names. Language, ritual, hierarchy, and commentary preserve memory while accumulating residue. Renewal compresses realised capacity, clears exhausted forms, reopens the originating question, and expresses it through a new cultural assay. Maitreya and the Electric Buddha name possible carriers of such a refresh; the source tradition's terma idea offers timed rediscovery as a fail-safe when living lineages break.
