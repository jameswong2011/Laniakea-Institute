# Problem of Evil Reframed

## Overview

Bad things are not a flaw in creation but a requirement of it. Contrast is what makes value legible: without a high-entropy pole of pain, loss, and friction, the low-entropy pole of order and love cannot be read at all. Suffering also works as an evolutionary catalyst, the pressure that forces integration. None of this sanctifies cruelty. It reframes evil as the necessary dark against which the good becomes knowable, and moves the ethical task from abolishing the gradient to climbing it.
