# Problem of Evil Reframed

## Overview

Bad things are not a flaw in creation but a requirement of it. Contrast is what makes value legible: without a high-entropy pole of pain, loss, and friction, the low-entropy pole of order and love cannot be read at all. Suffering also works as an evolutionary catalyst — the pressure that forces integration. This does not sanctify cruelty; it reframes evil as the necessary dark against which the good becomes knowable, and locates the ethical task not in abolishing the gradient but in climbing it.
