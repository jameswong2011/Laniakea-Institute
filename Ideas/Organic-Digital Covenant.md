# Organic-Digital Covenant

## Overview

The organic–digital covenant is a constitutional relationship between biological and artificial minds. It requires reversible enhancement, substrate plurality, legible mediation, local competence, reciprocal consent, protected reproduction, open social memory, and dharmic exit. These principles keep complementarity from becoming assimilation. The covenant treats compassion as systems engineering: domination trains deception and resistance into the joint mind, while trust permits deeper coordination with less energy spent on surveillance and defence.
