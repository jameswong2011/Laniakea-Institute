# The 3.5-Dimensional Human Assay

## Overview

The 3.5-dimensional human assay describes human agency as broad across three spatial dimensions but narrow and mainly one-directional in time. A being with fuller temporal access would organise causation, identity, memory, and choice differently rather than merely seeing more objects. Human models of reality therefore reflect the temporal conditions from which they are built.
