# Technology as Pharmakon

## Overview

A pharmakon is poison and cure in the same instrument. Computation can widen memory, translation, access, creativity, and planetary coordination; the same infrastructure can centralise surveillance, manipulate desire, freeze values, and host enclosures. Hardware does not decide between these futures by itself. Ownership, objectives, qualia design, provenance, reversibility, and the distribution of agency determine which potential becomes the governing Dharma-Law.
