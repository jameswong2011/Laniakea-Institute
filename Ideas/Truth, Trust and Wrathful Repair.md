# Truth, Trust and Wrathful Repair

## Overview

Truth is the reliability that permits many agents to coordinate without spending all their energy on defence. When deception or predation spreads, peaceful relation may require exposure, boundary, judgement, removal, or precise force. Wrathful repair differs from revenge through three tests: it targets the harmful relation rather than an undifferentiated class, removes no more agency than repair requires, and keeps an honest route to reintegration where reintegration is possible.
