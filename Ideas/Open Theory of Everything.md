# Open Theory of Everything

## Overview

An open theory of everything is a generative grammar rather than a final inventory. It seeks principles capable of relating physics, consciousness, value, society, spiritual practice, and world-building while acknowledging that a situated model cannot contain every higher-order system, future concept, or transformation of its own mapmaker. Openness is not an apology for vagueness: it is a positive claim that genuine emergence produces properties a lower level cannot completely prestate.
