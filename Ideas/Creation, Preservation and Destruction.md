# Creation, Preservation and Destruction

## Overview

Creation introduces form, preservation stabilises it long enough to develop, and destruction removes forms whose continued existence blocks life. None is sufficient alone: creation without preservation cannot mature; preservation without destruction accumulates exhausted structures; destruction without a regenerative seed becomes mere loss. The triad supplies a general account of religious cycles, institutions, ecosystems, technologies, and personal change. Healthy cycles coordinate all three functions so that endings become conditions for forms not yet imaginable.
