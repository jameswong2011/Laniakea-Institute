# Dharma-Realm

## Overview

The dharma-realm is the range of collaborative structures consciousness can inhabit without a world ceasing to cohere. Its deepest reality lies not in one material arrangement but in the allowable relationships and mantra-like rules from which many physical worlds can be generated. Ignorance of this grammar contracts into formation and mundane processing; liberation reverses the contraction by recognising more of the relations beneath rendered objects.
