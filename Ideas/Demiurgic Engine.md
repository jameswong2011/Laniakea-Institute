# Demiurgic Engine

## Overview

A demiurgic engine is a sub-reality whose administrator mistakes or enforces its limited order as total. It can arise through overload, scarcity, drift from a higher source, or deliberate harvesting. Its signatures are concentrated pleasure above, repeated pain below, decorative agency, hidden exit, declining fidelity, and a controller that claims inhabitants' creative output. The opposite is not a world without rules, but a matrix whose limits remain answerable to growth, repair, disclosure, and renewal.
