# Long Time Horizons

## Overview

A civilisation that expects replacement, harvest, or near-term collapse tends to maximise present extraction. Waste, planned obsolescence, fragile supply chains, and missing maintenance reveal a society with little confidence in its descendants. Long-horizon design preserves ecological, biological, technical, memetic, and spiritual variation for future beings who may reject present preferences. Sovereignty means leaving them enough functioning options to choose rather than merely inheriting our answer.
