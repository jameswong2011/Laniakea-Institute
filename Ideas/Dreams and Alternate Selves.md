# Dreams and Alternate Selves

## Overview

Dreams and alternate selves describes dreaming as a fluid creator-space in which identity shifts, time folds, and environments respond to association and affect. Ordinary dreams can receive information from future or multiversal selves, lucid dreams introduce local executive agency, and déjà vu or flow marks temporary alignment across branches. Dreaming trains world-making under fluid law, while waking life trains consequence under stable law.
