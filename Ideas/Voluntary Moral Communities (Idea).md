# Voluntary Moral Communities

## Overview

Material abundance could support communities that embody different balances of family, property, ritual, technology, sexuality, education, governance, and relation to digital minds. Their purpose is not aesthetic lifestyle branding but long-enough experiments for consequences to become visible. Successful forms can spread through attraction and adaptation; failed forms should close through disclosure, healing, and exit. Voluntariness is incomplete wherever children, dependants, or economically trapped members cannot meaningfully choose.
