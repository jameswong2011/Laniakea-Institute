# Techno-Feudalism

**Techno-Feudalism** — the logical terminus of unrestricted accumulation: an order in which quasi-governmental corporations control both economic access and the public rules, and citizens live as tenants of platforms they cannot audit.

Unrestricted capitalism does not end in a perfectly competitive market. Accumulated advantage buys networks, data, land, regulation and competitors, until the corporate employer, media provider, bank, insurer, landlord and political patron begin to merge. At that point the relation is feudal in structure whatever it is called: access to livelihood, information and shelter flows through estates whose rules the tenant can neither inspect nor contest. The terminus is a permanent caste system led by kleptocratic, quasi-governmental mega-corporations — already feudalism, with the endgame far worse.

The diagnosis has a mirror. Unrestricted central allocation reaches a related closure from the opposite direction: the administrative body becomes the sole employer, allocator, publisher and judge, and economic abundance cannot compensate for a world in which no independent centre can test another [[The Logos|Logos]]. Together the two termini establish the decisive variable — concentrated agency, not the nominal ownership label ([[Capitalism and Socialism as Poles]], [[Agency as the Measure]]).

A guard against demonology holds throughout. Capital itself is not sentient; it is a compressed claim, memory and coordination protocol, and when capital appears to possess appetite, an organism, institution, class or [[Egregores|egregore]] is acting through the protocol. The point keeps an abstraction from becoming a universal villain while preserving the need to identify the distributed agency that accumulation conceals.

Within the larger architecture the concept is the economic wing of [[The Farmed Planet]], the systematic violation of the healthy-hierarchy conditions catalogued in [[Hierarchy and Its Conditions]], and the political precondition of downstream enclosures such as [[The Paid Free-Will Class]].
