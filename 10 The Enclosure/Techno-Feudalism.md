# Techno-Feudalism

**Techno-Feudalism** — the logical terminus of unrestricted accumulation: an order in which quasi-governmental corporations control both economic access and the public rules, and citizens live as tenants of platforms they cannot audit.

Left unchecked, wealth stops competing and starts ruling. Unrestricted capitalism does not settle into a fair, open market. Accumulated advantage buys networks, data, land, regulation and rivals, until one power is at once your employer, your media, your bank, your insurer, your landlord and your political patron. When those roles fuse, the relationship becomes feudal whatever its official name. This is techno-feudalism: an order where access to work, information and shelter runs through private estates whose rules you can neither read nor challenge — you are a tenant, not a citizen. The endpoint is a permanent caste system led by mega-corporations that are kleptocratic (looting those they rule) and quasi-governmental (wielding the powers of a state). On this reading it is already feudalism today, with the endgame far worse.

The diagnosis has a mirror image. Push central planning to its own extreme and you reach a matching trap from the other side: the state becomes the only employer, allocator, publisher and judge. No amount of material plenty can make up for a world where no independent centre survives to test another [[The Logos|Logos]], another ordering of value. So the two extremes teach one lesson: the dangerous variable is concentrated agency, how few hands hold real choice, whatever label the door wears, "capitalist" or "socialist". Each names a necessary value that turns catastrophic when it swallows everything ([[Capitalism and Socialism as Poles]], [[Agency as the Measure]]).

One guard runs throughout: do not make a demon of capital itself. Capital is a compressed claim on the future, a store of memory and a protocol for coordinating strangers, and it is not alive. When it seems to have an appetite of its own, look closer — some organism, institution, class or [[Egregores|egregore]] is acting through the protocol. Naming that hidden agent keeps an abstraction from becoming a cartoon villain, while still refusing to let accumulation hide who is actually choosing.

This is old ground made new. Marx charted how capital concentrates until ownership and rule converge, and the economist Yanis Varoufakis gave the resulting order its name, techno-feudalism. The diagnosis here extends the warning in both directions, since unchecked central planning reaches the same enclosure from the opposite side. Within the wider architecture it is the economic wing of [[The Farmed Planet]], the systematic breaking of the conditions that keep rank healthy ([[Hierarchy and Its Conditions]]), and the political groundwork for later enclosures such as [[The Paid Free-Will Class]].
