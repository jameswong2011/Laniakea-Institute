# Education and the Incapacitation of Learning

**Education and the Incapacitation of Learning** — industrial schooling as an institution designed to disable the capacities it claims to develop: it trains obedience, batching and permission-seeking while suppressing curiosity and cross-domain synthesis.

What industrial schooling actually teaches is visible in its form rather than its syllabus: regular arrival, age-batched progression, external evaluation, answer reproduction and permission-seeking. These capacities suit the factory and the bureaucracy that designed the school. What they crowd out is self-directed curiosity, [[Dialectic|dialectic]], apprenticeship, and the ability to form a question that has no approved rubric.

Around this sits a credential loop. The institution sells credentials as evidence of wisdom while rebuilding public memory around whatever its gatekeeping structure can recognise. Learning becomes preparation for assessment; assessment becomes access to rank; rank becomes proof that the method worked — a self-protecting circle in which the school certifies the hierarchy that certifies the school. Schools function as social devices whose specific purpose is to incapacitate learning; universities legitimate the arrangement by perpetually reconstituting social memory, selling privilege as wisdom, and damaging high-capability individuals through enforced overspecialisation.

The anthropology sharpens the indictment. Humanity's vocation is synthesis: the [[The Glue Species|glue species]] whose core capacity is translation across domains, persons and incompatible minds, not the repetition of isolated facts. An educational system that separates disciplines, rewards counterargument without synthesis, and punishes unusually abstract or individual thought therefore suppresses the species' evolutionary function while claiming to develop it. This is the first panel of [[The Farmed Planet]] table, and the earliest-operating one: installed on top of [[The Developmental Matrix|the developmental matrix]], before the child can audit what is being installed, where it forecloses the interface described in [[Curiosity and the Sacred Question]].
