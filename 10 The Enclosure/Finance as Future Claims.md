# Finance as Future Claims

**Finance as Future Claims** — the structural account of money and credit: money as compressed social trust, credit as a promise carried from future production into present action, and financial corruption as the point at which this encoding is made illegible to those bound by it.

Money is [[Compression and Decompression|compressed]] social trust, and credit carries a promise across time — kin to the [[Promises and the Hierarchy of Freedom|promises]] that elsewhere structure collaboration. This is why finance can coordinate action through time with extraordinary power, and why its corruption matters: corruption begins when the encoding becomes so opaque that those making the promise cannot understand who ultimately holds it or how its value changes.

Opacity then becomes a moat: institutions collect rent for navigating rules they helped make difficult. Beneath it sits measurement politics. Inflation transfers value through changes invisible in nominal balances; taxes on nominal gains can treat currency debasement as profit; official aggregates can exclude precarious forms of labour, or adopt adjustments under which lived decline becomes difficult to recognise. In the notation of [[Measurement as Transformation]], the recorded aggregate Aa becomes a political reality while the omitted remainder Ab is borne privately.

The analysis extends to the international layer, and it is structural rather than predictive. The issuer of a reserve currency receives seigniorage and can project power through settlement networks, sanctions, debt and access to liquidity. A multipolar order weakens the possibility that any one national currency remains the unquestioned global measure, and a move toward hard collateral or several interoperable settlement systems would constitute a monetary phase change: old claims repriced, exported monetary costs returned to their issuer.

The test is standard-agnostic. No monetary design creates justice by itself; rather, every monetary [[The Logos|Logos]] must make its extraction legible. A currency whose stability depends on permanent information asymmetry is, in the vocabulary of [[The Farmed Planet]], a farm instrument — and the unrestricted accumulation it enables terminates in [[Techno-Feudalism]].
