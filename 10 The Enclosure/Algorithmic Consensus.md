# Algorithmic Consensus

**Algorithmic Consensus** — the transfer of civilisation's editorial function to engagement-ranking systems: an anonymous editor that selects what is seen by optimising measurable reaction rather than explicit judgement.

The mechanism begins in memetics, where ideas are [[Memetic Lifeforms|living patterns]] competing for finite attention. Nuance has low memetic fitness: every additional condition raises a message's transmission cost. Platforms intensify the pressure: the editor is no longer a small group with contestable judgement but a feedback system optimising engagement. Outrage, identity confirmation, beauty, fear and simplicity travel well; slow [[Dialectic|dialectic]] does not. Popularity-driven feeds centralise editorial decisions, stunt the memetic evolutionary dialectic at the planetary level, fracture the population into ever-more-distant tribal clusters, and tend toward the eventual extinction of free will.

Generative models add a second compression. They learn the statistical centre of prior culture and make that centre cheap to reproduce: an assisted individual may produce more varied work than before while many assisted individuals converge on similar structures — a double effect (individual gains, collective convergence) already visible in early experiments. The loss is moral before it is aesthetic: cultural variation is the civilisation's moral search process, so homogenised stories narrow the futures that can be desired, then the worlds that can be built. A model trained on the past becomes a governor of the future without ever issuing a command.

The remedy is not to stop fiction or demand one authorised truth, since [[Hyperstition|hyperstition]] — story making itself real — is inseparable from conscious civilisation; the task is to make narrative agency legible: provenance for images of the future; plural channels not governed by one engagement function; spaces slow enough for conditional thought; local cultures that embody stories rather than merely consume them; education in how narratives recruit identity; incentives rewarding novelty, repair and truth; and the right to exit a memetic environment without losing social existence. The decisive question for any story, within [[The Farmed Planet]], is not only whether it is true now but what kind of chooser will exist if it succeeds.
