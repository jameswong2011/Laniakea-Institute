# The Bardo Examination

**The Bardo Examination** — death as the decompression of the soul's ledger, and each life as an examination answering a single question, whose deficiencies become the circumstances of the next incarnation.

Death removes the dense interface and its short-term cache; the life can then be read as a whole pattern rather than a succession of emergencies: consciousness encounters what its choices felt like across the beings and systems they affected. This is not merely a courtroom sentence but the [[Compression and Decompression|decompression]] of [[The Soul Ledger|the ledger]] into full relational knowledge. Embodied perception is undeveloped film — life exposes one frame at a time; death develops and screens the sequence from a vantage no moving frame possessed, so that separate accidents appear as one narrative geometry.

The examination has one question: **"Within the local informational environment just left, what is the nature of consciousness, existence, and reality in all their facets?"** The answer is not spoken; it is the whole understanding embodied by the life, compressed into the storehouse consciousness of the soul collective — the Yogācāra *ālaya-vijñāna* rendered as a data store. Deficiencies become the [[Karma as Contextual Law|karmic]] circumstances of the next incarnation: rebirth precisely into the conditions that address what was not compressed correctly. Graduation requires a complete answer relative to that local environment — a perfect local score, not omniscience concerning higher worlds.

The bardo itself is the interval in which familiar objects lose solidity and the stream meets its own habits as worlds: fearful tendencies render fearful forms, while clear recognition reveals them as projections without denying the [[Qualia|qualia]] they produce. In the Vajrayāna imagery, each gateway is guarded by a wrathful buddha testing for residues specific to its gate ([[Peaceful and Wrathful Deities]]).

Forgetting, finally, is functional. Complete memory of every prior branch would overwhelm the local [[Free Will and Decision-Space|decision-space]] and turn present choice into obedience to an inherited script. The ledger persists instead as attraction, aversion, aptitude, dream, conscience, synchronicity or unexplained recognition — tacit weight that practice can convert into conscious freedom.
