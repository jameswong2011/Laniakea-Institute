# Time from Choice

**Time from Choice** — the thesis that time is not the container in which minds act but "the exhaust of minds acting": choice entails change, change entails succession, and a cosmos without conscious choosers would be a static informational manifold.

The dependence forms a chain — *without time, no change; without change, no choice; without choice, no local consciousness* — and runs equally in reverse: a choice means a state before and a different state after, and that difference is what time *is*. Time first appears as "the interval required for difference to matter"; a consciousness that could never move between states could not compare, prefer, learn or choose. Time is thus fundamental not to the universe but to the *experiencing* of it.

The mechanism is the first movement from unity into [[Polarity as Compression|polarity]], which takes two forms. Sequential change lets the field alternate between states, and generates time; parallel change lets one partition vary while another holds, and generates worlds — the branching face of the same process, treated under [[The Entropic Branching Engine]]. Together they supply the grid within which finite consciousness can develop and [[Free Will and Decision-Space|choose]].

Human time is narrow. Spatial options can often be surveyed and revisited; temporal movement appears one-directional, with memory giving partial access to the past, anticipation only symbolic access to futures, and the acting body held to one moving frontier. This asymmetry is the [[The 3.5-Dimensional Being|3.5-dimensional]] condition; a consciousness with fuller temporal access might inhabit a lifetime as one shape rather than a vanishing succession of moments.

Calendars make the narrow field governable. Day, month and year retain a relation to celestial motion, but the seven-day week and the five-days-on, two-days-off work rhythm are institutional programmes which, repeated through wages, school and ritual, come to feel like properties of time itself; a more conscious civilisation would treat calendars as revisable interfaces. Whether time is finally present-centred, branching, or relational and retrocausal is deliberately left uncollapsed — one of [[The Open Tensions|the open tensions]].
