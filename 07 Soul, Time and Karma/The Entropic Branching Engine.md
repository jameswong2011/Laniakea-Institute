# The Entropic Branching Engine

**The Entropic Branching Engine** — the mechanism by which every exercise of [[Free Will and Decision-Space|free will]] splits the world: a consciousness at state 1 choosing between actions A and B continues as 1A, while a differentiated continuation of the same prior stream realises 1B.

At each decision point, polarity supplies alternatives, and the unchosen possibility does not simply disappear. The next interval yields 1A–2A, 1A–2B, 1B–2A and 1B–2B, so that a lifetime's decisions fan out into an expanding tree of reality-instances. This is no curiosity but the branching mechanism by which the Creator explores its own potential — the parallel face of the process whose sequential face is [[Time from Choice|time itself]].

The structure resembles the many-worlds interpretation of quantum mechanics, its nearest physical parallel, but the branching extends beyond measurement outcomes into consciousness, value and soul continuity. Every available branch can be actual somewhere without every branch being equally inhabited by the same centre of experience: choice determines which successor carries a particular line of attention, memory and responsibility, and may affect how much experiential weight gathers in a family of outcomes.

The standing objection is that choice becomes meaningless if every option occurs. But the objection views reality from the full tree while living occurs along a path: every destination may exist in the atlas, yet selecting a road still determines which landscape is crossed, whom the traveller meets, what capacities are built and which [[Promises and the Hierarchy of Freedom|promises]] become binding. The branch called "not chosen here" belongs to another continuation.

Identity is accordingly a branching continuity, neither an indivisible bead nor an undifferentiated total of all copies. Before choice, two successors share a history; after it, each accumulates a different [[The Soul Ledger|ledger]] — genealogically related without remaining numerically identical, another expression of [[Anātman (No-Self)|no-self]]. [[Dreams as Creator-Space|Dreams]], moreover, may carry traffic among branches of the same soul.
