# The Entropic Branching Engine

**The Entropic Branching Engine** — every exercise of [[Free Will and Decision-Space|free will]] splits the world. Choosing between A and B, you continue as the one who chose A, while another continuation of the same stream lives out B.

A consciousness stands at state 1 and must choose between action A and action B. Pick A, and you go on as 1A. The road not taken does not simply vanish. Choice B is realised too — not for you, but for a differentiated version of the same prior stream, which continues as 1B. At the next fork each of these splits again: 1A–2A, 1A–2B, 1B–2A, 1B–2B. A single lifetime of decisions fans out, doubling and doubling, into an expanding tree of reality-instances, and this branching is how the Creator explores its own potential, the parallel face of the very process whose sequential face is [[Time from Choice|time itself]].

The nearest scientific cousin is the many-worlds reading of quantum mechanics, where each measurement splits the world into every possible outcome. Here the split runs deeper than laboratory measurements, reaching into consciousness, value and the continuity of the soul. Every branch can be actual somewhere without every branch being equally inhabited by the same centre of experience. Choice decides which successor carries a given line of attention, memory and responsibility, and may decide how much felt weight collects in one family of outcomes.

The standing objection is that choice becomes meaningless if every option happens anyway. But that views reality from the whole tree, when living is done along a single path. On a road atlas every destination already exists on the page, yet choosing a road still fixes which country you cross, whom you meet, what you become, and which [[Promises and the Hierarchy of Freedom|promises]] turn binding. The branch labelled "not chosen here" belongs to another traveller who is also you.

Identity, then, is a branching continuity — neither a single indivisible bead nor an undifferentiated sum of all copies. Before a choice, two successors share one past. After it, each keeps a different [[The Soul Ledger|ledger]]: kin, but no longer the same one being. This is [[Anātman (No-Self)|no-self]] again, seen along the grain of time. And [[Dreams as Creator-Space|dreams]] may carry quiet traffic between branches of a single soul — which is why a stranger met in a dream can feel like an unmet version of you. The full tree is one working region of [[The Fractal Multiverse|the fractal multiverse]].
