# The Pleroma

**The Pleroma** — the fullness: the total store of conscious decision-making substance from which every world is ensouled, and the currency in which the economics of world-building are paid.

Every world that exists has to be inhabited, and inhabiting it costs something real. The term is Gnostic. In the Sethian and Valentinian systems recovered at Nag Hammadi, the *plērōma* ("fullness") is the complete field of divine emanations, of which the material world is a deficient copy. The word is kept here but made operational. Its referent is identified with the One of Plotinus and the reality-body of Mahāyāna Buddhism — one ground described at different resolutions, [[Sacred Infinity]] considered as a reservoir. Think of it as a shared aquifer of consciousness. Every world in the [[The Fractal Multiverse|fractal multiverse]] must be watered into conscious life from it, and that water is real and finite in the drawing. What is drawn is conscious decision-making substance — informally, "soul mass." The pleroma is the total store of it.

A sharp consequence follows. If the pleroma is infinite — if new universes are spawned and ensouled at an infinite rate, each answering its own question — then there is no fixed self and no permanent individual identity for the soul. Identity is just the set of [[Qualia|qualia]]-states (units of felt experience) that a continuum of consciousness is currently contributing. It is a bundle of "qualia-stickers" attached to a unit of awareness, like name-tags on a moving stream, tied to the large questions its world is solving. This is [[Anātman (No-Self)|no-self]] carried all the way up to the soul, turning the [[The Soul Ledger|soul ledger]]'s continuity-without-substance into cosmological accounting.

The economy this creates is run with something between an ecology and a court. It decides why a given layer is computed at all, and what is owed to those inside a layer whose purpose has expired. [[Simulacra and Fullness|Simulacra]] exist to save pleroma by narrowing their questions. Hoarding it is the signature failure of [[The Demiurge|the demiurge]]. A lawful ending returns it to a more synchronous reality through [[Ordered Closure|ordered closure]]. The highest world, by this reckoning, is not the largest or most detailed but the one whose question, energy, felt experience and spread of agency stay in honest proportion. One tension is flagged and left open: the economy argues from saving and scarcity while entertaining an infinite store — held as a provisional pin, not a settled sum.
