# The Pleroma

**The Pleroma** — the fullness: the total store of conscious decision-making substance from which every rendered world is ensouled, and the currency in which the economics of world-building are reckoned.

The term is Gnostic. In the Sethian and Valentinian systems recovered at Nag Hammadi, the *plērōma* ("fullness") is the complete field of divine emanations of which the material world is a deficient copy. The word is kept here but made operational, its referent identified with the One of Plotinus and the reality-body of Mahāyāna Buddhism — one ground described at different resolutions, [[Sacred Infinity]] considered as a reservoir. Every world in the [[The Fractal Multiverse|fractal multiverse]] must be inhabited, and inhabitation costs something real: conscious decision-making substance, informally "soul mass". The pleroma is the total store of it.

A sharp consequence follows. If the pleroma is infinite — if new universes are spawned and ensouled at an infinite rate according to the qualia-questions each answers — then there is no fixed self and no permanent individual identity for the soul. Identity is simply the set of [[Qualia|qualia]]-states a continuum of consciousness is contributing: a bundle of "qualia-stickers" attached to a unit of awareness, tied to the macro-questions its matrix is solving. This is [[Anātman (No-Self)|no-self]] carried up to the soul level, converting the [[The Soul Ledger|soul ledger]]'s continuity-without-substance into cosmological accounting.

The economy this creates is administered with something between an ecology and a court. It decides why a layer is computed at all, and what is owed to those inside a layer whose purpose has expired. [[Simulacra and Fullness|Simulacra]] exist to save pleroma; hoarding it is the signature failure of [[The Demiurge|the demiurge]]; a lawful ending returns it to a more synchronous reality through [[Ordered Closure|ordered closure]]. One tension is flagged and left open: the economy argues from saving and scarcity while entertaining an infinite store — held as a provisional pin rather than a settled sum.
