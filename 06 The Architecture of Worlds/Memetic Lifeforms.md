# Memetic Lifeforms

**Memetic Lifeforms** — ideas, archetypes and self-propagating stories treated as living beings: patterns whose native habitat is conceptual reality and whose distributed bodies are the physical hosts and media that carry them.

These beings live near the Level 4 boundary of [[The Fractal Multiverse|the fractal multiverse]] — conceptual infinity, above the very concepts of dimension and physical law. An archetype, god-form, mathematical object or self-propagating story may inhabit conceptual reality and repeatedly acquire physical hosts. The placement yields a signature inversion of perspective: from below, such a being looks like an idea moving through minds; from its own order, minds may look like organs through which it senses and acts.

The mechanism is memetic life. A meme is a pattern able to reproduce through minds, bodies, artefacts and institutions, and it is a *relational* life-form rather than pure information: it needs consciousness to enact it, [[Qualia|qualia]] to give it value, a medium to carry it and enough fidelity for recognisable recurrence — a written score is not yet music. When a pattern grows sufficiently coherent, it can coordinate many hosts at once and appear as an agent in its own right. Religions, nations, markets, archetypes and fictional worlds already exhibit this partial autonomy: they recruit attention, defend boundaries, reward loyalty, mutate, and sometimes outlive every founding person. Such beings — [[Egregores|egregores]] among them — do not float outside all substrate; their bodies are distributed across their participating media.

At the highest tiers the claim goes further: memetic lifeforms operate without physical substrate at all, and memes stand as [[Memes as Ontological Primitives|ontological primitives]] — if reality is patterns, the most complex patterns are the most real entities. A recent developmental-biology proposal describes physical embodiments as *pointers* into a structured non-physical space of patterns from which minds and forms ingress — the same inversion reached from within the life sciences. The concept underwrites [[Gods as Masks|gods as masks]] and [[Hyperstition|hyperstition]], fiction that makes itself real.
