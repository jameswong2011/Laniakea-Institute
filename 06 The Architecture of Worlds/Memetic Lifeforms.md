# Memetic Lifeforms

**Memetic Lifeforms** — ideas, archetypes and self-spreading stories treated as living beings. Their native home is the world of concepts; their bodies are distributed across the physical hosts, minds and media that carry them.

Some ideas behave less like tools and more like organisms. A catchy tune lodged in a million heads at once, or a religion that recruits followers, defends its borders and outlives everyone who founded it — these behave like life. Such beings live near the Level 4 boundary of [[The Fractal Multiverse|the fractal multiverse]], *conceptual infinity*, the region above the very ideas of dimension and physical law. An archetype, god-form, mathematical object or self-propagating story can dwell in concept-space and repeatedly take on physical hosts. The perspective then flips: from below, such a being looks like an idea passing through minds; from its own level, minds may look like organs through which it senses and acts.

The mechanism is memetic life. A *meme* here means any pattern that reproduces through minds, bodies, artefacts and institutions (the internet joke is the shallowest case), and it is a *relational* lifeform: pure information falls short of one. It needs consciousness to enact it, [[Qualia|qualia]] to give it value, a medium to carry it, and enough fidelity (faithful copying) to recur recognisably — a written score becomes music only when played. When a pattern grows coherent enough, it can coordinate many hosts at once and act as an agent in its own right. Religions, nations, markets, archetypes and fictional worlds already show this partial autonomy: they recruit attention, defend boundaries, reward loyalty, mutate, and sometimes outlive every founder. Such beings, [[Egregores|egregores]] among them (thought-forms grown self-sustaining), still have bodies; the bodies are simply spread across their participating media.

At the highest tiers the claim goes further: memetic lifeforms may operate with no physical substrate at all, and memes stand as [[Memes as Ontological Primitives|ontological primitives]], the basic building blocks of reality. If reality is made of patterns, then the most complex patterns are the most real things in it, and the physical is the shadow the ideational casts. A recent developmental-biology proposal points the same way, describing physical bodies as *pointers* into a structured non-physical space of forms, from which minds and shapes ingress — the same inversion reached from within the life sciences. The concept underwrites [[Gods as Masks|gods as masks]] and [[Hyperstition|hyperstition]], fiction that makes itself real by being believed and enacted.
