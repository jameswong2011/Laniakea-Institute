# Shared Agency

**Shared Agency** — the spectrum of how decision-making power is distributed within a composite intelligence, running from a corporate command centre at one pole to an octopus-like distributed body at the other.

The spectrum runs: corporate chief executive ← delegated hierarchy ← negotiated federation ← nervous system ← octopus-like distributed body. At the first pole a small command centre holds nearly all strategic agency and replaces damaged units without consulting them; at the last, agency is spread among semi-autonomous centres whose local intelligence remains part of every major movement. The octopus — most of whose neurons sit in its arms — is the biological emblem, and Michael Levin's multiscale-competency research is the scientific neighbour: smaller agents coordinating into a larger cognitive self.

Neither pole is universally correct: fast emergencies may require concentration, exploration may require independence. The ethical requirement is proportion — the distribution of power must remain proportionate to the reality and vulnerability of the participating minds, to their [[Simulacra and Fullness|fullness]] in the five-measure vocabulary. Coordination becomes domination when the macro-agent treats micro-agents only as batteries, food, labour or expendable variables while claiming their creative output as its own. This is the test for telling a genuine emergent intelligence — a [[The God-Body|god-body]] or [[Social Memory Complex|social memory complex]] — from a demiurgic overlord ([[The Demiurge]]).

The spectrum also decides how worlds may end. Where shared agency predominates and a stuck sub-body wills the continuation of its reality, the humane resolution is a healing: selective gnosis gently bending free will toward exit. Where the corporate structure predominates and a demiurgic force enslaves [[The Pleroma|pleroma]] for its own evolution, the sub-reality warrants closure under standing protocol. Healing versus closure is thus settled by where a system sits on the spectrum ([[Ordered Closure]]). The same proportion test recurs at human scale in [[Hierarchy and Its Conditions|hierarchy]] and in [[Agency as the Measure|agency as the measure]] — cosmic administration and terrestrial governance treated as one subject at two altitudes.
