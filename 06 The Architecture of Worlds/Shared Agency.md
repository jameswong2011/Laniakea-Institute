# Shared Agency

**Shared Agency** — the spectrum of how decision-making power is spread inside a composite intelligence: a single mind built from many smaller minds. It runs from a corporate command centre at one pole to an octopus-like distributed body at the other.

Whenever many minds compose one larger mind, they must settle how power is shared — and there is no single right answer. Lay the options on a line: corporate chief executive ← delegated hierarchy ← negotiated federation ← nervous system ← octopus-like distributed body. At the first pole, a small command centre holds nearly all strategic agency and replaces damaged units without consulting them. At the last, agency is spread among semi-autonomous centres whose local intelligence stays part of every major move. The octopus — most of whose neurons sit out in its arms, so each arm partly thinks for itself — is the biological emblem. Michael Levin's work on multiscale competency is the scientific neighbour: smaller agents coordinating into a larger cognitive self.

Neither pole is right for all seasons. A platoon under sudden fire needs concentration; a jazz band needs distributed independence. The ethical requirement is proportion. The distribution of power must stay proportionate to the reality and vulnerability of the participating minds — to their [[Simulacra and Fullness|fullness]], their degree of genuine independent experience. The plain rule: the more real and the more woundable a system's members, the more it owes them a voice in the moves that will affect them. Coordination curdles into domination the moment the greater agent treats its smaller ones only as batteries, food, labour or expendable variables while claiming their creative output as its own. Which way do value and agency flow — returned to the parts, or hoarded above? That is the test for telling a genuine emergent mind — a [[The God-Body|god-body]] or [[Social Memory Complex|social memory complex]] — from a [[The Demiurge|demiurgic]] overlord.

The spectrum also decides how worlds may end. Where shared agency predominates and a stuck sub-body wills its reality to continue, the humane resolution is a healing: selective gnosis gently bending free will toward exit. Where the corporate structure predominates and a demiurgic force enslaves [[The Pleroma|pleroma]] (the shared store of conscious substance) for its own evolution, the sub-reality warrants closure under standing protocol. Healing versus closure is thus settled by where a system sits on the spectrum ([[Ordered Closure]]). The same proportion test recurs at human scale in [[Hierarchy and Its Conditions|hierarchy]] and in [[Agency as the Measure|agency as the measure]] — cosmic administration and everyday governance treated as one subject at two altitudes.
