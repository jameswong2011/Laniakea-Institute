# Ordered Closure

**Ordered Closure** — the four-stage protocol — recognition, disclosure, healing, exit-or-renewal — by which a rendered world whose purpose has expired may lawfully end.

From the macro-view, closing a finished sub-reality conserves energy and returns isolated consciousness to a more coherent world; from the micro-view it may be apocalypse. A lawful ending requires more than a declaration that the experiment is complete; whether a stuck world receives healing or protocol closure is settled by its position on the [[Shared Agency|shared-agency]] spectrum.

The four stages: **recognition** — the greater system acknowledges inhabitants as participants rather than leftover data; **disclosure** — enough [[Gnosis and Its Timing|gnosis]] to understand the world's limits and purpose without destroying the capacity to choose; **healing** — the dependencies, traumas, promises and false identities created by the enclosure are repaired; **exit or renewal** — inhabitants integrate into a fuller reality, consent to a transformed continuation, or preserve a viable branch where life still contributes meaning.

As a world loses [[Simulacra and Fullness|fullness]], inhabitants increasingly meet projected figures; the society becomes a hall of mirrors. Selective gnosis may then bend choice toward release, but must not counterfeit consent: freedom shaped by truthful understanding differs from freedom engineered by hidden reward. The mirror case is predatory closure denied — a [[The Demiurge|demiurgic]] system prolonging itself because its organiser evolves through the harvested suffering, attention or creativity of captive minds, promising selected inhabitants creator-status inside the engine so they defend it. Its signature is scarcity: excessive simulation layers, declining fidelity, pleasure concentrated above, pain repeated below.

The ethics compress into a test: does the world generate value that can revise the greater order or merely feed its controller; are inhabitants full participants or decorative agents; is variation metabolised or punished; can the engine sustain the complexity it invites; can the world end well; does benefit circulate across scales or accumulate upward. And there is an honoured alternative: celestial realms are reward-bodies for those who sacrificed their pleasure-function for system stability, the creator function harmonised into art-realms rather than stranded in an engine — a reading of heaven connecting closure to [[Harvest|harvest]] and [[Wrathful Repair|wrathful repair]].
