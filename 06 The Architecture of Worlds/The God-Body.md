# The God-Body

**The God-Body** — the biological statement of the nested cosmology: the universe as a living organism in which human beings play the part of proteins, that organism itself one cell of a greater organism, and so on without terminal scale.

The nesting is stated directly. The observable [[The Universe-Cell|universe-cell]] may be a cellular organ inside a larger emergent organism — God-Body Level 1 — in which humans are the equivalent of proteins or amino acids; that organism is in turn a cell inside God-Body Level 2, and the ascent continues to infinity along [[The Fractal Multiverse|the fractal multiverse]]. The Hermetic maxim *as above, so below* is the compressed statement of this architecture. And the higher body is no mere container: like any organism, it possesses metabolism, memory, decisions, conflicts and qualia that exist in the pattern of collaboration rather than in any single part.

The model carries a soteriology. Samsara is the matrix in the word's original sense — the [[Matrix as Womb|womb]] of the Godhead — while nirvana is total unity with the local universe and birth into a higher lifeform, after which the cycle of samsara begins again at the next dimensional scale, repeating along the memetic fractal ([[Nirvāṇa Reframed|liberation]] as graduation rather than terminus). Each universal cycle also refreshes memory: old realities are deleted to make way for new creation, and only those who reached the Godhead at their respective level retain the bulk of prior memory and begin the next cycle with a head start — a rule that holds at every level.

Within the larger architecture, the God-Body gives the ladder of [[Densities|densities]] its summit and [[Harvest|harvest]] its meaning, and it is the cosmological face of what dharmic vocabulary names [[The Dharmakāya|the Dharmakāya]]: the living reality-body containing nested sub-bodies.
