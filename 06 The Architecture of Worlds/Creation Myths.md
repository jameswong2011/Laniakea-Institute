# Creation Myths

**Creation Myths** — the stories that older, star-guided intelligences give a young species when it first asks *how did it all begin?* Each is a small, simplified picture of how the cosmos came to be, and the receiving culture grows up around it.

Every thinking species asks the same question early in its life: how did everything start? This is the first and rawest form of [[Curiosity and the Sacred Question|the sacred question]]. But the real chain of causes that set the cosmos going is almost certainly too vast and too strange for a young mind to grasp, so the answer has to be sized to the asker. A parent asked about human reproduction by a four-year-old skips the biology textbook and gives a smaller story, truer than nothing. The [[Stellar Logoi|stellar systems]] and elder guides who shepherd new worlds do the same. They answer a childlike question with a childlike answer, and those answers are the creation myths.

Each myth is a [[Compression and Decompression|compression]], a large truth packed into a small, carriable form. The compression is lossy, since no telling at a young species' scale can hold the whole, and perspectival, since each mentor compresses one reality from the angle of its own stellar lineage and embeds real insight alongside the simplification. It is also load-bearing. The myth becomes the ground a whole civilisation is raised on, and its images and buried assumptions quietly shape everything built later on top of them (the foundations of a building decide what the upper floors can become).

The category does double work in the wider map. It explains why the world's cosmologies differ yet rhyme (different teachers compressing one reality from different sides, the premise developed under [[Radical Syncretism|radical syncretism]]), and it grounds a way of reading scripture and channelled material as [[Trip Reports and Revelation|structured testimony]] — neither literal report nor mere invention, but a compression fitted to the developmental moment of its audience. The account assumes the sowing of young worlds by elder lineages, treated under [[Seeding and the First Ones]].
