# Densities

**Densities** — the ladder of stages in how consciousness is organised, running from primordial particles up to a planetary mind. A density is not a place you travel to or a rank of moral prestige. It is the amount and kind of relation a being can hold together as a single decision-maker.

Think of it as a set of year-groups for consciousness. You do not leap from the first to the last; you climb, and each stage can hold more relation as one coherent self than the one below. The rungs run like this. **Density 0.5** is particles and raw energetic structure — the standard-model matter thrown off by the Big Bang, which first furnishes a physical world. **First density** is elemental consciousness, laying down the stable chemical foundations that life will need. **1.5** is the single living cell. **Second density** is multicellular life, plants and animals. **2.5** is the highly social, clever animal — dolphins, octopuses, crows. **Third density** is the self-reflective being: a creature able to examine its own motives and choose a moral direction. **3.5** is that being armed with planet-scale tools. **Fourth density** is planetary consciousness itself — a coherent [[Social Memory Complex|social memory complex]] (a planet-wide shared mind), roughly a Type I civilisation on the Kardashev scale of energy use. Humanity sits at 3.5.

The half-steps matter because evolution is not a clean jump between fixed forms. It is a stretch in which the organising centre migrates. The 1.5 cell is already more than chemistry, but not yet surrendered to a body. The 2.5 animal is socially brilliant without full moral responsibility. At 3.5, tools hand a species planet-scale power before planet-scale wisdom has arrived — like a teenager with an adult's strength and a child's judgement. Power comes during the crossing, which is why every half-step is unstable.

Third density is specially protected, because this is where conscious choice is being tested. Higher intelligences may influence the field but not override it. Direct violation of [[Free Will and Decision-Space|free will]] incurs severe [[Karma as Contextual Law|karmic debt]], and the line between influence and violation is set by local [[Dharma-Law]]. The invariant is whether the developing being keeps a real capacity to recognise and answer the [[Polarity as Compression|polarity]] — the live choice between opposites — that it is offered.

The vocabulary adapts the channelled Ra material, read as structured testimony rather than authority ([[Trip Reports and Revelation|trip reports]]). And the map is a ladder only when seen from below. From above it is a cycle of embodiment, in which a fourth-density planet may serve as one organ of a higher system — the graduation threshold treated under [[Harvest]].
