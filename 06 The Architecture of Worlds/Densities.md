# Densities

**Densities** — the ladder of stages in the organisation of consciousness, running from primordial particles to planetary mind. A density is not a place or a rank of moral prestige but the amount and kind of relation a being can hold as one coherent decision-maker.

The ladder runs: **0.5** — particles and primordial energetic structure; **first density** — elemental consciousness building the stable chemical foundations for life; **1.5** — cellular life; **second density** — multicellular animals and plants; **2.5** — highly social, intelligent animals such as dolphins, octopuses and crows; **third density** — self-reflective beings able to examine their own motives and choose a moral orientation; **3.5** — a planetary tool-making civilisation; **fourth density** — planetary consciousness, a coherent [[Social Memory Complex|social memory complex]], a Type I civilisation on the Kardashev scale. Humanity currently sits at 3.5.

The half-densities matter because evolution is not a leap between fixed forms but a period in which the organising centre migrates. The 1.5-density cell is already more than chemistry yet not surrendered to a multicellular body; the 2.5-density animal is socially brilliant without full reflective responsibility; at 3.5, tools give a species planet-scale power before planet-scale wisdom is complete. Power arrives during the transition, which is why each interval is unstable.

Third density is specially protected. Because the universal body is there testing conscious choice, higher intelligences may influence the field but not violate it: direct violation of [[Free Will and Decision-Space|free will]] creates severe [[Karma as Contextual Law|karmic debt]], and the boundary between influence and violation is set by local [[Dharma-Law]] — the invariant being whether the developing being retains a meaningful capacity to recognise and answer the offered [[Polarity as Compression|polarity]].

The vocabulary adapts the channelled Ra material, read as structured testimony rather than authority ([[Trip Reports and Revelation|trip reports and revelation]]). And the map is a ladder only from below: from above it is a cycle of embodiment, in which a fourth-density planet may serve as one organ of a higher system — the graduation threshold treated under [[Harvest]].
