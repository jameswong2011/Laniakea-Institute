# Process Ontology

**Process Ontology** — the position that action rather than substance is the basic constituent of manifested reality: action itself is the substantive aspect of existence. Reality is not a warehouse of nouns but a field of verbs that temporarily stabilise into noun-like patterns.

The claim completes the analysis of [[Śūnyatā (Emptiness)|emptiness]]: when substances dissolve, process remains. The reversal is exact — there are not first people who then act. A person is a continuing style of sensing, remembering, valuing, choosing and relating; a star is a long combustion; a species is an evolving lineage; even a stone is an event extended beyond the human tempo.

Patterns nevertheless endure. A whirlpool persists while its water changes; a melody remains recognisable through different instruments; a self carries causal continuity without an immutable owner ([[Anātman (No-Self)|no-self]]). Stability is not proof of substance — it is the successful renewal of form. No detached spectator stands outside the field: subject and object each help produce the other, and the observer is itself a phenomenon inside a further act of observation ([[Measurement as Transformation|measurement]] all the way up).

The position converges with Western process philosophy, which treats event-like "actual occasions" — Alfred North Whitehead's term — rather than enduring substances as reality's basic units; the boundary remains that process philosophy supplies a mature vocabulary for becoming and relation, but not by itself the Logos–Dharma–compression architecture. The deeper convergence is a shared reversal: relation does not happen *between* complete things; apparently complete things precipitate from relation ([[Dependent Origination|dependent origination]]).

The ontological sequence runs: relation → action → experience → memory → pattern → renewed action. Every relation produces a new order with powers its components lacked alone. Memory illustrates the whole: an event passes once, but its pattern crosses time by becoming a compression selected by emotion, attention and present need ([[Information as Record]], [[Compression and Decompression]]). Permanence belongs to reproducible pattern, not frozen matter, and existence stays alive because no pattern is a perfect copy of its prior turn ([[Time from Choice]]).
