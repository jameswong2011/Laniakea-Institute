# Process Ontology

**Process Ontology** — the view that action, not substance, is the basic stuff of reality. ("Ontology" simply means an account of what exists.) Reality is a field of verbs that briefly stabilise into noun-like patterns, with no warehouse of nouns underneath. Action itself is the substantive aspect of existence.

The claim finishes the work of [[Śūnyatā (Emptiness)|emptiness]]. When you dissolve every fixed substance, something is still left: the processing itself. And the usual order is reversed: acting comes first, and "a person" is the name for a certain style of it. A person is a continuing way of sensing, remembering, valuing and choosing; a star is a long, slow combustion; a species an evolving lineage. Even a stone is an event, just one drawn out far beyond the human tempo, weathering over millions of years.

Patterns still endure, and this is the part that confuses people. A whirlpool holds its shape while the water rushes through it and is replaced; a melody stays recognisable on a piano or a violin though the two share not a single sound-wave; a self carries causal continuity without an unchanging owner ([[Anātman (No-Self)|no-self]]). Stability, then, is the successful *renewal* of a form, and no evidence of a hidden substance underneath. Nor is there a detached spectator standing outside the field. Subject and object help produce each other, and any observer is itself a further event being observed ([[Measurement as Transformation|measurement all the way up]]).

The position meets Western process philosophy, which drops lasting substances and takes momentary "actual occasions" (Alfred North Whitehead's term) as reality's basic units. That tradition supplies a mature vocabulary for becoming and relation. What it does not supply on its own is the compression architecture in which these processes are the unfolding of a seed. The shared insight is the reversal itself: relation comes first, and apparently complete things precipitate out of it ([[Dependent Origination|dependent origination]]).

The basic sequence runs: relation → action → experience → memory → pattern → renewed action. Each relation yields a new order with powers its parts lacked alone. Memory shows the whole cycle at work. An event happens once and is gone, yet its pattern crosses time by becoming a compressed record, selected by emotion, attention and present need ([[Information as Record]], [[Compression and Decompression]]). Permanence belongs to reproducible pattern and never settles into frozen matter. And existence stays alive precisely because no pattern is ever a perfect copy of its last turn ([[Time from Choice]]).
