# Ontic Structural Realism

**Ontic Structural Realism** — the modern position, argued by James Ladyman and Don Ross, that fundamental physics describes a world of *relations* with no self-standing things underneath them. Structure, not substance, is what ultimately exists. ("Ontic" means "concerning what there is".)

At bottom, reality is a pattern of relationships, and the "objects" are just knots in the pattern. When physics describes an electron it lists relations — its mass, its charge, how it responds to fields, how it interacts with other particles. Look for something further, a lump of "electron-stuff" wearing those properties, and nothing turns up. The properties *are* the electron. A chess knight makes the point in miniature: swap the carved plastic for a coin and the game continues untouched, because the knight is purely its role, how it moves, what it threatens, its relations to the other pieces. The relata (the things supposedly *being* related) dissolve into the relations. The old substances drop out as an idle posit, a placeholder that does no work.

The striking thing is where this arrives. Contemporary analytic philosophy, working only from the content of mature physics, lands almost exactly where the Madhyamaka landed by pure argument. Both deny the very same thing: intrinsic, context-free existence, *svabhāva*, the property of being what you are all on your own ([[Śūnyatā (Emptiness)|emptiness]]). One route analyses dependence, the other reads the equations, and both conclude that reality is relational all the way down, with no layer of self-standing atoms beneath the web ([[Dependent Origination|dependent origination]]). Two traditions that never met map one structure.

A relational world strains classical logic at its edges, throwing up boundary paradoxes, cases where a thing seems both to be and not to be something. Jay Garfield and Graham Priest note that Nāgārjuna's [[The Catuṣkoṭi|four-cornered negation]] already supplies the tools to hold such contradictions without collapse.

The agreement is load-bearing. It shows why [[Metaphysics Precedes Physics|metaphysics precedes physics]]: how you read the formalism is already doing ontological work, deciding what the mathematics is *about*. And it opens the door to treating structure as literally informational (pure pattern, which is code), feeding the programme that derives [[It from Bit|it from bit]] and [[Spacetime from Entanglement|spacetime from entanglement]]. Structural realism is one stone in the arch of [[The Open Theory of Everything|an open theory of everything]].
