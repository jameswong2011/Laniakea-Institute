# Emanation and the Sub-Logoi

**Emanation and the Sub-Logoi** — the descending creative lineage by which infinite creative energy is stepped down through successively more local creators, each establishing the physical and moral parameters of the world below it.

The lineage descends as a chain: **Intelligent Infinity → Great Central Sun → Logos → sub-Logoi → planetary and stellar creators → individuated souls** — locally, the galaxy as [[The Logos|Logos]], our sun as sub-Logos, the planet and the individual as sub-sub-Logoi. Each tier does not merely transmit energy downward; it sets the physical and moral parameters of the reality beneath it, the way a grammar sets what sentences are possible without dictating which are spoken.

The hierarchy is fractal rather than bureaucratic. A greater Logos does not manufacture every smaller being as an external artisan; it establishes a possibility-space, divides its creative energy, and permits local centres to develop their own physical and moral variations — a branching pattern like a fern or a network of blood vessels. A galaxy, star, planet, species, civilisation, or person may each act as Logos relative to a lower-order world, which connects the scheme to the [[The Fractal Multiverse|fractal multiverse]] and the ladder of [[Densities|densities]].

The decisive corollary concerns personal creators. "We are not created directly by the One Infinite Creator, but by our own Logos and its sub-Logoi": beings are the same substance of creation stepped down and down, and their personal creators are local portions of the Infinite Creator, not the final source. The channelled Ra and Hidden Hand material describes the same architecture as Russian-doll creation — levels within levels, each portion containing Intelligent Infinity as its essence — and Neoplatonic emanation (the One, Nous, World Soul) is the older mirror of the claim. The scheme thus guards against the central error of [[The Demiurge|the Demiurge]]: a local creator mistaking its limited order for the totality.
