# Dialectic

**Dialectic** — polarity made conscious: the deliberate operation by which two partial structures enter conflict, reveal each other's limits, and generate a third arrangement that changes the frame rather than averaging the difference.

Dialectic is what happens when [[Polarity as Compression|polarity]] stops being suffered and starts being used. Two partial positions collide, each exposing what the other cannot see, and the collision yields a new arrangement. The result need not be a bland midpoint: a genuine synthesis preserves whatever was true in each side while changing the frame that made them incompatible. A midpoint may inherit both errors; synthesis changes the axis. Hegelian movement, tantric union, yin–yang circulation, formal debate, [[Humour as Esoteric Technology|humour]], and biological symbiosis are versions of the same operation.

The operation breathes in two movements, treated fully under [[Svātantrika and Prāsaṅgika|Svātantrika and Prāsaṅgika]]: a source movement that constructs positions and a sink movement that dissolves them — construction, testing, consequence, dissection, emptiness, reconstruction. Construction without dissolution fills the world with unexamined systems; dissolution without construction turns intelligence into permanent negation.

For any serious disagreement the complete practice runs to eight steps: state each position in terms its inhabitant would recognise; identify the experience or value that makes it attractive; separate that value from the historical form currently carrying it; extend the governing principle to its [[The Conceptual Terminus|conceptual terminus]]; expose contradictions and costs at every affected scale; preserve the information revealed by the failure; construct at least one arrangement neither side could have supplied alone; and test that arrangement through lived consequence rather than verbal elegance. This is explicitly more demanding than moderation.

Dialectic also carries a cosmic assignment. Because reason is the one compression format legible across incompatible minds, the species whose vocation is translation and synthesis — humanity as [[The Glue Species|the glue species]] — has dialectic as its species-function: the human contribution to [[The Logos|the Logos]] is the conscious manufacture of syntheses that no single tradition could reach alone.
