# Dharma-Qualia

**Dharma-Qualia** — direct felt actuality: the character of experience before it has been filtered into a public concept; what consequence feels like from inside a conscious partition. It is the second of the two meanings held apart within *dharma*, alongside [[Dharma-Law|Dharma-Law]].

Redness, grief, warmth, humiliation, relief, moral clarity, and sacred presence are dharmas in this experiential sense — the direct sensed datum before conceptual overlay. The usage follows the Buddhist habit of calling the immediate constituents of experience *dharmas*, fused with the modern vocabulary of [[Qualia|qualia]]: at the human level dharma means truth and agreed principle, while at the divine level it means memory — what is kept as the sense of self — and realness — which aspect of the perceptive environment is deemed most critical as a vantage toward evolution.

The central formula holds reality and emptiness together. Qualia are real because experience is the site at which the universe receives its own activity; they are also empty because no quale appears without conditions — a sense field, a nervous or subtle body, a prior contrast, an object or interactee, a history of attention, and a consciousness able to be affected. **Qualia are actual as events and empty as substances.** These are not opposites but the same structure read twice, in the manner of [[The Two Truths|the two truths]] and [[Dependent Origination|dependent origination]].

The relation to Dharma-Law is a reciprocal audit. Law without qualia becomes dead code: regulation with no inward measure of its effects. Qualia without law become unstructured intensity: feeling unable to preserve a world. Law gives experience a reliable field; experience tells law whether the field is worth preserving. Because qualia arise at the intersection of [[Polarity as Compression|polarities]] — a home partition meeting its catalyst — Dharma-Qualia is also the answer to where felt difference comes from, feeding the treatment of [[Consciousness as Primary|consciousness as primary]].
