# Dharma-Qualia

**Dharma-Qualia** — direct felt actuality: the raw character of an experience before it has been filtered into a shared concept. It is what consequence feels like from inside a conscious partition — the second of the two meanings held apart within *dharma*, alongside [[Dharma-Law|Dharma-Law]].

Start with the obvious. Redness, grief, warmth, humiliation, relief, moral clarity, sacred presence — these are dharmas in the experiential sense, the sensed datum before any concept is laid over it. The usage follows the Buddhist habit of calling the immediate constituents of experience *dharmas*, fused with the modern vocabulary of [[Qualia|qualia]] (the felt "what-it-is-like" of an experience). The word stretches across scales. At the human level dharma means truth and agreed principle. At the divine level it means memory — what is kept as the sense of self — and realness: which slice of the perceived environment counts most as a vantage toward growth.

The central formula holds reality and emptiness together. Qualia are real because experience is the one site where the universe actually receives its own activity. They are also empty because no quale ever appears on its own. Each one needs conditions: a sense field, a nervous or subtle body, a prior contrast, an object or interactee, a history of attention, and a consciousness able to be affected. Remove the conditions and the redness simply vanishes — it was never a standalone thing. **Qualia are actual as events and empty as substances.** These are not opposites but the same structure read twice, in the manner of [[The Two Truths|the two truths]] and [[Dependent Origination|dependent origination]].

The relation to Dharma-Law is a reciprocal audit — each keeps the other honest. Law without qualia becomes dead code: regulation with no inward measure of its effects. Qualia without law become unstructured intensity: feeling that cannot hold a world together. Law gives experience a reliable field; experience tells law whether that field is worth preserving. And because qualia arise where a home partition of consciousness meets its catalyst — the felt spark struck at the meeting line of two [[Polarity as Compression|polarities]] — Dharma-Qualia is also the answer to where felt difference comes from, feeding the treatment of [[Consciousness as Primary|consciousness as primary]].
