# Radiance

**Radiance** — the compression pattern by which a coherent centre transmits to many receivers at once. It emits a single form that different minds can each unpack in their own way, without the centre controlling any one response.

One source reaches many minds at once, and each makes something of its own from what arrives: that is radiance, the broadcasting style proper to [[The Logos|the Logos]] (the ordering intelligence that turns chaos into workable order). Command issues instructions and monitors compliance. A radiant centre emits one coherent form — a symbol, a teaching, a spectrum of light — and lets very different receivers unfold it according to their own capacities. The sun aims no private beam at each leaf, yet every plant photosynthesises in its own way. The measure of quality is a particular kind of efficiency: good radiance produces the most meaningful, perception-improving activity for the least attentional mass. The paradigm case is a star, treated separately as [[Stellar Logoi|stellar Logoi]], whose light is at once power, pattern, and invitation.

Translating a concept into a symbol, image, ritual, interface, or world is not decoration but a high form of information preservation and [[Qualia Design|qualia design]] (the art of composing which experiences get rendered at all). The form survives precisely because the receivers do the [[Compression and Decompression|decompression]], each generation and culture unpacking it afresh. Religious iconography, a ritual calendar, and a well-made app interface are the same technology at different scales: durable radiant forms that keep organising perception long after their designers are gone. This is the mechanism by which [[Gods as Masks|gods function as interfaces]] and [[Memetic Lifeforms|memetic lifeforms]] persist.

The political corollary is blunt. Authority over symbolic design is authority over what a whole civilisation can notice, feel, imagine, and eventually do. A normative test follows from the definition: the best Logos creates a fertile decision-space — stable enough for consequence, open enough for surprise, legible enough for repair — and controlling every output has nothing to do with it. So radiance stands opposed on two fronts at once: to coercive command, and to the anonymous curation of attention examined under [[Algorithmic Consensus|algorithmic consensus]].
