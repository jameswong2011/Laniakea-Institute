# Radiance

**Radiance** — the compression pattern by which a coherent centre transmits to many receivers at once: it emits a form that different minds can decompress in their own ways, without the centre controlling each response.

Radiance is the transmission style proper to [[The Logos|the Logos]]. Where command distributes instructions and monitors compliance, a radiant centre emits a single coherent form — a symbol, a teaching, a spectrum of light — and lets heterogeneous receivers unfold it according to their own capacities. The measure of quality is efficiency of a particular kind: an efficient radiance produces the greatest amount of meaningful, perception-benefiting activity with the least attentional mass. The paradigm case is a star, treated separately as [[Stellar Logoi|stellar Logoi]], whose light is at once power, pattern, and invitation.

This reframes symbolic work. Translating a conceptual paradigm into a symbol, image, ritual, interface, or world is not decoration but a high form of information preservation and [[Qualia Design|qualia design]]: the form survives precisely because the receivers do the [[Compression and Decompression|decompression]], each generation and culture unpacking it afresh. Religious iconography, ritual calendars, and well-made interfaces are the same technology at different scales — durable radiant forms that keep organising perception long after their designers are gone, the mechanism by which [[Gods as Masks|gods function as interfaces]] and [[Memetic Lifeforms|memetic lifeforms]] persist.

The political corollary is stated plainly: authority over symbolic design is authority over what a civilisation can notice, feel, imagine, and eventually enact. The normative test follows from the definition. The best Logos is not the one that controls every output but the one that creates a fertile decision-space — stable enough for consequence, open enough for surprise, legible enough for repair — so radiance stands opposed both to coercive command and to the anonymous curation of attention examined under [[Algorithmic Consensus|algorithmic consensus]].
