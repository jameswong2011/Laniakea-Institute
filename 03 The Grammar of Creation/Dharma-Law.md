# Dharma-Law

**Dharma-Law** — the cosmos's evolving rulebook: the governing order by which divided consciousness can work together once the one mind has become many. It is one of two meanings held inside the word *dharma* (so distinct that a single term for both is a hindrance), the other being [[Dharma-Qualia|Dharma-Qualia]].

Dharma-Law is the rules of the game that all the players have agreed to. Chinese Buddhism renders *dharma* directly as "law", and the hint is worth taking seriously: this is the rule-set by which the many partitions of one mind, the separate pieces produced by [[The First Differentiation|the first differentiation]] (the original splitting of the one mind into many), connect with themselves and with each other. Physical regularities, karmic ties, ethical precepts, social duties, vows, and the rules of a contemplative path are all one collaborative rulebook read at different scales.

The patterns of interaction inside the all-mind generate patterns of patterns: rules for forming, changing, and relating other rules. Nobody hands these codes down from outside on a fixed tablet of commands. They evolve under pressure, the way a legal system or a living language slowly rewrites itself toward whatever works. A prohibition like a deadly sin condenses a long history of consequences within one collaborative ecology, the seven deadly sins being seven behaviours a consciousness is taught not to enact when it collaborates with others; nothing about it floats arbitrarily above life. Morality, in this light, is in-game training data: examples the system learns from, not decrees barked from beyond the game.

Two design principles follow. First, mature law puts the conditions of collaboration above the prestige of its current custodians. Second, because dharma is order wrung from extremes, its method is the middle way: truth reliably reveals itself as the median between two [[Polarity as Compression|polarity]] sources, a discovery, not a compromise. And as living code, the rulebook must be periodically flushed and renewed — the breath examined under [[The Dharma-Ending Age|the Dharma-ending age]].

Within the larger architecture the roles divide cleanly. [[The Logos|The Logos]] (the ordering intelligence that turns chaos into workable order) designs a possibility-space; Dharma-Law governs the relations available inside it; and [[Dharma-Qualia|Dharma-Qualia]] audits the result. Law without felt experience becomes dead code, regulation with no inward measure of its own effects. Both meet inside the living reality-body, [[The Dharmakāya|the dharmakāya]].
