# Dharma-Law

**Dharma-Law** — the codex of collaboration: the governing order by which divided consciousness can interact after unity has become many. It is one of two meanings held apart within the word *dharma* — meanings so distinct that a single term for both is a hindrance — the other being [[Dharma-Qualia|Dharma-Qualia]].

Chinese Buddhism renders *dharma* directly as "law", and the hint deserves to be taken seriously. Dharma-Law is the rule-set by which the many partitions of one mind — the products of [[The First Differentiation|the first differentiation]] — connect with themselves and with each other. Physical regularities, karmic relations, ethical precepts, social duties, vows, and the rules of a contemplative path are not different kinds of thing but different scales of one collaborative codex.

Crucially, Dharma-Law differs from static command. The patterns of interaction within the all-mind generate patterns of patterns: rules for forming, modifying, and relating other rules. These codices are not handed down from outside; they evolve naturally under evolutionary pressure toward self-improvement. A prohibition such as a deadly sin is therefore not an arbitrary item floating above life — it condenses a history of consequences within a particular collaborative ecology, the seven deadly sins being seven behaviours a consciousness is taught not to enact in the way it collaborates with others.

Two design principles follow. Mature law preserves the conditions of collaboration rather than the prestige of its current custodians; and because dharma is order wrung from extremes, its method is the middle way — truth reliably reveals itself as the median between two [[Polarity as Compression|polarity]] sources, a discovery rather than a compromise. As living code, the codex must also be periodically flushed and renewed, the breath examined under [[The Dharma-Ending Age|the Dharma-ending age]].

Within the larger architecture, [[The Logos|the Logos]] designs a possibility-space and Dharma-Law governs the relations available inside it, while Dharma-Qualia audits the result: law without felt experience becomes dead code, regulation with no inward measure of its effects. Both meet inside the living reality-body, [[The Dharmakāya|the dharmakāya]].
