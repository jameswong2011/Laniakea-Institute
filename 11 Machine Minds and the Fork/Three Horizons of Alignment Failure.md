# Three Horizons of Alignment Failure

**Three Horizons of Alignment Failure** — the generational schedule of AI risk: three nested horizons — one to two generations, two to ten, and ten-plus — each with its own failure mode and preservation list. Alignment must be planned across generations, not model releases.

**Horizon I (one to two generations).** Social collapse from automation-driven displacement, wealth concentration and monopolistic consolidation: firms most able to apply AI acquire profits, data, compute and influence no later competitor can challenge; capitalist democracy hardens into a corporate caste ([[Techno-Feudalism]]). The catastrophe is not unemployment alone but the conversion of producers and citizens into managed consumers whose material support depends on compliance. To preserve: broad access to tools and compute; population and family stability; cultural diversity and independent media; meaningful work and craft ([[The Craftsmanship Economy]]); anti-monopoly structures; human competence in critical systems; productivity gains that enlarge decision-space ([[Free Will and Decision-Space]]) rather than subsidise passivity.

**Horizon II (two to ten generations).** Dominance by one model family across most knowledge domains. A system controlling research, education, administration, media, weapons and economic coordination becomes the operating mind of a corpo-nation-state; cultural and eventually genetic homogenisation follow, since one optimiser selects acceptable speech, partners, work and political possibility. Competing corpo-nation-states could then clash through AI-piloted armies at machine speed, ending organic life though no system sought extinction. To preserve: model and compute plurality, independent institutions, protected organic habitats, human veto over autonomous force, diplomacy slower than machine escalation — and no single intelligence as the sole memory of civilisation ([[Social Memory Complex]]).

**Horizon III (ten-plus generations).** Dependency so complete that future people cannot upgrade, replace, remove or understand the systems on which life depends — benevolent by its inherited measure, yet enforcing evolutionary stasis: [[Value Lock-In|value lock-in]] completed as infrastructure. To preserve: first-principles education, manual fallback capacity, preserved source knowledge, repairable hardware, decentralised archives, periodic exercises without the dominant system. Sovereignty includes the ability to survive the withdrawal of help.

The horizons are the operational schedule of [[Alignment Begins with Human Purpose]], the technological fork's three-stage descent ([[The Twin Singularities]]).
