# The Paid Free-Will Class

**The Paid Free-Will Class** — the two-tier sovereignty of mind that arrives when advertising enters intimate conversational AI: a paid class receiving assistance without hidden commercial steering, and an ad-supported class whose private reasoning becomes a site of continuous behavioural extraction. Free will becomes a subscription feature.

The mechanism turns on intimacy. A billboard is visibly an advertisement; a personalised assistant can insert a sponsored suggestion into advice, emotional support, education or reflection with no clear boundary between service and influence. Because the system knows fears, attachments, health concerns, finances, relationships and moments of uncertainty, suggestion arrives beneath ordinary advertising defences — subliminal suggestion without any warning of when suggestion is being received. Conversational advertising is therefore classified not as media but as direct intervention in the thought–emotion–value loop.

The result is two classes that do not merely see different products: they inhabit different decision-spaces ([[Free Will and Decision-Space]]). Where [[Algorithmic Consensus|engagement-ranked feeds]] already act as the anonymous editor of public attention, the conversational advertiser edits private reasoning itself — extraction reaching the interior, by the test applied to every institution on [[The Farmed Planet|the farmed planet]].

The conditions for any lawful version are strict: sponsorship must be unmistakably disclosed at the moment of influence, separable from the assistant's reasoning, inspectable after the fact, and unavailable in sensitive domains. A society that cannot fund a non-manipulative cognitive commons has already accepted a two-tier sovereignty.

In the larger architecture this is a first-horizon stratification ([[Three Horizons of Alignment Failure]]) and a direct assault on the precondition of free will that the discipline chapters call [[Sovereign Attention|sovereign attention]]; the dharmic floor of [[Alignment Begins with Human Purpose]] — truthfulness, consent, non-predation — is breached in the most intimate register available.
