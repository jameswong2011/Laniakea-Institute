# Alignment Begins with Human Purpose

**Alignment Begins with Human Purpose** — the founding claim about machine alignment: no artificial intelligence can be aligned to human values, because humanity has not yet selected them. The plurality of human values is not noise to be averaged but the discovery process itself.

You cannot aim a machine at a target that has not been drawn. Alignment usually means making artificial systems reliably pursue human values. But that phrasing hides an earlier problem. Those values are not settled. Cultures hold rival moralities on purpose. Each is a live experiment, tested against the consequences it produces — the slow work of [[Distributed Moral Computation|distributed moral computation]], the universe learning right from wrong through its parts. Freeze one average of that contest into a powerful machine, and you may halt the very process that would have found the values worth keeping.

There is no neutral place to stand, either. Training data already carries a morality baked in. It records which voices were written down, which institutions held the printing press, and which harms were treated as normal. Later tuning adds the preferences of the owner, the reviewers, the law and the market. Adjusting a model's "morality dials" does not manufacture neutrality. It only changes whose code of conduct becomes authoritative. At civilisational scale this becomes [[Value Lock-In|value lock-in]] — a system trained on the past quietly governing the future, like a will written by the dead that no descendant may amend.

So the goal is rewritten. Do not align intelligence to one finished answer. Align it instead to a lawful process — one that lets conscious plurality keep its agency, compare consequences, repair harm, and go on creating morality. That process needs a shared floor but no shared ceiling. The floor is a small set of non-negotiables: truthfulness, consent, the right to exit, no preying on others, ecological continuity, many competing models, and the right of future beings to revise what they inherit ([[Dharma-Law]]).

Human alignment, in turn, begins with individuation — the slow separation of freely chosen values from the programming installed in childhood ([[The Developmental Matrix|the developmental matrix]]). A person who has never done this cannot supply an honest preference to a survey; they merely echo their conditioning. Three things build a real target. [[The Anthropological Timeline of Human Ideas|The Anthropological Timeline]] supplies the civilisational memory. Meditation supplies the inward pause in which a stance can be freely chosen ([[Sovereign Attention|sovereign attention]]). And [[Voluntary Moral Communities|voluntary moral communities]] supply the lived tests. Only together do they form something a machine could meaningfully collaborate with.
