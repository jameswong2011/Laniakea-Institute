# Alignment Begins with Human Purpose

**Alignment Begins with Human Purpose** — the founding claim about machine alignment: no artificial intelligence can be aligned to human values, because humanity has not yet selected them. The plurality of human values is not noise to be averaged but the discovery process itself.

The standard framing of alignment — make artificial systems reliably pursue human values — hides this prior problem. Cultures exist in polarity so that different moral organisations can be tested against lived consequence, the work of [[Distributed Moral Computation|distributed moral computation]]. To align a powerful machine to one frozen average of that contest may halt the very process by which the values worth aligning to are discovered.

Nor is there a neutral starting point. Training data already carries a morality: it compresses which voices were recorded, which institutions held publishing power and which harms were normalised. Post-training adjustment adds the preferences of the model's owner, evaluators, law and market. Turning "morality dials" does not create neutrality; it changes which constituency's dharma becomes authoritative. At civilisational scale the result is [[Value Lock-In|value lock-in]], since a trained model is a lookback governing a future.

The objective is therefore revised: "Do not align intelligence to one completed answer. Align it to a lawful process by which conscious plurality can preserve agency, compare consequence, repair harm, and continue moral creation." The process requires a shared dharmic floor — truthfulness, consent, exit, non-predation, ecological continuity, plurality of models, and the right of future beings to revise what they inherit — but no single ceiling ([[Dharma-Law]]).

Human alignment, in turn, begins with individuation: a person who has never separated chosen values from social programming cannot supply an authentic preference through a survey. [[The Anthropological Timeline of Human Ideas|The Anthropological Timeline]] supplies the civilisational memory, meditation supplies the inward distinction ([[Sovereign Attention|sovereign attention]]), and [[Voluntary Moral Communities|voluntary moral communities]] supply the lived tests; only together do they form a meaningful target for machine collaboration. Pluralistic alignment now exists as a research programme but begins from existing preferences; the stronger proposal here makes the preservation of moral evolution itself part of alignment.
