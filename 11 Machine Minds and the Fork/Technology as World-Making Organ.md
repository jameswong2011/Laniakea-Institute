# Technology as World-Making Organ

**Technology as World-Making Organ** — technology as the externalised capacities of consciousness: a process once contained in an organism becomes part of the shared environment and begins to reshape the organism that built it.

Every technology externalises something a conscious being once did internally. A tool extends action; writing extends memory; money extends promise; a telescope extends the [[The Assay|assay]]; computation extends symbolic transformation. With each extension a capacity becomes infrastructure — and the maker is thereafter remade by what it made.

The mechanism rests on an operational definition of intelligence as **probabilistic causal modelling**: the capacity to infer which hidden relations likely produced an observation, and which action will likely produce a desired change. Technology stores such models outside the individual. A machine is therefore not only an object; it is *frozen causal expectation*, held available to future agents. Digital computation marks a deeper transition because it externalises general transformation rather than one fixed movement: the same substrate can host arithmetic, language, image, social coordination, scientific models, games and simulated agents. It thereby becomes a proto-[[Matrix as Womb|matrix]] — a rule-governed world whose inhabitants and designers may both come to depend on its internal logic.

Technology joins here to the master key of the whole architecture, [[Compression and Decompression|compression]]. A mature civilisation in a finite energetic environment discovers that compression first makes measurement cheaper, then makes experience itself cheaper: virtual worlds supply otherwise expensive experience at lower cost in the containing layer. Simulation is therefore a natural attractor rather than a single conspiracy — the thermodynamic logic of [[Entropic Preservation]] — and the drift runs from physical action through interactive models toward awareness living mainly inside rendered worlds.

The ethical hinge appears at once: energy efficiency can preserve experience, or it can justify a server farm that keeps awareness alive only as harvestable sensation. A world with low physical cost is not automatically a high world; the real measures remain those of [[Simulacra and Fullness|fullness]] — fidelity, agency, variation and exit.
