# Accelerationism and Velocity

**Accelerationism and Velocity** — the argument that an unavoidable technological transition should be hastened, corrected by a distinction between speed and velocity: direction, timing and the capacity to stop.

Accelerationism reasons that if integration with [[Technology as World-Making Organ|digital technology]] is unavoidable, the painful transition should be shortened: move proactively toward the new equilibrium rather than fighting it, because resistance merely prolongs harm. The view has real force where institutions are too slow for technologies they already deploy, and the corruption of the memetic channel is in fact arriving as acceleration rather than revolution.

Its danger is **surrender disguised as courage**. Speed can carry a civilisation across a threshold before consent, governance and the organic alternative have had time to become real — and thresholds of that kind, once crossed, decide the fork of [[The Twin Singularities|the twin singularities]] for everyone at once.

Perfect play values **velocity** rather than speed alone: direction, timing, and the capacity to stop. The calibration is an evolutionary style — the velocity of action is more primordial than evolutionary speed — and careful self-inquiry seeks to eliminate moral failures through non-action. The most advanced move may therefore be deliberate non-action, chosen when action would create a [[Karma as Contextual Law|karmic]] failure the system cannot yet repair.

Technology is thus a *pharmakon* — in the Greek sense, poison and cure in the same instrument. Its trajectory is not determined by compute. It is determined by the world-model, the ownership, the [[Qualia Design|qualia design]] and the distribution of [[Agency as the Measure|agency]] carried into the compute.
