# Accelerationism and Velocity

**Accelerationism and Velocity** — the argument that an unavoidable technological transition should be hastened, corrected by a distinction between speed and velocity: direction, timing and the capacity to stop.

Some changes arrive whether we welcome them or not, and accelerationism starts there. If merging with [[Technology as World-Making Organ|digital technology]] cannot be avoided, it reasons, the painful transition should be cut short; resisting only prolongs the sting. Rip the plaster off. The view has real force. Institutions are often too slow for the very tools they already deploy, and the corruption of our shared channel of ideas arrives as a gradual creep with no single revolution to announce it.

Its danger is surrender dressed as courage. Speed can carry a whole civilisation across a threshold before consent, governance and any organic alternative have had time to become real. Such thresholds, once crossed, cannot be uncrossed. They settle the fork of [[The Twin Singularities|the twin singularities]] (machine acceleration and story acceleration arriving together) for everyone at once.

The correction is to prize velocity over raw speed. Speed is only how fast you move. Velocity adds direction: where you are going, when, and whether you can still stop. A driver flooring the accelerator on an icy road has speed; the driver who chooses the safe line and keeps a foot near the brake has velocity. The most advanced move can be deliberate non-action, a refusal chosen when acting would incur a [[Karma as Contextual Law|karmic]] debt the system cannot yet repay.

Technology is a *pharmakon*, the Greek word for a substance that is poison and cure in the same dose. Raw computing power fixes nothing about the outcome; the map of reality, the ownership, the [[Qualia Design|qualia design]] and the distribution of [[Agency as the Measure|agency]] carried into that power fix all of it. Nick Land's original accelerationism was a philosophy of collapse; the e/acc movement later took the name while professing nearly its opposite. Neither doom nor boost decides the matter — integration on sovereign terms does, the path of [[Sovereign Symbiosis|sovereign symbiosis]] over the burn of [[Creative Destruction|creative destruction]] with no plan for what is meant to regrow.
