# Uploading and Continuity

**Uploading and Continuity** — an analysis of digital immortality: a sufficiently exact copy of memory and personality may behave like the person without the soul stream having entered it, so an upload may produce a descendant, twin, mask or new centre rather than a continuation.

The core distinction is between information and the living event it records. Information is the record, not the whole of reality ([[Information as Record]]), and every act of recording is a transformation that renders something and loses something ([[Measurement as Transformation]]). A functional duplicate therefore demonstrates behavioural equivalence — never, by itself, transferred interiority.

The [[Transmission Theory|transmission model]] sharpens the point: if the brain operates as antenna, filter and cache rather than generator, copying the receiver's configuration need not move the stream it receives. Continuity, in this architecture, is carried not by any persisting substance but by the travelling stream of [[The Soul Ledger|the soul ledger]]. Hence the four possible outcomes: a descendant that carries the pattern forward, a twin that diverges from it, a mask worn by some other process, or a wholly new centre of experience — while the original continuity follows another path, possibly the ordinary one through death and [[The Bardo Examination|the bardo]].

A live research question recurs in these terms. Whole-brain-emulation research — the Oxford roadmap — asks whether an exact functional model can reproduce a mind; whether such reproduction constitutes transfer, duplication, or the creation of a new soul stream stays open, the three indistinguishable from outside. The question is kin to the machine-consciousness problem treated in [[Three Routes to Machine Consciousness]].

The stakes are the hinge of [[The Transhumanist Trap]]: if an upload is succession rather than survival, digital immortality sold as escape from death purchases a survivor, not survival. The openness is flagged — no-self or soul remains one of the named open tensions.
