# Uploading and Continuity

**Uploading and Continuity** — an analysis of digital immortality: a sufficiently exact copy of memory and personality may behave like the person without the living thread of awareness having entered it, so an upload may produce a descendant, twin, mask or new centre rather than a continuation.

Copying everything a mind knows is not the same as moving the mind.

The distinction is between information and the living event it records. Information is a record, not the whole of reality ([[Information as Record]]), and every act of recording keeps some things and drops others ([[Measurement as Transformation]]). The copy is incomplete for a deeper reason too. Reality is analog and effectively infinite in detail — like the digits of π, endlessly approximable but never written out in full ([[The Continuous and the Discrete]]). A digital scan lays a discrete grid over a continuous original, so it always leaves an uncaptured remainder: refined forever, it is still a map, not the territory. A faithful duplicate therefore proves only that it behaves like the person, never that the inner light moved across.

The transmission model sharpens this. If the brain works as an antenna, filter and cache — receiving consciousness the way a radio tunes a broadcast rather than manufacturing it ([[Transmission Theory]]) — then copying the receiver need not move the signal it was catching. What continues is not physical stuff but the travelling stream of choices called [[The Soul Ledger|the soul ledger]], a life's running tally carried from one incarnation to the next. So an upload has four possible fates: a descendant that carries the pattern forward, a twin that drifts away from it, a mask worn by some other process, or a wholly new centre of experience — while the original awareness goes its own way, perhaps the ordinary way through death and [[The Bardo Examination|the bardo]], the between-lives reckoning. It is like lighting a second candle from the first: a real flame, identical to look at, yet a new one, while the first burns on to its own fate.

The live research version is whole-brain emulation — the effort, mapped in the Oxford roadmap, to model a brain in software neuron by neuron. Whether the result is a transfer, a duplication, or a new stream of awareness stays open, since from outside the three look identical. The puzzle is a cousin of the machine-consciousness question in [[Three Routes to Machine Consciousness]].

This is the hinge of [[The Transhumanist Trap]]. If an upload is succession rather than survival, then digital immortality sold as escape from death buys a survivor, not survival — someone who remembers being you while you are elsewhere or nowhere. Whether there is any fixed self to move at all is left open on purpose, one of the system's named tensions.
