# Fractals as Compression Artefacts

**Fractals as Compression Artefacts** — the reading of natural self-similarity as the trace compression leaves on its output: a short recursive rule regenerating unbounded detail, repeated at every scale.

In algorithmic terms a pattern is compressible when a comparatively short instruction can regenerate a much longer expression. A fractal is the limiting case: a compact generative rule whose expression is inexhaustible. Wherever nature shows self-similarity across scales — coastlines, ferns, vasculature, neural arbours, the distribution of galaxies — this economy has left its signature. Self-similarity across scales is what maximal compression looks like from the inside.

The Hermetic axiom *as above, so below* is thereby re-read as an engineering statement rather than a mystical correspondence: a claim about scale-invariant generative architecture, the same short rules deployed at every level of a nested cosmos — cell, organism, [[The Universe-Cell|universe-cell]] — the structure of [[The Fractal Multiverse|the fractal multiverse]].

The concept closes a loop at the centre of the system. If creation is the [[Compression and Decompression|compression and decompression]] of an infinite informational fractal, then fractals in nature are artefacts in the engineer's sense — the trace the method leaves on what it renders. [[The Seed Phrase|The seed phrase]] is such a rule at the scale of a whole world; [[Gravity as Compression|gravity]] is the same economy in physics; [[Radiance|radiance]] is its social form, a centre transmitting pattern without controlling its unfolding.

Because the generative fractal is continuous, its expression can never be exhausted by any finite store — the boundary argued in [[The Continuous and the Discrete]] — which is why self-similarity is not decoration but the fingerprint of [[Sacred Infinity]] on every rendered layer.
