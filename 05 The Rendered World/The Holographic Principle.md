# The Holographic Principle

**The Holographic Principle** — the principle that the maximum information a region of space can hold scales with its boundary area, not its volume, so that the interior is a projection of data written on its surface.

The most information a region can contain is set by the size of its surface, not the size of its inside. That is the whole surprise. Start with *information* measured as entropy — the count of distinct states a system could occupy. Common sense says a big box holds more than a small one in proportion to its volume. The *Bekenstein bound* says otherwise: the ceiling on a region's information grows with its *boundary area*. Double the surface and you double the maximum contents; the interior volume is not what counts.

Gerard 't Hooft and Leonard Susskind lifted this from a fact about black holes into a general principle. The complete description of any volume of space is equivalent to a description living on its boundary, one dimension lower. The inside is a projection of data held on the surface that encloses it. The name is exact. A hologram is a flat film that stores a full three-dimensional scene; tilt it and the depth reappears. The solid depth of our world, the principle says, is a read-out of the same kind.

For a cosmology built on [[Compression and Decompression|compression]], this is decisive rather than curious. To store the contents of a volume on its lower-dimensional boundary is precisely a compression: the interior is the decompressed rendering of a smaller code. Physics has, in its own vocabulary, found the world to be an encoding — three-dimensional space as the unfolding of information held elsewhere and in less.

Juan Maldacena gave the idea its sharpest form. His AdS/CFT duality shows an exact equivalence between a world *with* gravity in a higher-dimensional interior and a theory *without* gravity on its lower-dimensional boundary. The two are the same physics written twice. If gravity and the depth of space can be captured completely by a boundary theory that contains neither, then substance and depth are derived, not fundamental. The durable gift of [[The String Theory Cautionary Tale|string theory]] is this hologram, not the string.

It feeds directly into [[Spacetime from Entanglement|spacetime woven from entanglement]] and into the world as [[It from Bit|answers drawn from an open substrate]]. Read rightly, holography is not proof that reality *is* information. It is the strongest sign that what we take for solid depth is the [[Information as Record|record]] of a lower-dimensional real.
