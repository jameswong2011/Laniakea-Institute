# The Lazy Render

**The Lazy Render** — the claim that the physical environment is computed only where and when observation forces the computation, so that entropy is spent only on what is actually witnessed.

The term is the engineer's lazy evaluation: the physical environment is not rendered until observed, and entropy is spent only where a conscious observer forces the computation. This aligns with the old doctrine of māyā — the world as projection — read not as a counsel of illusionism but as a description of rendering economics. Unwitnessed regions persist as compressed potential; observation decompresses them into fact.

Observation here is not a human gaze manufacturing particles by desire; it is the wider event in which a system interacts strongly enough to select and register a state. Attention is the consciousness-side of the selection, energetic exchange the physical side, and their exact union is held as an open problem. Light and attention occupy the boundary between potential and rendered fact — the threshold identified with Planck-scale dissipation rather than with a pixel grid ([[The Continuous and the Discrete]]).

Crucially, a lazily rendered world is not a fake one. To call reality a simulation need not mean that it is fake; it means the local world is rendered under rules from a level not wholly contained within the render. The laziness itself is motive evidence: worlds are built this way because entropic budgets are finite — the logic developed in [[Entropic Preservation]] — and it is why so few layers escape rendering altogether ([[Base Reality and Local Reality]]).

The device presupposes [[Consciousness as Primary|consciousness as primary]] — something must do the observing that forces the computation — and it feeds the moral question of what the render is for: [[Matrix as Womb|womb or prison]].
