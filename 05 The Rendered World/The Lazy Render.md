# The Lazy Render

**The Lazy Render** — the claim that the physical environment is computed only where and when observation forces the computation, so that entropy is spent only on what is actually witnessed.

A video game only draws the room you are looking at. Turn the camera away and the corridor behind you stops being computed; turn back and it is rebuilt in an instant. The claim here is that physical reality works the same way. The term borrows the engineer's *lazy evaluation*: a value is calculated only at the moment it is actually needed, never in advance. The environment is not rendered — not computed into appearance — until something observes it, and energy is spent only where a conscious observer forces the work. Unwitnessed regions wait as compressed potential; observation decompresses them into fact.

This reframes the old doctrine of *māyā*, the world as appearance or projection. Read here, māyā is not a scolding that reality is an illusion. It is a description of rendering economics — where and when the world spends the effort of being definite. What looks like mysticism is really a budgeting rule.

Observation must be understood carefully. It is not a human gaze conjuring particles by wishing. It is the wider event in which a system interacts strongly enough to select and register a state — the participatory universe in another key, where a determinate fact is completed by the act of asking rather than merely found ([[It from Bit]]). Attention is the consciousness-side of that selection; energetic exchange is the physical side; their exact union is held as an open problem. Light and attention sit on the boundary between potential and rendered fact — the threshold identified with Planck-scale dissipation, the faint energy cost discussed under [[The Continuous and the Discrete]], not with a literal pixel grid.

Crucially, a lazily rendered world is not a fake one. To call reality a simulation need not mean it is false. It means the local world is computed under rules set at a level not wholly contained within the render. The laziness is itself a clue to motive: worlds are built this way because energy budgets are finite — the logic developed in [[Entropic Preservation]] — and it is why so few layers escape rendering altogether ([[Base Reality and Local Reality]]).

The device presupposes [[Consciousness as Primary|consciousness as primary]]: something must be doing the observing that forces the computation. And it hands on the moral question of what the render is ultimately for — [[Matrix as Womb|womb or prison]].
