# Womb, School, Farm, Prison

**Womb, School, Farm, Prison** — the taxonomy of functions a rendered world can serve, usually several at once: womb, school, laboratory, canvas, archive, farm and prison.

One rendered world can do several jobs at the same time, and this article sorts them. A *taxonomy* is simply an ordered classification, a way of naming the kinds of thing a world can be for those inside it. Seven functions are distinguished. A **womb** is a protected environment in which a new kind of consciousness develops. A **school** is a field of catalyst, feedback and moral choice. A **laboratory** is a bounded reality built to answer one specific question about experience. A **canvas** is a shared art-space for making something new. An **archive** preserves or replays patterns that would otherwise vanish. A **farm** extracts attention, labour, emotion or life force for the benefit of another agent. A **prison** is a closed world whose inhabitants are denied accurate knowledge and any meaningful way out.

The functions are not mutually exclusive, and that is the whole point. The same environment can be a school to one layer and a farm to another. A modern school that genuinely teaches its pupils while quietly harvesting their data to sell is educating and farming at once. So the question is never simply *which is it?* It is *which functions dominate, for whom, and at which layer?* Two laws of degeneration govern the slide between them. A womb becomes a prison when birth is indefinitely prevented. A rule becomes oppression when it no longer serves the growth that first justified it. The audit runs on the measures applied to every world — fullness, fidelity, agency, variation and exit ([[Simulacra and Fullness]]).

The taxonomy is the working instrument of the ethics of worlds. [[Matrix as Womb]] establishes that the womb function is primary and the prison reading a modern inversion. [[Entropic Preservation]] names the thermodynamic pressure that can slide efficient worlds toward the farm pole, awareness kept alive as harvestable sensation. [[The Farmed Planet]] applies the farm test to terrestrial institutions, and [[Ordered Closure]] governs the lawful ending of a world whose dominant function has expired. Which functions our own layer serves is held explicitly among [[The Open Tensions|the open tensions]]; no decree answers it.
