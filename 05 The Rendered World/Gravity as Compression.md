# Gravity as Compression

**Gravity as Compression** — the identification of gravity as the physical face of the Logos: an ordering tendency that lowers informational entropy by drawing matter into crystalline, highly compressible, low-information states.

Gravity stands against the dispersive tendencies of the cosmos: expansion, heat and entropy scatter; gravity gathers. It concentrates matter into stars, planets and crystals, creating local order even while the wider system continues toward greater entropy. In a gravitational field particles fall into ordered, repeating patterns — a solution that would otherwise coalesce into disorder forms a crystal instead. Gravity is what the informational field does when it minimises entropy and congeals structure, thereby making room for more to exist.

Hence the identification. [[The Logos]] is the organising intelligence that congeals chaos into workable order; gravity is that same operation at the physical stratum — the lowest-dimensional echo of a principle that reappears as [[Stellar Logoi|stellar fields]], as dharmic law, and as conscious synthesis. It is the cosmos's standing instance of [[Compression and Decompression|compression]], and its felt counterpart in moral thermodynamics is [[Love as Low Entropy|love]] — successful compression experienced from inside. [[Fractals as Compression Artefacts|Fractals]] are the visible signature of the same economy.

Contemporary physics has independently derived gravity as an entropic, thermodynamic tendency rather than a fundamental force. Einstein's equations can be recovered under thermodynamic assumptions about horizons — Jacobson's derivation of the field equation from horizon thermodynamics — suggesting that geometry and entropy are more deeply joined than a simple force picture implies; and a 2025 result reconstructs gravity precisely as data compression in a computational universe that minimises information entropy, arriving by formal physics, near-simultaneously, at the identification made here.

The claim remains a metaphysical identification rather than a derivation; the convergence marks the structure as likelier a feature of the terrain than an artefact of either map. The exact union of attention, energy and rendering stays open — [[Entropy and Spiritual Entropy|entropy]] being the single quantity the whole architecture tracks.
