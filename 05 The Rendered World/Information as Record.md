# Information as Record

**Information as Record** — the revision of informational ontology: information is not the substance of reality but the measurement of the substance, the record produced when a consciousness reads the real.

Information is the read-out, not the thing read. That is the single correction this article makes. The fashionable view — *informational ontology*, the claim that reality is at bottom made of information — is half right and half wrong. It is right that manifested things are known only through difference, relation, pattern and constraint, and that physics keeps tying information to heat, computation, black holes and spacetime. It is wrong to mistake the record of a living process for the process itself. A map is not the territory. Sheet music is not the performance. A photograph of a meal holds no taste.

*Information*, then, is what appears when one consciousness measures, reads or remembers a fractal reality. It is a relation among three things — the measured, the measurement and the reader — not a self-standing substance floating outside all consciousness. The inversion at its sharpest: **information is not the substance; it is the measurement of the substance.** Information arises when a secondary consciousness tries to read a primary one — an attempt to read the dream. The fractal is the dream. Information is the reading. And the reading always loses what the dreaming held, because every [[Measurement as Transformation|measurement transforms]], rendering something and silently discarding the rest.

This keeps Wheeler's participatory [[It from Bit|it from bit]] — the thesis that every physical *it* is drawn from a registered yes-or-no answer — but re-orders it. The bit is the record read from the real, not its ultimate substance. Landauer's principle belongs here too: erasing a bit of information releases a minimum quantity of heat, so information carries a genuine physical cost. The revised hierarchy runs: consciousness is the capacity; relation is the grammar; qualia are the actuality; information is the record; compression is the method of carrying selected structure across limits. Consciousness is not one more data type inside a universal database ([[Consciousness as Primary]]). [[Qualia|Qualia]] are the actuality the record is about. [[Compression and Decompression|Compression]] carries it across limits.

The growing convergence of physics on an informational picture — the [[The Holographic Principle|holographic principle]] above all — is the strongest evidence the wider metaphysics can claim. But it describes the record, not the ground.

The test cases are blunt. A map of pain is not pain. A perfect behavioural description contains no inward event unless some subject undergoes it. A genome records developmental constraints without being the organism. A language model compresses human expression without holding the continuous conditions that produced human meaning. The residual question — information-primary or information-as-measurement — stays on the register of [[The Open Tensions|open tensions]].
