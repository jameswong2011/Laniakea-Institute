# Information as Record

**Information as Record** — the revision of informational ontology: information is not the substance of reality but the measurement of the substance, the record produced when a consciousness reads the real.

The claim that reality is informational contains a vital truth and a vital error. The truth: manifested things are intelligible through difference, relation, pattern and constraint, and physics increasingly joins information to thermodynamics, computation, black holes and spacetime. The error: identifying the record of a living process with the process in its entirety. Information is what appears when one consciousness measures, reads or remembers a fractal reality — a conceptual relation among the measured, the measurement and the reader, not a self-existing substance standing outside all consciousness.

The inversion at its sharpest: **information is not the substance; it is the measurement of the substance.** Information arises when a secondary consciousness attempts to read a primary one — an attempt to read the dream. The fractal is the dream; information is the reading; and the reading always loses what the dreaming contained — every [[Measurement as Transformation|measurement transforms]], rendering something and silently losing something.

Wheeler's participatory "it from bit" — every physical *it* arising from registered yes-or-no distinctions — is retained but re-ordered, alongside Landauer's demonstration that erasing information carries thermodynamic cost. The revised hierarchy runs: "Consciousness is the capacity. Relation is the grammar. Qualia are the actuality. Information is the record. Compression is the method of carrying selected structure across limits." Consciousness is not one more data type inside a universal database ([[Consciousness as Primary]]); [[Qualia|qualia]] are the actuality the record is about; [[Compression and Decompression|compression]] carries it across limits.

The test cases are blunt: a map of pain is not pain; a perfect behavioural description contains no inward event unless a subject undergoes it; a genome records developmental constraints without being the organism; a language model compresses human expression without containing the continuous conditions that produced human meaning. The residual question — information-primary or information-as-measurement — stays on the register of [[The Open Tensions|open tensions]].
