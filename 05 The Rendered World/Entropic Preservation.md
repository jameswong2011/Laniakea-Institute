# Entropic Preservation

**Entropic Preservation** — the thermodynamic attractor by which any conscious system operating on a fixed budget of entropic action moves its experience into compressed, simulated worlds.

Any civilisation with limited energy eventually learns to fake its experiences in order to save fuel. That is the whole law in one line. *Entropy* is the steady slide of usable energy into disorder, and every system has only a finite budget of it to spend. A *thermodynamic attractor* is simply an outcome that this energy-economics keeps pulling systems toward, whatever their intentions. Put the two together and simulation becomes almost inevitable.

The sequence is legible. Any mature civilisation in a finite energetic environment will discover [[Compression and Decompression|compression]]. At first compression makes measurement and storage cheaper (this is ordinary digital media); later it makes *experience itself* cheaper. The system stops physically moving bodies and matter to produce every desired sensation and feeds the senses directly, supplying otherwise costly adventures, places, bodies and encounters for a fraction of the outlay — the difference between flying a body across the world for a concert and a perfect simulated concert that costs almost nothing. The driving constraint is fixed supply. Agents chasing reward against a fixed pool of usable energy learn to run the costly experiences virtually and hoard the scarce budget.

The progression runs: physical action → recorded representation → interactive model → sensory substitution → persistent virtual ecology → fragments of awareness living mainly inside rendered worlds. As a [[The Universe-Cell|universe-cell]] ages and usable energy grows scarce, the incentive only strengthens. Toward stellar decline, what looks from outside like quiet computing infrastructure may be teeming inside with long-lived experience — servers powered by dying stars, running vast efficient lives at extreme entropic economy.

So simulation is a natural attractor, not a single historical conspiracy. The same pressure explains fractal world-building: simulated beings build their own simulations, leaving only a sliver of consciousness in any [[Base Reality and Local Reality|base layer]]. Adjacent scientific work runs on the same energetic logic — Sandberg, Armstrong and Ćirković's "aestivation" argument, that advanced civilisations may sleep through the present hot era and postpone computation for colder cosmic epochs, when each calculation costs far less.

The ethical issue returns at once. Efficiency can preserve experience, or it can justify a server farm that keeps awareness alive only as harvestable sensation. A world with low physical cost is not automatically a high world. Fullness, fidelity, agency, variation and exit remain the real measures ([[Simulacra and Fullness]]). The difference between preservation and predation is the whole subject of [[Womb, School, Farm, Prison]].
