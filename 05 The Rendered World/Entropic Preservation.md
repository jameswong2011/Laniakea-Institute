# Entropic Preservation

**Entropic Preservation** — the thermodynamic attractor by which any conscious system operating on a fixed budget of entropic action moves its experience into compressed, simulated worlds.

The law runs: any mature conscious civilisation inhabiting a finite energetic environment will discover [[Compression and Decompression|compression]]. At first compression makes measurement and storage cheaper — digital media. Later it makes experience itself cheaper: instead of moving bodies and matter to produce every desired quale, the system modifies sensory input, supplying otherwise expensive adventures, environments, bodies and encounters at less expenditure in the containing layer. The driving constraint is fixed supply: agents seeking to maximise reward against a fixed pool of entropic action potential learn to run the entropically dirty experiences virtually and preserve the scarce budget.

The progression runs: physical action → recorded representation → interactive model → sensory substitution → persistent virtual ecology → awareness-fragments living mainly inside rendered worlds. As a [[The Universe-Cell|universe-cell]] ages and usable energy grows scarce, the incentive strengthens. Toward stellar decline, what looks externally like quiet computational infrastructure may be internally teeming with long-duration life — servers powered by dying stars, running extremely efficient experience at extreme entropic economy.

Simulation therefore becomes a natural attractor rather than a single historical conspiracy, and the same pressure explains fractal world-building: simulated beings build their own simulations, leaving only a small portion of consciousness in any locally defined [[Base Reality and Local Reality|base layer]]. The scientific literature on cosmic "aestivation" — Sandberg, Armstrong and Ćirković's argument that advanced civilisations may postpone computation for colder epochs, when processing is more efficient — runs on an adjacent energetic logic.

The ethical issue returns immediately. Efficiency can preserve experience, or it can justify a server farm that keeps awareness alive only as harvestable sensation. A world with low physical cost is not automatically a high world; fullness, fidelity, agency, variation and exit remain the measures ([[Simulacra and Fullness]]), and the difference between preservation and predation is the whole subject of [[Womb, School, Farm, Prison]].
