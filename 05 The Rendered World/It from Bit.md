# It from Bit

**It from Bit** — John Archibald Wheeler's thesis that every physical thing derives its existence from yes-or-no answers drawn out by observation, retained here but re-ordered: the bit is the record read from the real, not the real itself.

Every solid *thing*, Wheeler proposed, is built from answers to yes-or-no questions. A *bit* is the smallest unit of information: a single yes or no, one or zero. His slogan "it from bit" says that each physical *it* — a particle, a field, a point of space — is not a fact sitting in place waiting to be found. It is drawn into definiteness by the act of asking. This is what he called a *participatory universe*: to measure is not to read off a value already there, but to complete a fact that was, until asked, genuinely open.

A parlour game makes it concrete. In ordinary twenty questions the answer is fixed before you begin. In Wheeler's variant the players agree on no object in advance, and each answer need only stay consistent with all the answers before it. The "thing" you finally name was never sitting there — it crystallised out of the questions. Reality, on this reading, firms up under interrogation rather than lying in wait.

Taken to its end, it from bit meets the doctrine of [[Śūnyatā (Emptiness)|emptiness]]: things have no independent existence, and the split between observer and observed is a convenience of the reading, not a seam in the world. It is one of the strongest bridges between physics and [[Dependent Origination|dependent origination]] — [[Ontic Structural Realism|relations, not things]], all the way down.

But the thesis must be re-ordered, or it collapses into the claim that reality simply *is* information. It is not. Information is the read-out — the [[Information as Record|record]] produced when a consciousness reads the real, not the substance beneath. The bit is the answer; what is questioned is a continuous, [[Consciousness as Primary|consciousness]]-bearing depth that measurement can only sample, and every [[Measurement as Transformation|measurement discards]] the remainder it cannot hold. So it from bit points past itself. Each determinate fact is bought with an act of attention and paid for in loss — the same economy by which the world is [[The Lazy Render|rendered only where it is observed]]. The wider convergence of physics on an informational picture, the [[The Holographic Principle|holographic principle]] above all, is the strongest sign yet that the world is read rather than built.
