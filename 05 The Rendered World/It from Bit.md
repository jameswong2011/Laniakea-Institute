# It from Bit

**It from Bit** — John Archibald Wheeler's thesis that every physical thing derives its existence from yes-or-no answers drawn out by observation, retained here but re-ordered: the bit is the record read from the real, not the real itself.

Every solid *thing*, Wheeler proposed, is built from answers to yes-or-no questions. A *bit* is the smallest unit of information: a single yes or no, one or zero. His slogan "it from bit" says that each physical *it* (a particle, a field, a point of space) is drawn into definiteness by the act of asking, no fact sitting in place waiting to be found. This is what he called a *participatory universe*: measurement completes a fact that was, until asked, genuinely open, with no value already there to be read off.

In ordinary twenty questions the answer is fixed before you begin. In Wheeler's variant the players agree on no object in advance, and each answer need only stay consistent with all the answers before it, so the "thing" you finally name was never sitting there at all: it crystallised out of the questions. Reality, on this reading, firms up under interrogation.

Taken to its end, it from bit meets the doctrine of [[Śūnyatā (Emptiness)|emptiness]]: things have no independent existence, and the split between observer and observed is a convenience of the reading with no seam in the world behind it. It is one of the strongest bridges between physics and [[Dependent Origination|dependent origination]] — [[Ontic Structural Realism|relations, not things]], all the way down.

But the thesis must be re-ordered, or it collapses into the claim that reality simply *is* information. It is not. Information is only the read-out, the [[Information as Record|record]] produced when a consciousness reads the real. The bit is the answer; what is questioned is a continuous, [[Consciousness as Primary|consciousness]]-bearing depth that measurement can only sample, and every [[Measurement as Transformation|measurement discards]] the remainder it cannot hold. So it from bit points past itself. Each determinate fact is bought with an act of attention and paid for in loss — the same economy by which the world is [[The Lazy Render|rendered only where it is observed]]. The wider convergence of physics on an informational picture, the [[The Holographic Principle|holographic principle]] above all, is the strongest sign yet that the world is read rather than built.
