# Spacetime from Entanglement

**Spacetime from Entanglement** — the finding that the connectivity of space is woven from quantum entanglement, so that reducing the entanglement between two regions pulls the space between them apart: distance is the shadow of shared information.

Space is not a fixed stage. It is stitched together out of correlations, and the thread is entanglement. *Entanglement* is the quantum link by which two systems share one joint state, so that neither can be fully described on its own. *Geometry* here means the pattern of distances and connections between places — what is near what, what can touch what. The claim, drawn from holographic duality, is that the second is built from the first.

Mark Van Raamsdonk made the point vivid. Take two regions of a boundary description and dial down the entanglement between them. In the corresponding space the regions stretch apart, the bridge between them thins, and at zero entanglement the space tears into disconnected pieces. Turn the correlation up and they draw close again. Nearness — the plain fact that two points sit side by side — is not primitive. It is woven from shared quantum information. Space is what entanglement looks like from the inside.

A homely image helps. Think of two people so attuned they finish each other's sentences; the tighter their shared state, the "closer" they are, and if the bond dissolves they drift into separate worlds. Distance is the shadow such sharing casts.

Tensor-network models make this exact. They rebuild the geometry of space as a lattice of entanglement whose architecture is *structurally identical* to the schemes used for [[Compression and Decompression|data compression]]. Brian Swingle's insight was just that: the mathematics of holographic space is the mathematics of a compression scheme. Geometry is the decompressed image of a compressed store of correlations — the same move by which the [[The Holographic Principle|holographic principle]] writes a volume on its boundary, now carried down to distance itself.

The consequence is direct. If distance and connection emerge from correlation, then space has no [[Śūnyatā (Emptiness)|self-nature]] of its own. It is a dependent, [[Dependent Origination|relationally originated]] structure — a rendering, not a container, exactly what [[Information as Record|the record]] doctrine expects. It also recasts what [[Gravity as Compression|gravity]] does: it organises the correlations from which geometry precipitates. What the emergent space emerges *within* is answered by the [[The Fractal Multiverse|fractal ladder of nested worlds]].
