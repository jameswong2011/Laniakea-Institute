# Propagation and Expansion

**Propagation and Expansion** — the two fundamental rates a rendered cosmos runs on: the speed of light governing how fast change moves through local spacetime, and the exponential constant governing how fast the render itself unfolds.

A rendered world admits two kinds of change, and they are not the same thing. One is motion through the field: a photon crossing a room, a planet swinging round its star. The other is growth of the field itself: the stretching of space that pushes distant galaxies apart without anything moving through the space between them. The first is governed by *c*, the speed of light — three hundred million metres per second. The second, during the universe's opening moments, followed an exponential curve tracing *e*, the base of natural growth.

The distinction is easiest to see on an inflating balloon. Draw two dots on its surface. An ant crawling from one dot to the other has a top speed — that is *c*, motion through the surface. But the balloon is also stretching, and the stretching moves the dots apart without the ant crossing any surface at all. During inflation that stretching ran on an exponential curve — that is *e*, growth of the surface itself. Two kinds of speed, orthogonal to one another.

In a [[The Lazy Render|lazily rendered]] cosmos, *c* is the ceiling on how fast a change ripples through whatever portion of the field has been computed. *e* is the constant that shapes how the field itself expands — the unpacking of compressed potential into rendered fact. The same exponential shape recurs whenever the underlying reality branches or differentiates: the One becoming the Many, the multiverse fanning from each free choice ([[The Entropic Branching Engine|the entropic branching engine]]), memes spreading through a culture, a cell dividing in a dish. Growth that is not motion through space — branching, differentiating, decompressing — reliably traces an *e*-shaped curve.

Cosmic inflation makes the distinction concrete. In the universe's first fraction of a second, space stretched at an exponential rate — doubling, and doubling, and doubling — while nothing inside it moved faster than light. The stretching was shaped by *e*. The internal speed limit remained *c*. Mainstream cosmology treats this as a fact about initial conditions. The present reading treats it as the physical signature of ongoing [[Compression and Decompression|decompression]] — [[The Logos|the Logos]] unpacking [[The Seed Phrase|the seed phrase]] into a universe, and *e* as the mathematical watermark of that unfolding. [[The Continuous and the Discrete|The foundation is continuous]], so the expansion is not a tick or a frame. It is continuous decompression, a growth function whose shape is exponential.

Two constants. Two axes. *c* governs propagation within a layer. *e* governs growth of the layer itself — and, in the nested architecture, the branching of the fractal, the rate at which compressed possibility becomes rendered world.
