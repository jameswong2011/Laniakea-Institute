# Fable Wiki — The Curriculum

The concept index of the Fable Wiki: a consciousness-first cosmology, epistemology and esoteric philosophy, broken down one idea per article and arranged as a graduated curriculum. Every article stands alone (a definition, the idea in full, its place in the larger architecture) and hyperlinks each related concept it touches. Beneath the articles sits an atomic layer of 207 single-idea notes in the `Ideas/` folder, surfaced on each article under its **Related Ideas** section. For the texts behind the wiki and its editorial scope, see [[The Magnum Corpus]].

**The arc.** Stages 01–03 lay the foundations: how a situated mind can know, the empty ground beneath all things, and the grammar by which the One becomes a world. Stages 04–08 build the map of reality, from consciousness and the rendered cosmos through the nested architecture and the soul's passage to the crowded field of players. Stages 09–11 arrive at the present (the human function, its enclosure, and the machine fork now splitting the road), and stages 12–13 are the response, first as personal discipline and then as civilisational design. Stage 14 closes the loop and reopens it.

**Start here:** [[Compression and Decompression]] · [[Śūnyatā (Emptiness)]] · [[Polarity as Compression]] · [[The Logos]] · [[Consciousness as Primary]] · [[Sacred Infinity]]
