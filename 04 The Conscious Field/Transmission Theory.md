# Transmission Theory

**Transmission Theory** — the model of the brain–mind relation: the brain is an antenna, filter, cache and action-selector for a consciousness it receives, constrains and stores, but does not generate.

A radio catches a broadcast already in the air and narrows it to one station you can actually listen to; the music is made elsewhere. Transmission theory says the brain works the same way. The claim follows directly from [[Consciousness as Primary]]. If consciousness is the universal capacity, produced by no neuron, then the organism's job is to localise awareness — to tune the wider field down to one body, one history, one survivable stream of what matters. The body is that interface. The brain acts as antenna, filter, cache and action-selector, a local memory and edge-storage node. It generates nothing.

The narrowing is the point. A living animal cannot afford the whole broadcast; it needs only the trickle relevant to staying alive. This is a compression — vast possibility encoded down to a retrievable, low-entropy stream. And the aperture is adjustable: it can widen, most obviously in sleep, where the filter loosens and more of the signal comes through ([[Dreams as Creator-Space]]).

The lineage is real. William James developed the transmission model in *Human Immortality*: the brain limits and directs a wider consciousness it does not produce from nothing. Aldous Huxley named the filter's character with his phrase the reducing valve, the nervous system throttling a cosmic consciousness down to the survivable trickle a biological life requires.

The standard objection is neural injury. Damage the brain and experience changes or disappears, so surely the generator has broken. But a damaged receiver behaves the same way. Injury can weaken the received signal, corrupt the stored memory, or disable the capacity to express what is received, none of which proves the brain created the field. A cracked radio proves the set matters. It says nothing against the broadcast.

The model carries much of the surrounding architecture: [[The Pineal Nexus|the pineal nexus]] becomes intelligible as the receiver's coordinating hub; dreaming reads as what the antenna does when the filter relaxes; death prunes the local interface while a deeper record persists ([[The Soul Ledger]]); and [[Uploading and Continuity|uploading]] becomes the copying of a cache, with no stream transferred.
