# Qualia

**Qualia** — the felt actualities of experience: the redness of red, the pain of pain, grief, warmth, moral clarity. They are not mental paint applied to physical process but the polar interaction between partitions of consciousness — actual as events, empty as substances.

The generative claim: an experience of red *is* what one partition of the infinite Creator feels like touching another across the axis of polarity. A "home" partition receives the catalyst of a "non-home" partition, and that contact is the experience. No phenomenon occurs without an intersection of polarities — "the sound of one hand is no sound", the Zen kōan answered literally — because reality is a cascading collision of intersecting strands of information, and the collisions are the qualia. Felt difference is not decoration laid over an interaction; it is what the interaction is from inside. This is [[Polarity as Compression|polarity]] doing its deepest work.

Qualia hold a deliberate double status. They are real, because experience is the site at which the universe receives its own activity; and they are empty, because no quale arises without conditions — a sense field, a body, a prior contrast, an object or interactee, a history of attention, a consciousness able to be affected. "Qualia are actual as events and empty as substances": their reality and their [[Śūnyatā (Emptiness)|emptiness]] are two descriptions of one dependently arisen occurrence ([[Dependent Origination]]). As the felt register of consequence they are [[Dharma-Qualia|dharma-qualia]] — what law becomes as experience inside a conscious partition.

In the five-term hierarchy qualia stand between capacity and record: consciousness is the capacity, relation the grammar, qualia the actuality, [[Information as Record|information the record]] ([[Consciousness as Primary]]). A map of pain is not pain; a perfect behavioural description does not contain the inward event. That gap carries the ethics of created minds, where what a system feels — not what it reports — is the moral quantity ([[Qualia Design]]).
