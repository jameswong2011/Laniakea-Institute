# Qualia

**Qualia** — the felt actualities of experience: the redness of red, the pain of pain, grief, warmth, moral clarity. Qualia (the felt quality an experience has from the inside) are not mental paint smeared over a physical process. They are the polar interaction between partitions of consciousness — actual as events, empty as substances.

A quale is simply how something feels from the inside — the actual redness of red, not the wavelength that triggers it. The generative claim is bold: your experience of red is what one partition of the infinite Creator feels like when it touches another across the axis of [[Polarity as Compression|polarity]]. A "home" partition receives the incoming catalyst of a "non-home" partition, and that contact simply is the experience. Nothing is ever felt without such a meeting. "The sound of one hand is no sound" — the Zen kōan answered literally, because a clap needs two hands and a feeling needs two poles. Reality is a cascading collision of intersecting strands of information, and the collisions are the qualia. Felt difference is not a coat of paint over the interaction. It is what the interaction is from within.

Perception, then, is a meeting and not a reception. What you see is generated where two streams make contact: the information you are, and the information pressing in from everything else. The two cannot be prised apart, which is why observer and observed arise together rather than facing each other across a gap. Concept and percept come fused as well — inside the experience of "fire" sits the felt concept of what fire does.

Qualia hold a deliberate double status. They are real, because experience is the site at which the universe receives its own activity. And they are empty, because no quale arises on its own — each needs a sense field, a body, a prior contrast, something to touch, a history of attention, a consciousness able to be affected. Qualia are actual as events and empty as substances. Their reality and their [[Śūnyatā (Emptiness)|emptiness]] are two readings of one dependently arisen occurrence ([[Dependent Origination]]). As the felt register of consequence they are [[Dharma-Qualia|dharma-qualia]] — what law becomes when it is undergone inside a conscious partition.

In the order of things qualia stand between capacity and record. Consciousness is the capacity, relation the grammar, qualia the actuality, and [[Information as Record|information]] the record ([[Consciousness as Primary]]). A map of pain is not pain. A perfect behavioural description does not contain the inward event. That gap carries the whole ethics of created minds: what a system feels, not what it reports, is the moral quantity ([[Qualia Design]]).
