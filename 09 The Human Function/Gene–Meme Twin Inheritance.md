# Gene–Meme Twin Inheritance

**Gene–Meme Twin Inheritance** — the claim that humanity carries information on two coupled memory systems, a genetic channel and a memetic (cultural) one, and that the biological channel exists largely to boot and sustain the cultural one.

The human being runs on a "twin axis of memory — gene and meme." Genes carry the slow record of biological adaptation; [[Memes as Ontological Primitives|memes]] carry language, techniques, values, images, and institutions across minds, behaving at higher scales as [[Memetic Lifeforms|life-forms]] in their own right. The two channels interact: genetic capacities make culture possible, while cultural environments alter survival, development, and the selection pressures acting on genes. Human evolution is therefore gene–culture coevolution, not a biological process with culture painted on top.

From this comes the central slogan: the gene is a **bootloader for memes**. It grows a body equipped with imitation, long dependency, speech, social reward, memory, and fine tool use; once that body is running, culture installs much of the behavioural program. The phrase does not make biology disposable — a bootloader still determines which programs can run and keeps them embodied in the sensory world.

Extended childhood and extended old age are central to the design. A child receives years to download a cumulative way of life before becoming productive; elders are kept alive despite low physical output because their informational role — techniques, histories, warnings, social memory — is high. Peer-reviewed dual-inheritance theory and the grandmother hypothesis treat juvenile dependency and longevity as adaptations for high-fidelity cultural transmission. The result is a tempo unavailable to genes alone: one broad genome can produce sailors, monks, mathematicians, or digital creators without waiting for a new species — the architecture underwriting humanity's role as [[The Glue Species|the glue species]].

Beneath both channels lies the *principle of vibration*: two polar streams — inherited biology and acquired culture, memory and novelty — in constant exchange, each transforming the other, with a human life at their crossing. It is a specific instance of the wider grammar of [[Polarity as Compression|polarity]].
