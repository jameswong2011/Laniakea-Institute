# Memes as Ontological Primitives

**Memes as Ontological Primitives** — the promotion of the meme from a metaphor for cultural replication to a fundamental unit of reality, on the argument that if reality is pattern, the most complex patterns are the most real entities.

A meme is not just a picture with a caption. It is a unit of pattern that copies from mind to mind — a word, an idea, a style, a value. Here that unit is promoted from a handy metaphor to an ontological primitive: one of the basic building blocks of what exists. The claim is bold. Memes, not [[Gene–Meme Twin Inheritance|genes]], are the more fundamental unit of the universe. The reason is that at the highest tiers [[Memetic Lifeforms|memetic lifeforms]] operate with no physical body at all. And if reality is ultimately pattern, then the most complex patterns are the most real things. On this view the physical world is a shadow cast by the world of ideas — an emanation flowing downward, not a foundation lying beneath.

Picture creation as a network map. Along one axis sit nodes of human [[Consciousness as Primary|consciousness]]. Along a second, at right angles, sit nodes of Platonic ideas — ideal patterns such as circle, justice, or blue. The edge joining a mind to an idea is the physical rendering of that idea into something perceivable. So physical arrangements are not always prior realities that stories later copy. Many begin as Forms — ideal patterns moving between agents — and become material only once attention and labour hold them in place. This is the same causal power that [[Hyperstition|hyperstition]] shows at the scale of culture, and the reason the physical can be read as an [[Information as Record|informational record]] of a deeper process.

Support arrives from an unexpected quarter. A recent line of work in developmental biology describes physical bodies as pointers into a structured non-physical space of patterns, from which minds and forms ingress — that is, cross over into physical form. It reaches the same inversion with no metaphysical intent, and is noted here without endorsing any single study. The lineage behind the idea is old: Plato's Forms, and the shared outlook of Eastern and ancient Greco-Egyptian-Mesopotamian metaphysics, set against modern physicalist reductionism. It is a clear case of [[Metaphysics Precedes Physics|metaphysics preceding physics]], and the ground on which [[The Glue Species|the glue species]] does its work.

It closes on an image rather than a proof. A human being is artwork painted by higher lifeforms on a three-dimensional canvas, trying to make out the painter. The recursive loop between the higher concept and the lower plane is the very essence of creation. This is offered as an interpretation of what reality is, not as a demonstration.
