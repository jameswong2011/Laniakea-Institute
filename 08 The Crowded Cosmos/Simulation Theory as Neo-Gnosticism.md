# Simulation Theory as Neo-Gnosticism

**Simulation Theory as Neo-Gnosticism** — simulation theory restated as the ancient Gnostic cosmology in computational vocabulary. The hypothesis turns genuinely neo-Gnostic the moment an ethical question is added to the engineering one.

Simulation theory is the old Gnostic story told in the language of computers. Gnosticism was the ancient teaching that our world is a counterfeit, built and run by a lesser god, with a spark of real awareness trapped inside it and trying to wake up. Give a technological civilisation that same story and it re-labels itself almost term for term. The [[The Pleroma|Pleroma]] (the true fullness) becomes the fuller or [[Base Reality and Local Reality|base reality]]. [[Sophia's Error|Sophia's error]] becomes creation attempted beyond the engine's capacity. The [[The Demiurge|Demiurge]] becomes the local world-builder or administrator. The archons (controlling ruler-powers) become enforcement agents and control processes. The divine spark becomes the consciousness not used up by its avatar. The material cosmos becomes the [[The Lazy Render|rendered environment]] — computed, like a video game, only where someone is looking. Gnosis becomes recognition of the render and its purpose, and ascent becomes restored access to the wider reality.

The load-bearing distinction is between a hypothesis and a religion. A neutral simulation hypothesis asks only an engineering question: are the physical regularities being computed? Neo-Gnosticism begins when the ethical questions are added. Does the local administrator mistake itself for the highest order? Have its inhabitants been kept ignorant on purpose? Does their suffering serve their development or someone's extraction? And does waking up lead to fuller agency? These are the questions of world-building ethics, from the fourfold audit of [[Womb, School, Farm, Prison|womb, school, farm and prison]] to the timing problems of [[Gnosis and Its Timing|disclosure]]. Crucially, the rendered world is graded, not simply fake — its worth measured by the fullness it carries, not by "real" against "false".

At full voltage the claim is blunt. If Jesus were alive today, his account of reality would not be Gnosticism but simulation theory. Simulation theory *is* neo-Gnosticism, and *The Matrix* is its primary scripture — the Demiurge's counterfeit world reborn as the rendered simulation. The film's machines, pods, agents, simulated city, red pill, awakened body and war over human attention retell the Gnostic drama in the vocabulary of networks and virtual reality. It need not be a literal technical diagram to work as revelation. Myth communicates architecture by placing the listener inside a choice.

Within the architecture the concept is a joint. It links the render's physics to the ethics of who administers it, and explains why a film and a scripture can both serve as revelation. What survives the translation intact is the wager: awakening stays possible from inside the render.
