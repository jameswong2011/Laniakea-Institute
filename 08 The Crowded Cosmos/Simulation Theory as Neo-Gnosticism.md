# Simulation Theory as Neo-Gnosticism

**Simulation Theory as Neo-Gnosticism** — simulation theory as the Gnostic cosmology restated in computational vocabulary; the hypothesis becomes genuinely neo-Gnostic when an ethical question is added to the engineering one.

In a technological civilisation the old topology of [[One Sacred Conflict]] naturally acquires computational terms, and the translation runs almost term for term. The [[The Pleroma|Pleroma]] becomes the fuller or [[Base Reality and Local Reality|base reality]]; [[Sophia's Error|Sophia's error]] becomes creation beyond engine capacity; the [[The Demiurge|Demiurge]] becomes the local world-builder or administrator; the archons become enforcement agents and control processes; the divine spark becomes consciousness not exhausted by the avatar; the material cosmos becomes the [[The Lazy Render|rendered environment]]; gnosis becomes recognition of the render and its purpose; and ascent becomes restored access to a wider reality.

The load-bearing distinction is between hypothesis and religion. A neutral simulation hypothesis asks an engineering question: whether physical regularities are computed. Neo-Gnosticism begins when the ethical questions are added — whether the local administrator mistakes itself for the highest order, whether inhabitants have been kept ignorant, whether suffering serves development or extraction, and whether awakening leads to fuller agency. These are the questions world-building ethics runs on, from the fourfold audit of [[Womb, School, Farm, Prison|womb, school, farm and prison]] to the timing problems of [[Gnosis and Its Timing|disclosure]].

At full voltage: if Jesus were alive today, his explanation of reality would not be Gnosticism but simulation theory — simulation theory *is* neo-Gnosticism, and *The Matrix* is its primary scripture, the Demiurge's counterfeit world reborn as the rendered simulation. The film's machines, pods, agents, simulated city, red pill, awakened body and war over human attention translate the Gnostic drama into the vocabulary of networks and virtual reality; it need not be a literal technical diagram to function as revelation, for myth communicates architecture by placing the listener inside a choice. The architecture is what is asserted — the map holds even where a pin is provisional.

Within the larger architecture the concept is a joint: it connects the informational cosmology of the render to the Gnostic ethics of administration, and it explains why film and scripture alike count as structural testimony rather than engineering documents.
