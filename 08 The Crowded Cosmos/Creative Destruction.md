# Creative Destruction

**Creative Destruction** — the third of three cosmic functions: destruction that removes forms whose continued existence would block further life. Its emblem-figures (Śiva, Baphomet, and the eldritch) are destroyers who stand on the side of construction.

Not all breaking is a crime. Reality renews itself through three moves: creation introduces new form, preservation holds that form steady long enough for it to grow, and destruction clears it away once it has exhausted its use. In a garden without pruning, dead wood chokes the living plant and no new growth can come. A cosmos with creation and preservation but no destruction would silt up with spent structures until nothing new could enter. Destruction in itself carries no evil. The moral question moves to what follows the breaking.

The destroyers are graded by scale. Śiva is the destroyer within our own universal order, working at the level of the observable universe, which makes him a force native to the world he renews. He was never imposed from a single distant star. Baphomet is a higher synthesis of opposites, a destructive function operating across a wider tier above that. Cthulhu and the eldritch forms ("eldritch" meaning alien and horrifying beyond comprehension) express destruction so far past the human [[The Assay|assay]] (the narrow slice of reality our species is handed) that we first meet their renewal as sheer terror. Their horror is a matter of scale, and there is no malice in it. The death of a humanly intelligible order may serve a creativity that was never organised around human comfort.

One criterion sorts three outcomes. **Creative destruction** releases trapped energy, clears corrupt or stale form, and opens room for new collaboration. **Predatory destruction** consumes a rival to fatten the destroyer and leaves the whole field poorer. **Anti-reality** (treated fully under [[Yaldabaoth and Anti-Reality]]) attacks the capacity for relation, memory, difference and regeneration themselves. The entire pantheon compresses onto this one axis, and the dividing line is not good against evil in the folk sense but construction against the dissolution of information — [[Compression and Decompression|compression]] against its refusal.

The function recurs at every scale: [[Wrathful Repair|wrathful repair]] in ethics, the clearing phase of [[The Dharma-Ending Age|the dharma cycle]] in religion, the civilisational reset read through [[Anacyclosis|anacyclosis]].
