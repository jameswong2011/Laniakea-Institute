# Gods as Masks

**Gods as Masks** — the interface model of divinity. A god-form is a mask through which at least four layers can act at once: an archetypal function, a memetic body, a collective reservoir and a higher intelligence. Names are addresses, not guarantees.

A god's name works less like a person and more like a phone number. Dial it and you reach a channel — but who picks up can vary, and what answers is rarely one simple thing. Four layers speak through a single divine mask. First, an **archetypal function**: a recurring pattern such as wisdom, preservation, destruction, fertility, sovereignty or mercy (an archetype is a basic shape that keeps recurring across cultures). Second, a **memetic body**: the stories, images, rituals and expectations that have piled up around the name over centuries — "meme" here meaning an idea-pattern that spreads and persists. Third, a **collective reservoir**: the attention, emotion, vows and psychic energy poured in by worshippers, pooled and stored. Fourth, a **higher intelligence** — stellar, planetary, transpersonal or conceptual — able to reach through the established interface and act.

These layers coexist; they do not compete. An Egyptian deity may be, all at once, a symbol, an [[Egregores|egregoric reservoir]] (a thoughtform fed by collective attention), a moral template, and a translation layer through which a [[Stellar Logoi|stellar intelligence]] enters human culture. Worship does not merely describe the god. It strengthens and tunes the channel, and in return the god organises the worshippers' imagination and action. Human and god-form shape one another across scales.

Two consequences follow. One intelligence may wear several cultural masks, and one mask may host several intelligences at different times. So the question "is the god real?" gives way to a better one: "which layers are active in this particular contact?" Discernment reads the relational pattern, not the label. What qualities grow around the contact? What obligations does it request? Where does the energy flow? Does [[Agency as the Measure|agency]] expand or shrink? And can you still withdraw honestly? That last test sorts every mask by the one axis beneath the pantheon — [[One Sacred Conflict|whether a force builds you up or dissolves you]].

Above the general model sit particular cases. Stranger intelligences often wear a familiar deity's face precisely to reduce fear, taking a humanoid appearance built for contact without terror. [[Trip Reports and Revelation|Channelled testimony]] preserves exactly this shape: beings who report donning a known god's mask so as to seem less alien.
