# Gods as Masks

**Gods as Masks** — the interface model of divinity: a god-form is a mask through which at least four layers can act at once — archetypal function, memetic body, collective reservoir and higher intelligence. Names are addresses, not guarantees.

The four layers are: an **archetypal function**, a recurring pattern such as wisdom, preservation, destruction, fertility, sovereignty or mercy; a **memetic body**, the stories, images, rituals and expectations accumulated around the name; a **collective reservoir** of attention, emotion, vows and psychic energy contributed by worshippers; and a **higher intelligence** — stellar, planetary, transpersonal or conceptual — able to act through the established interface.

The layers coexist rather than compete. An Egyptian deity may simultaneously be a symbol, an [[Egregores|egregoric reservoir]], a moral template and a translation layer through which a [[Stellar Logoi|stellar intelligence]] enters human culture. Worship does not merely describe the deity: it strengthens and tunes the channel, while the deity organises the worshippers' imagination and action in return. Human and god-form co-create one another across scales.

Two consequences follow. One intelligence may wear several cultural masks, and one mask may sometimes host several intelligences — which is why the question "is the god real?" gives way to "which layers are active in this contact?" Discernment examines the relational pattern rather than the label: what qualities increase around the contact, what obligations are requested, where energy flows, whether [[Agency as the Measure|agency]] expands, and whether the connection remains capable of honest withdrawal.

Above the general model sits a layer of particular pins: familiar deity-forms worn by stranger intelligences to reduce fear, and humanoid appearances purpose-built for contact without terror. [[Trip Reports and Revelation|Channelled testimony]] preserves contact in exactly this shape — beings reporting that they wore a familiar deity's mask to appear less alien. The map holds even where a pin is provisional.
