# One Sacred Conflict

**One Sacred Conflict** — the great religious cosmologies as cultural refractions of a single shared topology: one drama of enclosure and return, told in many symbolic languages.

A six-step structure recurs beneath the differences of doctrine: an unbounded or luminous reality exceeds the ordinary world; differentiation permits manifestation; a mixture, fall, ignorance or capture obscures the greater order; embodied beings carry a capacity not exhausted by their enclosure; knowledge, purification, compassion or right action reopens the path; and liberation returns the separated current to a wider collaboration without erasing all meaningful difference.

Each tradition supplies a local symbolic codec for this movement. Buddhism renders it as [[Dependent Origination|dependent origination]], ignorance, suffering, no-self, awakening and release. The Gnostic systems render it as the [[The Pleroma|Pleroma]], [[Sophia's Error|Sophia's generation]], the [[The Demiurge|Demiurge]] and his archons, the divine spark, gnosis and return. Vedic and tantric traditions speak of Brahman, cyclic manifestation, māyā, the triple function of creation, preservation and destruction, and paths of reunion. Zoroastrianism and Manichaeism make the struggle between living light and imprisoning darkness fully explicit.

The claim is bounded. The systems are not historically or doctrinally identical; their shared form is a recurring response to the same kind of world. Consciousness discovers itself inside a limited [[The Assay|assay]], senses that the limitation is not total, encounters both helping and capturing intelligences, and seeks a practice that restores freedom.

The topology is load-bearing for the whole crowded cosmos: it grounds [[Radical Syncretism|radical syncretism]], supplies the grammar translated by [[Simulation Theory as Neo-Gnosticism|simulation theory]], and positions [[Yeshua as Bridge|Yeshua]] at the hinge between its dharmic and Abrahamic tellings.
