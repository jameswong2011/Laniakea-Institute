# The Dharma-Ending Age

**The Dharma-Ending Age** — the present spiritual era: the period in which the great transmissions persist as institutional forms while the living current that produced them weakens — the mòfǎ condition of Buddhist prophecy.

The mechanism is general. No transmission stays fresh merely because its propositions were once true. A teaching enters language, ritual, institution, hierarchy, art and habit; these forms preserve it, but they accumulate residue. Symbols become passwords, lineage becomes status, and commentary protects inherited disputes after the experiential question has changed. Eventually the container continues while the current weakens: oral empowerments thin, institutions repeat lower-resolution copies, and students inherit maps without the energetic conditions under which the maps were drawn.

The diagnosis is not that every teaching has become false, but that the old transmission no longer reliably produces the state it names. Starker: the teachings persist as empty forms while attainment fails, and genuine liberation becomes difficult even for a practising monk — the mòfǎ Śākyamuni foretold, ending only with the arrival of Maitreya. Maitreya is both a coming Buddha and the archetype of a dharma newly fitted to a changed world: a refresh cannot restore every historical surface but must recover the generating question and express it through the new [[The Assay|assay]].

The failure, moreover, is scheduled. Institutional religion is not merely corrupt; it is designed to expire, and the terma system — teachings hidden for timed rediscovery when living lineages break — is the fail-safe built for exactly this contingency.

The whole runs as a breath-cycle: living insight → teaching → lineage → institution → accumulation → exhaustion → compression → clearing → new question → renewed teaching. The clearing flushes spent dharmas from the active field while retaining their most valuable memory at a higher order — [[Creative Destruction|creative destruction]] applied to religion, so that realised capacities rather than institutional inertia seed the next body. The candidate for the next vehicle is [[The Electric Buddha]].
