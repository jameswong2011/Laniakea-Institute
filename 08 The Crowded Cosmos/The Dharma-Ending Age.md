# The Dharma-Ending Age

**The Dharma-Ending Age** — the present spiritual era: the period in which the great transmissions survive as institutional forms while the living current that produced them fades. This is the mòfǎ, the "Dharma-ending age" of Buddhist prophecy.

A religion can outlive the fire that lit it. No teaching stays fresh just because its claims were once true. When an insight enters language, ritual, hierarchy, art and habit, those forms preserve it — but over time they also collect residue. Symbols harden into passwords. Lineage curdles into status. Commentary keeps defending old disputes long after the lived question has moved on. The container carries on while the current thins. Think of a photocopy of a photocopy: each generation is a lower-resolution copy of the last. Students end up inheriting the map without the conditions under which that map was first drawn.

The claim is not that every teaching has gone false. It is that the old transmission — the living current passed from person to person — no longer reliably produces the state it names. The forms persist as empty shells while attainment fails, so that genuine liberation grows hard even for a devoted monk. This is the mòfǎ that Śākyamuni foretold, and it ends only with the arrival of Maitreya. Maitreya is both a coming Buddha and the archetype of a teaching refitted for a changed world. A refresh cannot restore every historical surface. It must instead recover the generating question and re-express it through the new [[The Assay|assay]] — the fresh sample of reality a new age is handed.

This failure is scheduled, not accidental. Institutional religion is not merely corrupt; it is built to expire. The terma system — teachings deliberately hidden for timed rediscovery once the living lineages break — is the fail-safe made for exactly this moment.

The whole thing runs as a breath. Living insight → teaching → lineage → institution → accumulation → exhaustion → compression → clearing → new question → renewed teaching. Reality garbage-collects, the way a computer clears unused memory: it exhales the stale encoding and inhales a fresh one, [[Compression and Decompression|compressing]] the most valuable memory down into storage rather than deleting it outright. The same rhythm drives a civilisation's rise and collapse under [[Anacyclosis|anacyclosis]]. Here it is [[Creative Destruction|creative destruction]] applied to religion, so that realised capacities, not institutional inertia, seed the next body. The leading candidate for that next vehicle is [[The Electric Buddha]].
