# The Magnum Corpus

**The Magnum Corpus** — the pair of documents in which the system is set down in full: **Magnum Codex Primaris** and **Magnum Fable Primaris**, two projections of a single synthesis — one architecture rendered at two voltages.

The **Codex** is the systematic treatise: eight parts and forty chapters, running from the conditions of knowing to the transhumanist fork, closed by a Coda and five appendices — a compact architecture and glossary (Appendix A, home of [[The Twelve Axioms|the twelve axioms]]), a register of distinctive propositions (B), the catalogue of [[The Open Tensions|open tensions]] (C), research notes on the real thinkers and findings engaged (D), and an editorial charter of fragments not integrated (E).

The **Fable** is the single-thesis essay: thirteen parts organised by the one act of [[Compression and Decompression|compression]], written in a sharper, more committed register. It names its speculative claims as provisional pins — "the map holds even where a pin is provisional" — and closes with an audit of its novel departures (Appendix A) and its own charter of threads set aside (B).

Both were generated from one source corpus: the @MAW Archive (449 archived posts and threads, sorted into thematic raw files) and the @Project research notes. A version-1.0 predecessor exists as *The Compression of Sacred Infinity*, whose lineage title the Codex still carries as its version 2.0. They are best read not as rival theories but as two decompressions of one seed.

Their editorial charters govern this wiki. Codex Appendix E ("Fragments Not Integrated") and Fable Appendix B ("Threads Set Aside") name the material each document declined to build on — retaining structural theses while omitting specifics, as "a statement about scope… not a verdict on the excluded material". The wiki honours both charters by exclusion: what the documents set aside is absent here as well.

Read together, the Codex supplies architecture and the Fable voltage: the treatise shows how the load-bearing parts hold, while the essay runs one current through all of them at speed. They converge on the same closing figure and the same refusal — [[The Lemniscate and the Helix|the lemniscate corrected into a helix]], and [[Sacred Infinity]] as the reality neither document will close.
