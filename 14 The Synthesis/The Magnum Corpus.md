# The Magnum Corpus

**The Magnum Corpus** — the pair of documents in which the whole system is written down in full: the [[Magnum Codex Primaris|Codex]] and the [[Magnum Fable Primaris|Fable]], two versions of a single synthesis. One architecture, rendered at two voltages.

Think of a cathedral described twice. The first description is an engineer's blueprint. It labels every load-bearing beam and shows exactly how the structure stands. The second is a guided tour that makes you feel the height and the light. The Codex is the blueprint; the Fable is the tour. Neither is truer than the other. They are the same building, drawn for different purposes.

The **Codex** is the systematic treatise — a reference manual for the cosmology. It runs across eight parts and forty chapters, from the conditions of knowing to the transhumanist fork, and closes with a Coda and five appendices. Those appendices hold the working parts. Appendix A is a compact architecture and glossary, and the home of [[The Twelve Axioms|the twelve axioms]]. Then follow a register of distinctive claims (B), the catalogue of [[The Open Tensions|open tensions]] (C), research notes on the real thinkers engaged (D), and a charter of fragments left unintegrated (E).

The **Fable** is the single-thesis essay, written in a sharper and more committed register. It runs one idea — the single act of [[Compression and Decompression|compression]] — through thirteen parts. Compression here means folding a vast reality into a small seed that can later unfold, and the essay treats every part of the cosmos as one such unfolding. It marks its boldest claims honestly as provisional pins, holding that "the map holds even where a pin is provisional". It ends with an audit of its own novel departures.

Both grew from one root. The source was an archive of 449 posts and threads, sorted into thematic raw files, together with a body of research notes. An earlier version-1.0 draft exists under the title *The Compression of Sacred Infinity*, and the Codex still carries that lineage as its version 2.0. The two documents are best read not as rival theories but as two unfoldings of a single seed.

Their editorial charters govern this wiki. Each document names the material it declined to build on. Each keeps the load-bearing theses while setting aside specifics — "a statement about scope… not a verdict on the excluded material". The wiki honours both charters by the same exclusion. What the documents set aside is absent here as well.

Read together, the Codex supplies the architecture and the Fable the voltage. The treatise shows how the load-bearing parts hold. The essay runs one current through all of them at speed. They arrive at the same closing image and the same refusal — [[The Lemniscate and the Helix|the lemniscate corrected into a helix]], and [[Sacred Infinity]] as the reality neither document will ever close.
