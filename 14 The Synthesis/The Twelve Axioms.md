# The Twelve Axioms

**The Twelve Axioms** — the twelve working rules from which the whole system can be regrown: its founding constitution in a dozen lines.

An *axiom* is a starting assumption — a rule you agree to build on, from which everything else follows. Geometry grows from a handful of them; so does this cosmology. Think of the twelve as a founding constitution. A constitution is short, yet a whole body of law unfolds from it, and it can be amended when it fails. These axioms work the same way. They are *working* axioms, not claimed self-evident truths: commitments to build with, the smallest seed that still regrows the living whole. Here the system applies its own doctrine to itself.

1. **Situated knowledge** — every knower receives reality through a biological, cultural, linguistic, dimensional and historical filter, the [[The Assay|assay]]; never the whole, only a sample.
2. **Transformative measurement** — every record captures one aspect and loses or alters another, so measuring changes the thing measured, A → Aa + Ab ([[Measurement as Transformation]]).
3. **Dependent being** — nothing that appears has isolated, permanent existence of its own; it is relation all the way down ([[Dependent Origination]]).
4. **Process priority** — action and relation come first, and the stable "objects" are abstractions drawn out of them ([[Process Ontology]]).
5. **Consciousness priority** — consciousness is the capacity in which any world or record can become present at all ([[Consciousness as Primary]]).
6. **Qualitative actuality** — [[Qualia|qualia]], the felt inside of experience, are the actuality of relation, and the basis on which consequence carries value.
7. **Polar generation** — meaningful difference makes the gradient that choice, experience and compact storage all require ([[Polarity as Compression]]).
8. **Logos and Dharma** — the [[The Logos|Logos]] organises the space of the possible; [[Dharma-Law]] governs how its parts collaborate; [[Dharma-Qualia]] is what that collaboration feels like.
9. **Fractal emergence** — agents combine into greater agents and worlds into greater bodies, with new properties at each level, as cells make a body ([[The Fractal Multiverse]]).
10. **Compressed continuity** — genes, memes, souls, myths and models carry selected patterns across boundaries without saving the whole event ([[Compression and Decompression]]).
11. **Agency as ethical measure** — a fertile order enlarges its members' capacity to understand, choose, repair and create again ([[Agency as the Measure]]).
12. **Perpetual opening** — no locally complete model exhausts [[Sacred Infinity]]; every answer can become the seed of another question.

Read in order, they trace the whole arc. Axioms 1–2 fix the knower's situation. Axioms 3–4 give the empty, process ground. Axioms 5–6 make the consciousness-first turn. Axioms 7–9 are the generative machinery. Axioms 10–11 carry continuity and ethics. Axiom 12 returns to openness. Three words hold their spine: consciousness is the substance, information the medium, compression the verb. And that final axiom is load-bearing: it licenses [[The Open Tensions|the open tensions]] and forbids the system to mistake itself for a finished object.
