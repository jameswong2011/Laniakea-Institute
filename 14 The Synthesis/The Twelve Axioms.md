# The Twelve Axioms

**The Twelve Axioms** — the twelve working axioms of the system: numbered commitments from which the whole architecture can be regrown.

They are *working* axioms: commitments to build with, not self-evident truths — the system applying its own doctrine to itself, the smallest seed that still regrows the essential living difference.

1. **Situated knowledge** — every knower receives reality through a biological, cultural, linguistic, dimensional and historical [[The Assay|assay]].
2. **Transformative measurement** — every record renders some aspect and omits or alters another, A → Aa + Ab ([[Measurement as Transformation]]).
3. **Dependent being** — no manifested entity possesses isolated, permanent own-being ([[Dependent Origination]]).
4. **Process priority** — action and relation are ontologically prior to the stable objects abstracted from them ([[Process Ontology]]).
5. **Consciousness priority** — consciousness is the capacity in which any world or record becomes present ([[Consciousness as Primary]]).
6. **Qualitative actuality** — [[Qualia|qualia]] are the inward actuality of relation, the basis on which consequence has value.
7. **Polar generation** — meaningful difference creates the gradient required for choice, experience and compact representation ([[Polarity as Compression]]).
8. **Logos and Dharma** — the [[The Logos|Logos]] organises a possibility-space; [[Dharma-Law]] regulates its collaboration; [[Dharma-Qualia]] reveals what that collaboration feels like.
9. **Fractal emergence** — agents form greater agents and worlds form greater bodies, with new properties at each tier ([[The Fractal Multiverse]]).
10. **Compressed continuity** — genes, memes, souls, myths and models carry selected patterns across limits without preserving the whole event ([[Compression and Decompression]]).
11. **Agency as ethical measure** — a fertile order enlarges its participants' capacity to understand, choose, repair and create again ([[Agency as the Measure]]).
12. **Perpetual opening** — no locally complete model exhausts [[Sacred Infinity]]; every answer can become the seed of another question.

Read in order, they trace the arc of the system: epistemic situation (1–2), the empty processual ground (3–4), the consciousness-first inversion (5–6), the generative machinery (7–9), continuity and ethics (10–11), and the return to openness (12), which licenses [[The Open Tensions|the open tensions]] and forbids the system to mistake itself for a finished object.
