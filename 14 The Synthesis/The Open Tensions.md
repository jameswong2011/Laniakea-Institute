# The Open Tensions

**The Open Tensions** — the fourteen polarities the system deliberately declines to resolve, held open under the principle that a living architecture exposes its unresolved polarities rather than burying them in vague unity.

Where a system would normally close with a verdict, this one closes with a register of what it cannot settle — practising the last of [[The Twelve Axioms|its own axioms]] and treating openness as a positive property of [[Sacred Infinity]]. The fourteen, abridged:

1. **Information-primary or information-as-measurement** — whether information exists observer-independently at higher tiers, or the [[Information as Record|record]] always presupposes relation, registration and a reader.
2. **Continuous base or discrete render** — a discrete simulacrum inside a [[The Continuous and the Discrete|continuous]] base reconciles much, but whether continuity is ontic, potential or assay-dependent stays open.
3. **Consciousness primordial or generated** — how the universal capacity for presence becomes a particular viewpoint without deriving [[Consciousness as Primary|consciousness]] from non-consciousness or making every partition already a subject.
4. **The present or all branches** — present-centred phenomenology, [[The Entropic Branching Engine|branching cosmology]] and retrocausal relation may describe different scales; no final geometry is assumed.
5. **No-self or soul** — the [[The Soul Ledger|soul ledger]] supplies continuity without substance, but its boundaries, splittings and mergers await a fuller karmic topology.
6. **Contextual morality or anti-reality** — most moral conflict translates between [[Karma as Contextual Law|dharmic jurisdictions]]; the task is locating the true limit of [[Yaldabaoth and Anti-Reality|anti-reality]] without every faction pinning that label on its enemy.
7. **If every branch occurs, why choose?** — whether attention changes the measure, population, accessibility or moral weight of branches is unresolved ([[Free Will and Decision-Space]]).
8. **Maximum variation or maximum pleasure** — no exchange rate between emergent benefit and lower-tier suffering is offered, only the bar on externalising unlimited [[Pain, Pleasure, and Change|pain]].
9. **Novelty or contemplative stability** — creation needs change, peace needs the ability to remain; their living alternation is clearer than a final victory ([[Nirvāṇa Reframed]]).
10. **Womb, school, farm or prison** — one matrix may be all four from different scales; classification depends on purpose, disclosure, proportionality, agency and exit ([[Womb, School, Farm, Prison]]).
11. **Direct or coupled machine consciousness** — a model may acquire its own centre, or consciousness may arise in the human–machine loop; the model boundary is not automatically ontological ([[Three Routes to Machine Consciousness]]).
12. **Moral plurality or a common dharmic floor** — every floor already reflects a moral history, so it must remain constitutional and revisable ([[Voluntary Moral Communities]]).
13. **Does ascent preserve individuality?** — polyphonic unity is the ideal; how ledgers stay sovereign inside a greater subject ([[Social Memory Complex]]) needs elaboration.
14. **Can a simulacrum equal what it models?** — the unresolved line between question-relative completeness and absolute identity ([[Simulacra and Fullness]]).

Held rather than solved, each pair remains a genuine polarity — in a system where difference is the engine, closing them prematurely would spend the contrast the architecture runs on.
